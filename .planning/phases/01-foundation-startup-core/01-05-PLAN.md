1. SaaS: MRR, ARR, churn (logo + revenue), NRR, CAC, LTV, LTV/CAC, CAC payback, Rule of 40, NDR

3. E-commerce: Revenue, AOV, CAC, LTV, repeat purchase rate, gross margin, inventory turnover
4. Fintech: AUM/loan book, default rate, CAC, LTV, revenue per user, regulatory capital ratios
