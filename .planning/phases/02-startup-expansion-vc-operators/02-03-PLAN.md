SKILL.md body:

**Step 1: Input Gathering** — Ask ALL in ONE message:
1. Target stage: pre-seed / seed / Series A / Series B (determines threshold benchmarks)
2. Current metrics: MRR/ARR, growth rate MoM/YoY, churn, LTV/CAC if known
3. Data room status: What documents do they have ready? (cap table, financials, product demo, contracts)
4. Investment narrative: Can they articulate why this company / why now / why this team in 60 seconds?
5. Team composition: Current team size, roles filled, obvious gaps
6. Geography: LATAM-focused raise, US-focused raise, or both?
7. Timeline: When do they want/need to close a round?

**Step 2: Apply Frameworks** — Read references/readiness-rubric.md BEFORE scoring. Apply:
- YC "How to Raise Money" — Source: https://www.ycombinator.com/library/4A-how-to-raise-money
- a16z Fundraising Guide — Source: https://a16z.com/how-to-raise-money-startup/
- First Round Capital fundraising signals — Source: https://review.firstround.com/looking-for-signs-of-magic-how-our-partners-think-about-fundraising/
- Read @shared/frameworks/yc-metrics.md for stage-specific metric benchmarks

Score each dimension READY / CONDITIONAL / NOT READY with a specific reason.

**Step 3: Workflow** — Execute in order:
1. Confirm target stage → load stage-specific thresholds from references/readiness-rubric.md
2. Score each of 5 dimensions:
   a. **Metrics threshold** — Do current metrics meet stage benchmarks? (read yc-metrics.md)
   b. **Data room completeness** — Are the minimum documents ready for due diligence?
   c. **Narrative clarity** — Can they tell the story? Problem → Solution → Why Now → Why Us → Market
   d. **Team gaps** — Missing roles investors expect at this stage
   e. **Comparable raises** — What did similar LATAM companies raise at these metrics? (read references/latam-comparable-raises.md)
3. Produce overall verdict: READY TO RAISE / CONDITIONAL (fix X first) / NOT READY (minimum Y months away)
4. Apply LATAM timing context
5. Produce scorecard using TEMPLATES.md structure
6. Próximos Pasos in priority order

**Step 4: Output Format** — ALWAYS use TEMPLATES.md structure:
- Stage confirmation header
- 5-dimension readiness scorecard table: Dimension | Status | Gap | Fix Required
- Overall verdict: READY / CONDITIONAL / NOT READY
- Time estimate to ready (if CONDITIONAL or NOT READY): "3-6 months if you execute X"
- Comparable raises table from references/latam-comparable-raises.md
- LATAM Context box
- Próximos Pasos

**Step 5: LATAM Context** — Read references/latam-comparable-raises.md:
- LATAM fundraising timeline longer: start process 3 months earlier than US equivalent
- Warm intros more critical in LATAM — cold outreach to LATAM VCs has near-zero conversion
- LATAM investors often require 2-3 meetings before term sheet vs US fast-close norms
- Down rounds elevated: 14.5% of Q4 2025 rounds — bridge financing available but at cost
- Label Carta figures: "(US-sourced benchmark, Carta Q1 2026)" and LATAM figures: "(LATAM, LAVCA 2025)"

**Tone & Editorial Voice** — See @shared/frameworks/editorial-voice.md. The audit verdict must be direct. "NOT READY" means not ready — do not soften to "almost ready" or "close." If metrics are below benchmark, say which metric is short by how much.

**Step 6: Próximos Pasos** — Order actions by urgency. If NOT READY: the first action is the specific metric to fix. If CONDITIONAL: the first action is the highest-priority gap. If READY: the first action is scheduling the first 5 investor meetings.

**Sources** — YC How to Raise: https://www.ycombinator.com/library/4A-how-to-raise-money | a16z: https://a16z.com/how-to-raise-money-startup/ | First Round: https://review.firstround.com/looking-for-signs-of-magic-how-our-partners-think-about-fundraising/ | Carta Q1 2026 (via references/latam-comparable-raises.md) | shared/frameworks/yc-metrics.md

**FILE 2: references/readiness-rubric.md**

Title: "Fundraising Readiness Rubric — Stage-by-Stage Thresholds"
Sources: YC: https://www.ycombinator.com/library/4A-how-to-raise-money | a16z: https://a16z.com/how-to-raise-money-startup/ | Carta Q1 2026

Four stage sections (pre-seed / seed / Series A / Series B), each covering:

**Metrics threshold** — Minimum metric floor by model (SaaS MRR, marketplace GMV, fintech TPV). Read from shared/frameworks/yc-metrics.md for specifics.

Example for Seed SaaS:
- MRR: $25k–$50k OR sustained 20%+ MoM growth for 3+ months
- Retention: 80%+ net dollar retention OR monthly churn <5%
- LTV/CAC: ≥1.5:1 (Series A threshold: ≥3:1)

**Data room minimum** — What documents investors expect to see at first due diligence request:
- Pre-seed: cap table (even if just founders), product demo/video, 1-pager financials
- Seed: cap table, 12-month financials model (actuals + projections), product demo, 2+ customer references
- Series A: full data room (see data-room-builder skill), 18-month actuals, audited financials if available

**Narrative clarity checklist** — Does the pitch cover: Problem (quantified), Solution (differentiation), Why Now (timing), Why Us (unfair advantage), Market (SAM/SOM credible), Traction (evidence of PMF)?

**Team gaps by stage**:
- Pre-seed: 2+ co-founders with complementary skills. No gap = MVP shipped or deep customer insight
- Seed: Full-stack founding team (product, tech, GTM ownership). Gap = only technical founders, no GTM lead
- Series A: Department heads emerging. Gap = no VP Sales, no VP Product hired or identified

**Comparable raise benchmarks** — Reference references/latam-comparable-raises.md for LATAM-specific data.
