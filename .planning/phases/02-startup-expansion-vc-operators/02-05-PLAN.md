SKILL.md body (8 sections, under 500 lines):

**Step 1: Input Gathering** — Ask ALL in ONE message:
1. Product and business model (what it does, who it's for)
2. Target customer: Company size, industry, geography, decision-maker role (job title)
3. Current sales status: Zero customers / first 10 / $50k-$500k ARR / scaling?
4. Average contract value (ACV): rough estimate if known
5. Current biggest sales challenge: generating leads / converting demos / closing / churn
6. Geography: LATAM only / US / both
7. Team: Founder-led sales only / have SDR / have AE

**Step 2: Apply Frameworks** — Read references/sales-playbook-framework.md BEFORE designing:
- Aaron Ross "Predictable Revenue" — Outbound prospecting, SDR/AE split, sequence design
  Source: https://predictablerevenue.com/book/
- "The Challenger Sale" (Dixon & Adamson) — Challenger profile (teach, tailor, take control), objection handling
  Source: https://www.challengerinc.com/the-challenger-sale/
- Gong.io conversation data — Win rate benchmarks, talk-to-listen ratios
  Source: https://www.gong.io/resources/labs/

**Step 3: Workflow** — Execute in order:
1. Define ICP (Ideal Customer Profile): industry + company size + geography + trigger events + job title of buyer + job title of user
2. Design prospecting sequence (5-7 touches, multi-channel, Day 1 through Day 21)
3. Build objection handling matrix for the 5 most common objections in this market
4. Outline demo script (Opening → Discovery → Demo → Close → Next Step)
5. Recommend CRM setup (fields, stage definitions, conversion rate targets)
6. Apply LATAM context from references/latam-sales-context.md — adapt sequence to LATAM channels
7. Output using TEMPLATES.md structure
8. Próximos Pasos focused on the user's stated biggest sales challenge

**Step 4: Output Format** — ALWAYS use TEMPLATES.md structure:
- ICP definition summary (5-7 fields)
- Prospecting sequence table: Day | Channel | Message Type | Template Snippet | Expected Response Rate
- Objection handling matrix: Objection | Root Cause | Challenger Response | Evidence to Use
- Demo script outline (5 phases with time allocations)
- CRM setup recommendation (stage names, conversion rate targets, key fields)
- LATAM Context box
- Próximos Pasos

**Step 5: LATAM Context** — Read references/latam-sales-context.md BEFORE finalizing sequence:
- **WhatsApp is Channel 1 for LATAM B2B prospecting.** 85%+ open rates vs 22% cold email. Every prospecting sequence must include WhatsApp messages — not as a channel option but as the primary channel.
- Relationship-first motion: warm intro > LinkedIn connection > WhatsApp DM > cold email. Cold email only after exhausting warm channels.
- Sales cycle benchmarks: LATAM enterprise B2B typically 3–6 months vs 2–4 months US. Budget accordingly.
- Decision-maker title mapping: "Director Comercial" = VP Sales, "Gerente General" = CEO/MD, "Jefe de Sistemas" = IT Director, "Director de Operaciones" = COO. Use Spanish titles in ICP definition for LATAM sales.
- Reference request behavior: LATAM buyers frequently request reference calls. Build 2-3 happy customers as reference accounts before broad outbound.

**Tone & Editorial Voice** — See @shared/frameworks/editorial-voice.md. Call out the cold email trap directly: "A US-style 5-touch cold email sequence will fail in LATAM. Open rates are 22% vs 85% WhatsApp. If your sequence has no WhatsApp touches, rewrite it."

**Step 6: Próximos Pasos** — Address the founder's stated biggest challenge first. If "generating leads": first action is building a list of 50 target accounts this week. If "closing": first action is reviewing the last 3 lost deals for pattern.

**Sources** — Predictable Revenue: https://predictablerevenue.com/book/ | Challenger Sale: https://www.challengerinc.com/the-challenger-sale/ | Gong.io: https://www.gong.io/resources/labs/ | references/latam-sales-context.md

Header: "ALWAYS use this exact structure. Every section is required."

Example company: "LogiTrack" — Bogotá B2B SaaS for fleet and logistics management, targeting Colombian SME logistics companies (20-200 trucks), ACV $8,400/year ($700/month), 5 paying customers, founder-led sales.

ICP definition:
- Industry: Transportation & Logistics (Colombia primary, Mexico secondary)
- Company size: 20–200 vehicle fleet (revenue $2M–$50M)
- Geography: Colombia Tier 1 cities (Bogotá, Medellín, Cali), Mexico City expansion
- Trigger events: Fleet insurance renewal, new route expansion, driver management crisis, GPS system contract expiry
- Buyer title: Gerente de Operaciones / Director de Logística / Gerente General (companies <50 employees)
- User title: Coordinador de Flota / Supervisor de Operaciones

Prospecting sequence table (8 touches over 21 days):
| Day | Channel | Message Type | Template Snippet | Expected Response Rate |
|-----|---------|-------------|-----------------|----------------------|
| Day 1 | LinkedIn | Connection request | "Hola [Name], veo que gestionas la flota de [Company]. Tenemos clientes en logística colombiana con 35% menos tiempo en reportes. ¿Conectamos?" | 35-45% accept |
| Day 3 | WhatsApp | First message (if connected) | "Hola [Name], te escribo desde LogiTrack. Vi tu empresa en LinkedIn — ayudamos a [similar company] a reducir sus costos de flota un 22% en 90 días. ¿Tienes 15 minutos esta semana?" | 50-65% read |
| Day 5 | WhatsApp | Case study follow-up | Send 1-pager PDF: "[Similar company] case study" — no ask, just value | 70% open |
| Day 7 | WhatsApp | Soft ask | "¿Tuviste oportunidad de ver el caso de estudio? ¿Aplica a su operación?" | 40% response |
| Day 10 | Email | Formal intro | Subject: "Cómo [Company] puede reducir 22% sus costos de flota — LogiTrack" | 15-25% open, 3-8% reply |
| Day 14 | LinkedIn | Comment on post | Meaningful comment on their company post (not generic) | N/A — visibility play |
| Day 17 | WhatsApp | Final ask | "Última oportunidad esta semana — ¿15 minutos el jueves o viernes?" | 30% response from engaged prospects |
| Day 21 | WhatsApp | Breakup message | "Entiendo si no es prioridad ahora. ¿Puedo seguirte en 3 meses?" | 20% revive for later |

Objection handling matrix (5 rows):
| Objection | Root Cause | Challenger Response | Evidence to Use |
|-----------|-----------|--------------------|----|
| "Ya tenemos un sistema" | Status quo bias — switching cost fear | "¿Qué problema de su operación actual solucionaría si empezaran de nuevo?" (Challenger: reframe around unsolved problems) | Customer testimonial about switching cost being worth it |
| "No tenemos presupuesto" | Budget not allocated — not "no money" | "¿Cuánto les cuesta hoy el tiempo que pierden en reportes manuales de flota?" (Challenger: quantify cost of inaction) | ROI calculator showing 8-month payback |
| "Mándame información" | Avoiding decision — not genuine interest | "Claro — para enviarte lo más relevante, ¿cuál es su mayor dolor en gestión de flota hoy?" (Re-engage with discovery question) | Do NOT send generic PDF — send targeted 1-pager |
| "Primero habla con mi jefe" | Wrong level — need to go higher | "Claro — ¿quién sería la persona correcta? ¿Tienes 5 minutos para presentarme con [name]?" (Champion-building play) | Champion enablement deck |
| "Están muy caros" | Value not communicated | "¿Caros comparado con qué?" — then: ROI conversation, not price negotiation | Customer case study with hard numbers |

Demo script outline (5 phases, 45 minutes total):
1. Opening (5 min): Why you requested the meeting → their problem statement back to them
2. Discovery (10 min): 3 open questions about current pain (listen 70% / talk 30%)
3. Demo (20 min): Show ONLY the 2 features that solve the pain they described (not feature tour)
4. Close (5 min): "What would need to be true for this to be a fit?"
5. Next Step (5 min): Specific next step with date — never leave without a date

CRM setup table: Stage name | Definition | Conversion target | Key activity
5 stages: Prospecting → Qualified → Demo Scheduled → Demo Done → Proposal Sent → Closed Won/Lost

**FILE 3: references/latam-sales-context.md**

Title: "LATAM B2B Sales Context — Channels, Cycles, and Decision-Makers"

Sections:
1. WhatsApp as primary B2B channel:
   - 85%+ open rates for WhatsApp Business messages vs 22% cold email
   - Best practices: business account verified, professional photo, short messages, voice notes accepted in LATAM
   - When to use: After LinkedIn connection OR when you have phone number from referral
   - When NOT to use: First touch without any connection — still intrusive without warm intro

2. Relationship-first sales motion:
   - LATAM buyers buy from people they know or people vouched for by people they know
   - Warm intro > LinkedIn connect > WhatsApp DM > Cold email
   - Accelerants: industry events, accelerator networks, WhatsApp groups by industry vertical

3. Decision-maker titles (Spanish):
   - CEO/Founder of large company: Director General, CEO, Presidente
   - CEO of SME (<100 employees): Gerente General, Dueño, Socio
   - VP Sales: Director Comercial, Director de Ventas, Gerente Comercial
   - COO: Director de Operaciones, Gerente de Operaciones
   - IT Director: Jefe de Sistemas, Director de TI, Director de Tecnología
   - CFO: Director Financiero, Gerente Administrativo y Financiero

4. Sales cycle benchmarks:
   - SME (<50 employees): 2-4 months in LATAM vs 1-2 months US
   - Mid-market (50-500 employees): 3-6 months in LATAM vs 2-4 months US
   - Enterprise (500+ employees): 6-18 months in LATAM (procurement + legal slow due to manual processes)

5. Reference request behavior:
   - LATAM buyers request reference calls at much higher rate than US
   - Build 2-3 "lighthouse customer" accounts willing to take reference calls before broad outbound
   - Case studies in Spanish > English (obvious but often missed)

Sources: Gong.io LATAM data (directional): https://www.gong.io/resources/labs/ | WhatsApp Business statistics (Meta): https://business.whatsapp.com/ | Predictable Revenue: https://predictablerevenue.com/book/
