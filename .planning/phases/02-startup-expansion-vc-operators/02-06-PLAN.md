SKILL.md body (8 sections, under 500 lines):

**Step 1: Input Gathering** — Ask ALL in ONE message:
1. Company name, stage, and sector
2. Current metrics snapshot: ARR/MRR, growth rate, key unit economics if available
3. Funding ask: amount, pre-money valuation, proposed terms (or "no terms yet")
4. Investment thesis fit: How does this deal fit the fund's thesis?
5. Team background: Founders' names, prior companies, relevant experience
6. Competitive context: Who else is doing this? What's the moat?
7. Key risk(s): What is the GP/analyst most concerned about?
8. Fund context: What fund, what stage is the fund in (investment period vs. harvest)?

**Step 2: Apply Frameworks** — Read references/ic-memo-framework.md BEFORE writing the memo:
- a16z Deal Memo Structure: https://a16z.com/how-a16z-evaluates-startups/
- Sequoia Investment Memo framework: https://www.sequoiacap.com/article/elements-of-enduring-companies/
- Market Opportunity: Read @shared/frameworks/vc-backable-test.md for market sizing criteria
- Financial benchmarks: Read @shared/frameworks/yc-metrics.md for stage-appropriate thresholds

The memo must arrive at a clear recommendation: INVEST / PASS / DEFERRED (with conditions for Deferred).

**Step 3: Workflow** — Execute in order (9-section IC memo):
1. Executive Summary: 1 paragraph — investment thesis + key conviction + recommendation
2. Company Overview: what it does, stage, metrics snapshot
3. Market Analysis: TAM/SAM/SOM, market timing, "why now" — apply vc-backable-test.md
4. Product & Technology: differentiation, moat, roadmap, technical risk
5. Team Assessment: founder-market fit, team gaps, reference check considerations, **LATAM operator experience specifically**
6. Financial Analysis: current metrics vs stage benchmarks (yc-metrics.md), unit economics, projection basis
7. Competitive Landscape: positioning, key threats, defensibility
8. Risk Register: top 3-5 risks with mitigation thesis for each
9. Investment Recommendation: INVEST / PASS / DEFERRED, proposed terms, conditions

For LATAM deals: integrate LATAM Fund Context from references/latam-fund-context.md — FX risk section is mandatory if the company has USD cost base + local currency revenue.

**Step 4: Output Format** — ALWAYS use the exact structure in TEMPLATES.md. IC memo sections must appear in the order listed in Step 3. Every section requires substantive analysis — no section should be less than 2 sentences in a real memo.

**Step 5: LATAM Fund Context** — Read references/latam-fund-context.md BEFORE finalizing memo:
- FX risk: LATAM companies with USD costs (tech talent, AWS) and local currency revenue face structural margin compression in devaluation events. This MUST be in the Risk Register.
- Regulatory environment: Evaluate regulatory risk per country (Brazil LGPD, Mexico fintech law, Colombia data protection). Fintech/healthtech/edtech deals always include regulatory risk in Risk Register.
- LATAM operator experience: Prior US startup experience is insufficient evidence of LATAM market operator capability. Assess specifically: "Has this founder sold to LATAM enterprise buyers / built LATAM-native products / navigated LATAM regulatory environments?"
- Label benchmarks: "(US-sourced benchmark, YC / a16z)" and LATAM adjustments: "(LATAM, LAVCA 2025)"

**Tone & Editorial Voice** — See @shared/frameworks/editorial-voice.md. This is a GP/partner voice — senior decision-maker. The memo is written to convince a skeptical IC partner. Wishy-washy language is rejected: instead of "the team seems capable," write "Founder A's prior exit (Empresa X, $40M Series B) demonstrates capital-efficient scaling in the SME fintech vertical — directly applicable here."

**Step 6: Recommendation** — The final section must end with one of three positions: INVEST (with proposed terms), PASS (with specific reasons — one per fatal flaw), or DEFERRED (with exactly what must change before reconsidering, and when to re-evaluate).

**Sources** — a16z: https://a16z.com/how-a16z-evaluates-startups/ | Sequoia: https://www.sequoiacap.com/article/elements-of-enduring-companies/ | @shared/frameworks/yc-metrics.md | @shared/frameworks/vc-backable-test.md | references/ic-memo-framework.md

Sections:
1. FX risk assessment (mandatory for any deal with USD cost + local currency revenue):

   - Mexico: CNBV (fintech), COFEPRIS (health), SAT (tax compliance) — identify applicable regulatory body

   - Note: Regulatory timelines in LATAM typically 3-5x longer than US equivalents

3. LATAM operator experience assessment:
   - What counts: prior startup/scaleup at LATAM company, B2B sales to LATAM enterprise, navigated LATAM regulatory environment, has LATAM-specific network (investors, customers, talent)
   - What does NOT count: US startup experience, studying abroad, or "customer base includes LATAM"

4. Exit environment context:
   - LATAM M&A activity: Thinner than US — strategic acquirers limited (local banks, large LATAM conglomerates, US tech entering LATAM)
   - IPO: Brazil B3 exchange viable for $500M+ companies; others must target NYSE/NASDAQ
   - Secondary market: minimal — build for acquisition or US IPO at fund II/III horizon
