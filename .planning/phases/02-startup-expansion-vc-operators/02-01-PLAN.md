**Step 1: Input Gathering** — Ask ALL questions in ONE message before calculating:
1. Business model: SaaS / marketplace / transactional (e-commerce) / fintech — REQUIRED, calculation branches on this
2. For SaaS: Monthly recurring revenue (MRR), average revenue per account (ARPA/mo), monthly churn rate (%), gross margin (%), monthly S&M spend, new customers acquired last month
3. For marketplace: GMV, take rate (%), transaction count, customer acquisition cost per buyer and seller
4. For transactional/e-commerce: Average order value (AOV), repeat purchase rate (%), COGS %, S&M spend, customer count
5. For fintech: Total payment volume (TPV), revenue margin, default rate (%), S&M spend, active user count
6. Stage: idea / pre-revenue / early revenue / growth (affects benchmark thresholds)

State assumptions explicitly with [ASSUMPTION: ...] if data is missing.

**Step 2: Apply Frameworks** — Read references/unit-economics-formulas.md BEFORE calculating. Apply David Skok SaaS Metrics 2.0 definitions:
- CAC = Total S&M spend in period ÷ New customers acquired in same period
- LTV = ARPU × Gross Margin % ÷ Monthly Churn Rate [GROSS MARGIN ADJUSTED — not revenue-based]
- LTV/CAC ratio — floor is 3:1 at Series A; top-quartile LATAM SaaS hits 5:1
- CAC Payback = CAC ÷ (ARPU × Gross Margin %) — in months
- Contribution Margin = Revenue - COGS - Variable Costs (per unit or per period)
- Break-even = Fixed Costs ÷ Contribution Margin %

CRITICAL anti-pattern to call out: If user provides LTV = ARPU × lifetime_months (revenue-based), flag this as the most common unit economics error and recalculate with the gross-margin-adjusted formula.

**FILE 2: references/unit-economics-formulas.md**

Title: "Unit Economics Formulas — Definitions and Calculation Guide"
Source attribution header: "Primary source: David Skok, SaaS Metrics 2.0 (https://www.forentrepreneurs.com/saas-metrics-2/)"

Sections:

**SaaS Formulas**
- CAC = Total Sales & Marketing Spend in period ÷ New Customers Acquired in same period
  - Include only fully loaded S&M: salaries, ad spend, tools, allocated overhead
  - Common error: Using blended CAC (total customers) instead of period CAC (new customers)
- LTV = ARPA × Gross Margin % ÷ Monthly Churn Rate
  - CRITICAL: Must use gross-margin-adjusted ARPA, not raw revenue
  - Common error: LTV = ARPA × average customer lifetime months — this overstates LTV by 20-60%
  - Alternative: LTV = (Gross Margin per Customer per Month) ÷ Monthly Churn Rate
- LTV/CAC Ratio benchmarks by stage: Pre-seed: any positive, Seed: ≥1.5:1, Series A: ≥3:1, Series B+: ≥5:1
- CAC Payback = CAC ÷ (ARPA × Gross Margin %) — result in months

**Marketplace Formulas**
- CAC = Platform-side S&M ÷ New buyers (and separately: new sellers)
- Revenue = GMV × Take Rate %
- LTV for marketplaces: (Average transaction value × Take Rate × Gross Margin) ÷ Monthly transaction churn
- Unit margin = (Transaction revenue - Platform COGS per transaction)

**Transactional / E-commerce Formulas**
- CAC = Total S&M ÷ New customers in period
- LTV = AOV × Gross Margin % × Repeat purchase frequency per month ÷ Monthly attrition rate
- Contribution Margin per order = AOV - COGS - Shipping - Payment processing

**Fintech Formulas**
- Revenue = TPV × Net Take Rate (after interchange/payment processing costs)
- LTV = Monthly revenue per active user × Gross Margin % ÷ Monthly churn rate
- Default rate adjustment: Subtract expected credit loss from LTV for lending products

**Benchmark Table** (cite sources):
| Metric | Pre-Seed | Seed | Series A | Source |
|--------|---------|------|---------|--------|
| LTV/CAC | Any positive | ≥1.5:1 | ≥3:1 | David Skok |
| Gross Margin (SaaS) | N/A | ≥60% | ≥65% | Bessemer BVP |
| CAC Payback (SaaS) | N/A | ≤24mo | ≤18mo | OpenView |
