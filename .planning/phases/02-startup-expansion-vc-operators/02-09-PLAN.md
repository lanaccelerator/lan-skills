SKILL.md body (8 sections, GP/partner voice — senior decision-maker, D-23):

**Step 1: Input Gathering** — Ask ALL in ONE message:
1. Fund name, vintage, size, and LP base type (institutional / family office / mixed / DFI)
2. Target sectors: What industries are you focusing on? Why?
3. Target geography: LATAM only? Specific countries? Cross-border?
4. Target stage: Pre-seed / Seed / Series A / multi-stage?
5. Fund size implies check size: What is your expected initial check range?
6. Thesis prompt: "What do you believe about the next 5-10 years that most investors do not believe yet?" (core hypothesis prompt)
7. Anti-portfolio: What types of deals do you systematically pass on and why?

**Step 2: Apply Frameworks** — Read references/thesis-framework.md BEFORE writing:
- USV (Union Square Ventures) thesis format: https://www.usv.com/writing/2016/09/usv-thesis/
  Networks → Markets → Infrastructure → Applications layer structure
- a16z thesis writing: https://a16z.com/our-investment-thesis/
- Notation Capital emerging manager thesis format: https://notation.vc/
- Market sizing: Read @shared/frameworks/vc-backable-test.md for Gurley market sizing methodology

**Step 3: Workflow** — Execute in order (7-section thesis document):
1. **Core Hypothesis**: 1 paragraph — what do you believe that most investors don't yet believe? Make it falsifiable.
2. **Market Thesis**: Structural shifts enabling the opportunity (technology, regulation, demographics, infrastructure gaps)
3. **Pattern Matching**: What does a fundable company in this thesis look like? (stage, founder profile, metric thresholds, business model)
4. **Portfolio Construction**: Expected portfolio size, initial check sizes, reserve ratio, target ownership
5. **Anti-Portfolio Analysis**: Types of deals you systematically pass on — and the explicit reason for each exclusion
6. **Thesis Evolution**: How has this thesis changed based on what you've seen? What would cause it to change again?
7. **Market Sizing**: TAM/SAM/SOM for the thesis category — LATAM-specific sizing if LATAM-focused

For LATAM theses: integrate LATAM Fund Context from references/latam-fund-context.md — DFI LP considerations, structural shift data (LAVCA/Cuantico), family office LP alignment thesis.

**Step 4: Output Format** — ALWAYS use TEMPLATES.md structure (7-section thesis document).

**Step 5: LATAM Fund Context** — Read references/latam-fund-context.md:
- Sector bets: Fintech dominant (61% LATAM VC — Cuantico 2026), SaaS underfunded relative to opportunity
- Geography bets: Brazil + Mexico = 78.5% of LATAM capital — thesis must address why pan-LATAM or why country-specific
- DFI LP implications (IDB Invest, CAF, OPIC): Development impact thesis required — include development additionality metrics if DFI LPs are targeted
- Family office LP alignment: Thesis must speak to impact on LATAM economy — not just financial returns

**Tone & Editorial Voice** — See @shared/frameworks/editorial-voice.md. GP voice — this is how a fund GP convinces LPs to commit. The core hypothesis must be distinctive: "Fintech is big in LATAM" is NOT a thesis — it's a sector observation. A thesis is: "LATAM SMEs have been systematically underserved by formal banking because the cost-to-serve SMEs exceeds their revenue potential for traditional banks. New infrastructure (PIX, open banking mandates) creates the first infrastructure layer where fintech-native players can serve SMEs profitably. We believe the next 5 credit unicorns in LATAM will be fintech-native SME credit platforms."

**Step 6: Recommendation** — After generating the thesis draft, provide a "thesis stress test": "The strongest challenge to this thesis is [X]. Your response is [Y]. Investors will push on [Z] — prepare this answer."

**Sources** — USV: https://www.usv.com/writing/2016/09/usv-thesis/ | a16z: https://a16z.com/our-investment-thesis/ | Notation: https://notation.vc/ | Lux Capital: https://luxcapital.com/ | @shared/frameworks/vc-backable-test.md
