**references/due-diligence-criteria.md** (~70 lines):
5-dimension scoring rubric from LP perspective:
1. Track Record (1-5): Score 5 = top-quartile DPI >1.5x on realized portfolio + TVPI >2.0x on all investments; Score 1 = no realized returns, only paper marks
2. Portfolio Construction (1-5): # companies, check size, pro-rata reserve ratio, follow-on strategy — ILPA-aligned = 5
3. Terms (1-5): 2/20 is baseline; management fee ratchet, carry hurdle, GP commit ≥1% = toward 5; deviation without justification = toward 1
4. Team (1-5): GP stability, key person clause strength, succession plan, LATAM operator experience
5. LATAM Fit (1-5): Deal flow sources, portfolio company LATAM exposure, LATAM regulatory navigation demonstrated
Red flag triggers: DPI < 0.3x on a fund 5+ years old, no-fault divorce clause absent, management fee on committed capital with no step-down, GP commit < 0.5%.

**references/latam-context.md** (~50 lines):
- LATAM LP base: Corporates/CVCs ~25%, Family Offices ~30%, DFIs ~20%, Institutional ~25% (LAVCA 2025)
- LATAM fund median TVPI 2022 vintage: 1.33x vs US 1.47x (Carta Q1-2026, LATAM discount ~30-50%)
- Exit environment: Brazil 65% of LATAM exits 2024, Mexico 20%; IPO path limited (US listings or local bolsas)
- Key LP concern in LATAM: Currency reserves — USD-denominated fund investing in local-currency revenue companies creates structural FX drag
- Hurdle rates in LATAM funds: typically 6-8% vs 8% US (ILPA standard); justify deviation

- Step 5 LATAM Context: Read references/latam-context.md. Adjust for LATAM: longer time to exit (7-10 years vs 5-7 US), lower median returns (30-50% LATAM discount at same vintage), limited Series B+ local market — many LATAM angel investments require follow-on capital unavailable locally.
- Tone: @shared/frameworks/editorial-voice.md — "Your 5-company portfolio is not diversified. At a 12% LATAM angel-to-Series-A conversion rate, you need 20+ bets to expect 2 to reach Series A."
- Step 6 Próximos Pasos: 3-5 specific portfolio actions.

**references/construction-frameworks.md** (~70 lines):
Core portfolio math (AngelList / Correlation Ventures):
- Power law: top 10% of companies generate >90% of returns (Correlation Ventures, 6,000 investments analyzed)
- AngelList median angel portfolio: 20-30 companies for meaningful diversification
- Return target math: to target 3x portfolio, need minimum 20 companies with 1 expected 10x+; 5x target needs 30+
- Check size vs ownership: $50k at $5M pre-money = 1% ownership. At $250M exit = $2.5M gross return (50x). Dilution typically 70% over lifetime = $750k net = 15x — still strong
- Reserve ratio: 30-40% of fund for follow-ons; pro-rata rights must be negotiated at entry
- Stage allocation: 60% pre-seed/seed (higher risk, higher upside), 30% Series A (de-risked, lower upside), 10% opportunistic
- Thiel concentrated vs diversified: Thiel advocates 1-3 high-conviction bets; AngelList data supports diversification for most angels; LATAM context favors diversification given higher failure rate

**references/latam-context.md** (~50 lines):
LATAM-specific portfolio math:
- Median LATAM angel portfolio size: 8-12 companies (under-diversified vs optimal 20-30)
- LATAM angel-to-Series A conversion: ~12% (vs 22% US) — requires larger initial portfolio to expect same # of follow-ons
- Exit timeline: LATAM angel exits average 7-10 years (US: 5-7 years) — affects IRR calculation
- Brazil/Mexico = 78.5% of deal flow — geographic concentration risk for LATAM-only portfolio
- FX: USD-denominated returns but LATAM investments often in local currency — hedge recommendation
- Family office LATAM context: 30% of LATAM LP base; typically 5-10% allocation to venture, check sizes $100k-$500k
