**references/lp-analysis-criteria.md** (~70 lines):
LP evaluation criteria for each fund term (perspective layer ONLY — no term definitions):
- Management Fee: ILPA standard = 2% on committed capital, stepping down to 1.5-1.75% in harvest period. RED FLAG: >2.5% or no step-down. Negotiate: step-down trigger after investment period.
- Carry: ILPA standard = 20% with 8% hurdle. RED FLAG: 25%+ carry without commensurate GP commit. Negotiate: European waterfall (whole-fund carry) vs American waterfall (deal-by-deal).
- Hurdle Rate: ILPA standard = 8% preferred return. Acceptable: 6% for LATAM funds (lower risk-free rate). RED FLAG: <5% hurdle or no hurdle.
- GP Commit: ILPA standard = 1% of fund size minimum. RED FLAG: <0.5%. Negotiate: higher GP commit aligns incentives.
- Clawback: Must exist. RED FLAG: No clawback provision = LP has no recourse if GP over-distributes carry on early exits. Negotiate: escrow requirement for early carry distributions.
- Key Person Clause: Must name specific individuals, require LP vote to continue if >1 key person leaves. RED FLAG: Clause names the entire team (dilutes protection).
- No-Fault Divorce: ILPA standard = 75-80% LP vote to terminate GP. RED FLAG: >90% required (practically impossible to remove bad GPs).
ACCEPT criteria: all terms within ILPA band, no RED FLAGs. NEGOTIATE: 1-2 terms outside ILPA band but correctable. PASS: management fee >2.5% with no step-down AND no-fault divorce >90% AND GP commit <0.5% (all three = structural misalignment).

**references/latam-context.md** (~45 lines):
LATAM LP-specific term considerations:
- FX clause: USD fund investing in local currency — check if fund agreement specifies USD distributions or local currency at GP discretion; USD commitment with local currency distribution is a hidden return drag
- LATAM family office LP rights: informal relationship culture means LPs often sign without reading; ILPA education is key
- Political risk provisions: Argentina, Venezuela exposure — check if fund can gate withdrawals
- Brazilian LP legal vehicle: some LATAM funds structured as FIP (Fundo de Investimento em Participações) — different fee/carry treatment under Brazilian law vs Cayman LP

SKILL.md body:
- Step 1 Input Gathering: 7 questions in ONE message — company name + sector, current ARR/MRR or revenue (or "pre-revenue"), ask valuation (pre-money), stage (pre-revenue/pre-seed/seed/Series A), comparable company/transaction data if available, geographic market, and who is asking (founder checking their ask OR investor checking deal price — this determines framing of the output).
- Step 2 Apply Frameworks: Read references/valuation-methods.md for the 5 methods. Apply ALL applicable methods (skip DCF for pre-revenue). Triangulate: if 3+ methods agree within 30%, valuation is "in range"; if methods diverge >50%, flag as speculative.
- Methods to apply in order: (1) Revenue Multiple — current ARR × median SaaS multiple for stage/sector (Damodaran), (2) Comparable Transactions — 3-5 LATAM deals at similar stage/sector from latam-benchmarks.md, (3) Berkus Method — for pre-revenue only; 5 risk factors × $500k each (US) / $250k each (LATAM per LAVCA data), (4) Bill Payne Scorecard — 6 factors vs average pre-money for region/stage, (5) DCF — only for Series A+; note DCF is highly sensitive to terminal value assumptions.
- Step 3 Workflow: gather inputs → apply applicable methods → show each method's implied valuation → triangulate → flag if ask is >2x any single method → produce output per TEMPLATES.md → Próximos Pasos
- Step 4 Output: Valuation Analysis table (method | implied pre-money | notes), verdict (IN RANGE / STRETCHED / OVERPRICED), LATAM comparable transactions table, LATAM Context box with valuation discount annotation, recommendation framed for the audience (founder: "how to defend your ask" OR investor: "how to negotiate or walk away").
- Step 5 LATAM Context: Read references/latam-context.md. LATAM valuation discount: Seed pre-money median $3-5M (US: $6-9M Carta 2024). Series A median $8-12M (US: $15-25M). These are NOT lower quality — they are LATAM market reality. Apply discount consistently.
- Tone: @shared/frameworks/editorial-voice.md — "Your $12M pre-seed valuation is 4x the LATAM median. You will kill this round in a LATAM context unless you have extraordinary traction. US comparables do not apply here."
- Step 6 Próximos Pasos: Framed for audience type. Founder: 3 actions to defend the valuation. Investor: 3 actions to negotiate or price the risk.

**references/valuation-methods.md** (~75 lines):
5-method reference document:
1. Revenue Multiple (Damodaran): SaaS Seed = 5-8x ARR (US, 2024); LATAM adjustment = 3-5x ARR (LATAM, LAVCA 2025). Apply: multiply current ARR by median multiple → implied pre-money. Source: https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/
2. Comparable Transactions: Use named LATAM comparables (Konfío Seed, Alegra Seed, etc.). Build table: company | stage | ARR at raise | pre-money | multiple. Source: PitchBook LATAM data + LAVCA
3. Berkus Method (pre-revenue only): 5 factors — Sound idea (max $500k US / $250k LATAM), Prototype (max $500k/$250k), Quality mgmt team (max $500k/$250k), Strategic relationships (max $500k/$250k), Product rollout/sales (max $500k/$250k). Max pre-revenue valuation: $2.5M US / $1.25M LATAM. Source: Dave Berkus https://berkus.com/the-berkus-method/
4. Bill Payne Scorecard: Compare vs average pre-money for region/stage. 6 factors with weights: Team (30%), Size of Opportunity (25%), Product/Technology (15%), Sales Channels (10%), Stage of Business (10%), Other (10%). Multiply by regional average. Source: Bill Payne https://billpayne.com/
5. DCF (Series A+): Terminal value dominates for early-stage — flag the sensitivity. Use 10-year horizon, 5-7% terminal growth rate for LATAM SaaS, 25-35% discount rate (LATAM venture risk premium). Flag: DCF at seed stage is almost purely speculative terminal value.

**references/latam-context.md** (~50 lines):
LATAM startup valuation benchmarks with sources:
- Pre-seed pre-money median: $3-5M (LATAM, LAVCA 2025) vs $6-9M (US-sourced benchmark, Carta 2024)
- Seed pre-money median: $6-10M (LATAM, LAVCA 2025) vs $12-18M (US-sourced benchmark, Carta 2024)
- Series A pre-money median: $15-25M (LATAM, LAVCA 2025) vs $30-50M (US-sourced benchmark, Carta 2024)
- LATAM SaaS revenue multiples at Seed: 3-5x ARR (vs 5-8x US) — reflect lower exit multiples
- Fintech LATAM premium: +20-30% to SaaS multiples due to regulated market moat (CNBV/BACEN)
- Key adjustment: founders with US accelerator backgrounds often anchor on US valuations; LATAM investor reality is the benchmark that closes rounds
