YAML frontmatter:
```yaml
name: angel-investment-analyzer
description: >
  LP and angel investor due diligence on startup deals. ALWAYS invoke when an
  angel investor, LP, or seed fund evaluator needs to analyze whether a startup
  deal is worth investing in — producing a structured investment scorecard with
  thesis fit, red flags, LATAM comparables, and a INVEST / PASS / WATCH
  recommendation. NOT for founders evaluating their own startup (use
  venture-model-validator for that). NOT for VC fund operators writing IC memos
  (use deal-memo-writer for that).
metadata:
  author: lan-accelerator
  version: "1.0"
  suite: lp-angel
license: MIT
```

SKILL.md body must include:
- Step 1 Input Gathering: 6 questions in ONE message — deal name, sector/stage, ask + valuation, why this team, competitive moat, thesis fit for the investor's portfolio. State assumptions if unanswered.
- Step 2 Apply Frameworks: Read @shared/frameworks/vc-backable-test.md — YOU ARE THE INVESTOR, not the founder. For each of the 5 criteria, apply the investor's question (see references/angel-investment-criteria.md for the inversion table). Score each 1-5; any criterion below 3 is a PASS signal.
- Step 3 Workflow: gather inputs → score frameworks → flag red flags explicitly → research LATAM comparables → apply LATAM context (read references/latam-context.md) → produce scorecard output per TEMPLATES.md → end with Próximos Pasos
- Step 4 Output Format: ALWAYS use TEMPLATES.md structure. Output is an Angel Investment Scorecard: overall score (/25), framework breakdown table (investor question | score | finding), red flags section with [RED FLAG] callouts, LATAM comparables table, LATAM Context box (US benchmark labeled + LATAM-adjusted labeled), INVEST / PASS / WATCH recommendation with rationale.
- Step 5 LATAM Context: Read references/latam-context.md. Label every US benchmark `(US-sourced benchmark)` and LATAM figure `(LATAM, LAVCA 2025 / Cuantico 2026)`.
- Tone & Editorial Voice: See @shared/frameworks/editorial-voice.md — you are a seasoned angel. "This deal fails the monopoly test — three well-funded competitors entered this exact space in Q3 2025" is the expected tone.
- Step 6 Próximos Pasos: 3-5 specific due diligence actions for the investor within 2 weeks. Concrete: "Request 12-month customer cohort retention data from the founder." Not vague: "Do more diligence."
- Sources section with URLs for Jason Calacanis "Angel" methodology, AngelList return data, Naval Ravikant notes, and @shared/frameworks/vc-backable-test.md.

**references/angel-investment-criteria.md** (~60 lines):
Investor perspective inversion table — for each of the 5 framework criteria in vc-backable-test.md, show the INVESTOR question vs the FOUNDER question:
| Criterion | Founder question (venture-model-validator) | Investor question (this skill) |
|---|---|---|
| Market Size | "Is my TAM large enough?" | "Can this SAM return 10x my check at my ownership %?" |
| Monopoly/Moat | "Can I dominate a niche?" | "Is there a defensible moat preventing my investment from being crowded out?" |
| PMF Signals | "Do I have PMF evidence?" | "Has this team proven they can find and retain customers at this price?" |
| Enduring company | "Can this be category-defining?" | "Is there a credible path to the exit multiple I need?" |
| Market timing | "Is now the right time to build?" | "Is now the right time to invest — or will I be too early/late?" |

Also include: INVEST criteria (score ≥18/25, no criterion below 3, clear path to 10x), PASS criteria (any criterion below 2, or market too small to return check), WATCH criteria (score 14-17, one criterion at 3, meaningful catalyst could unlock). Red flag triggers: "no competition" claim, pre-revenue with no comparable exits in sector, valuation >10x revenue at pre-seed.

**references/latam-context.md** (~50 lines):
LATAM angel investment benchmarks — all sourced from LAVCA 2025 / AngelList LATAM data:
- Median LATAM angel check: $25k-$100k USD (US median: $100k-$500k) (LATAM, LAVCA 2025)
- LATAM seed rounds 2024: 82 deals < $1M, down 39% YoY (LATAM, LAVCA 2025)
- Brazil + Mexico = 78.5% of LATAM angel deal flow (LATAM, LAVCA 2025)
- LATAM angel-to-Series A conversion: ~12% (US: ~22%) — lower follow-on rate means angel must be more selective
- Fintech + edtech = 45% of LATAM seed deals (LATAM, LAVCA 2025)
- Median time angel→Series A: 24 months LATAM vs 18 months US
- Include application instructions: when a deal is in Brazil/Mexico, check if founder has navigated local regulatory environment; when fintech, require CNBV/BACEN regulatory path clarity before scoring moat as >3
