# LATAM Context: Weekly Team Meeting

See @shared/frameworks/latam-benchmarks.md for canonical LATAM benchmark data.
When a KPI is compared against a benchmark, label US figures as
`(US-sourced benchmark, [Source])` and add the LATAM figure.

## Small teams without a tracker

- Many LATAM Seed teams (5-15 people) run tasks in WhatsApp groups, a
  spreadsheet, or nowhere. The skill must not require a project tracker.
  Commitments recorded in the previous agenda page and in meeting notes are
  the tracker.
- If tasks only live in WhatsApp, ask the user to paste the relevant thread
  or a weekly summary; do not try to infer commitments from chat banter.

## Calendar

- Long weekends and national holidays vary by country (feriados puente in
  Argentina, Semana Santa across the region, Carnaval in Brazil). A week with
  a holiday has fewer working days; adjust the pace rule expectation and say
  so instead of flagging every team as off track.
- Quarter close often coincides with tax deadlines and aguinaldo or 13th
  salary (December); finance capacity drops, plan around it.

## Currency and metrics

- Report revenue KPIs in the currency the company reports to investors
  (usually USD). If revenue is collected in local currency, show the FX rate
  used and note FX-driven deltas separately from real growth; a 10% move in
  the local currency is not a sales win.

## Culture

- Public recognition matters and is cheap: name the person behind each win.
- Public criticism lands hard in many LATAM teams. Delivery rates are shown
  by team, not by person; individual misses go to one-on-ones.
