# Metrics System — Trezo
**Date:** April 2026
**Stage:** Early post-revenue (0–1M ARR)
**Business Model:** B2B SaaS (recurring billing for Paraguayan SMBs)

---

## North Star Metric

**Active paying customers with successful recurring billing in the last month.**

This metric captures value delivered to the SMB (their billing actually works) and revenue to Trezo simultaneously. It passes all three NSM tests:

1. If it drops 15% in a week, the entire org investigates (not just one team).
2. Captures customer value AND business value.
3. Durable for 12+ months given the business model.

## Anti-patterns Detected and Removed

- **"Launch $99 tier"** — original phrasing was a task. Reformulated as outcome: "Launch $99/month tier with 10 customers."
- **"Close Seed round"** — discarded as Objective. Not controllable in one quarter by the founder alone. Replaced with controllable inputs (conversations + term sheet exploration).
- **"MRR of $130K"** — kept as KR but flagged as risk given current $95K. The team must explicitly own the gap.
- **No "LTV:CAC" KPI** — would be premature at this stage. Replaced with CAC payback, which is the right early-post-revenue efficiency metric.

## Cadence

- Quarterly OKR review and reformulation in the last week of the quarter.
