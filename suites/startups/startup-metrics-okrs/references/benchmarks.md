# KPI Benchmarks for Metrics & OKRs (checked 27 Oct 2026)

**When to use:** startup-metrics-okrs, Step 2.3, to fill the Benchmark column. Label US data
`(US-sourced benchmark)`. Rows marked **directional** are practitioner ranges (LAN's read),
not published datasets: show them as orientation, never as a hard bar. No public LATAM
report publishes KPI benchmarks; the canonical LATAM data layer is
shared/latam-benchmarks.md.

## Pre-revenue

| KPI | Benchmark | Source |
|-----|-----------|--------|
| Sean Ellis test, % "very disappointed" | 40%+ signals PMF | Sean Ellis PMF survey |
| Discovery interviews per week | 5-10 | Directional |
| Landing page visitor-to-signup (B2B) | 2-5% | Directional |
| Time to first paying customer | Shrinking month over month | Directional |

## Early post-revenue (under $1M ARR)

| KPI | Benchmark | Source |
|-----|-----------|--------|
| Weekly growth | 5-7%/week good, 10%/week exceptional during YC | Paul Graham, Startup = Growth (Sep 2012) |
| Weekly growth, AI-native | Close to 10%/week for top funds | LAN's read (Oct 2026) |
| YoY growth under $1M ARR | Median 100% AI-native vs 75% other SaaS | High Alpha and Growth Unhinged, 2025 SaaS Benchmarks (12 Nov 2025) |
| MoM growth, single-country LATAM B2B | 10%+ needs no explanation at seed | Directional |
| Monthly logo churn | under 5%, target under 3% | Directional |
| Cohort retention | 6-month cohorts flattening, 70%+ active | Directional |
| CAC payback (gross margin) | under 12 months | Directional |
| NPS | above 30 healthy, above 40 strong | Directional |

## Post-PMF ($1M+ ARR)

| KPI | Benchmark | Source |
|-----|-----------|--------|
| YoY growth $1-5M ARR | Median 110% AI-native vs 40% other SaaS | High Alpha and Growth Unhinged, 2025 SaaS Benchmarks |
| YoY growth $1-10M ARR, top performers | 230%+ | Bessemer, Scaling to $100 Million (Sep 2021) |
| NRR | Public SaaS median 110%; top performers 130%+ | Clouded Judgement (23 Oct 2026); Bessemer (2021) |
| Gross margin, SaaS | ~70% average at $1-25M ARR | Bessemer, Scaling to $100 Million (2021) |
| Gross margin, AI products | ~41% (2024), projected ~52% (2026) | ICONIQ State of AI bi-annual snapshot (Jan 2026) |
| Burn multiple (net burn / net new ARR) | under 1x amazing, 1-1.5x great, 1.5-2x good, over 3x bad | David Sacks, The Burn Multiple (Apr 2020) |
| LTV:CAC | 3:1 minimum | David Skok, SaaS Metrics 2.0 |
| Rule of 40 | growth % + profit margin % ≥ 40 | Bessemer; applies from scale, not at seed |

## AI-native additions

| KPI | Why |
|-----|-----|
| Tasks completed and accepted without human fix | The outcome customers pay for; the honest NSM for agents |
| Inference cost per unit of work | Drives gross margin; should fall every month |
| Revenue per employee | AI-native teams run small; investors compare it |
