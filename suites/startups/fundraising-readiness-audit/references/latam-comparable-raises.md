# LATAM Comparable Raises: Stage Benchmarks and Market Data

**When to use:** dimension e (comparable raises) and the LATAM Context box of the scorecard.

**Annotation protocol:**
- LATAM figures: `(LATAM, <source>, <year>)`. US figures: `(US-sourced benchmark, <source>, <year>)`.
- Figures marked **directional** are LAN's operator read, not a published dataset. Say so in the
  output; never upgrade a directional figure to a sourced one.
- Never present US figures as LATAM norms.

---

## 1. Stage ranges (directional)

No public dataset publishes LATAM round sizes and valuations by stage. The ranges below are LAN
Accelerator's operator read from deals seen in 2024-2026. Use them to frame, then replace them with
live comparables (section 3) whenever possible.

| Stage | Typical round | Typical pre-money | What usually gets funded | Active investor examples |
|-------|---------------|-------------------|--------------------------|--------------------------|
| Pre-seed | USD 100K-500K | USD 2M-5M cap | Team + MVP + first paying customers | Angels, accelerators (LAN, 500 Global, Platanus, Y Combinator) |
| Seed | USD 750K-3M | USD 5M-12M | Repeatable early revenue, clear growth rate | Seed funds (Magma Partners, Latitud, Kamay, iThink VC, Norte, ONEVC, MAYA, Canary, DILA) |
| Series A | USD 3M-15M | USD 20M-60M | USD 1M+ ARR or equivalent, 2-3x YoY, working GTM | KASZEK, monashees, QED, Valor, NXTP, ALLVP |
| Series B+ | USD 15M+ | USD 60M+ | Category leadership, path to USD 10M+ ARR | KASZEK, General Atlantic, General Catalyst, SoftBank, Endeavor Catalyst |

Investor names come from LAVCA's "most active fund managers" lists (2024-1H 2025), plus ALLVP
and accelerators known for the stage. Check each fund's current activity before recommending it.

**How to read it for the founder:**
- 2 in 100 LATAM seed companies raise a Series A within a year; about 12 in 100 within three.
  A seed round must buy enough runway to reach Series A metrics without a second raise, or plan a
  bridge from day one.
- Capital is concentrating in fewer, larger rounds. Outside Brazil and Mexico, expect smaller
  rounds and fewer local leads; a regional or US co-lead is often needed.

## 3. Live comparables (required, never invented)

Comparable raises are the most persuasive evidence and the easiest to fabricate. Rules:

1. Search for 3-5 raises in the same vertical, stage and region from the last 24 months:
   Crunchbase News, LAVCA Venture Bulletin, Contxto, LatamList, Bloomberg Línea, the fund's own
   announcement.
2. For each: company, country, stage, amount, lead investor, date, and the source URL. Revenue or
   valuation only if the source states it.
3. If nothing verifiable is found, write "No verified comparables found" and fall back to the
   directional ranges in section 1. Never fill the table with plausible-looking companies.

## 4. LATAM fundraising process norms (LAN operator read)

1. **Plan 6-9 months for a LATAM seed process**: plus 2-4 months of legal close if a flip or
   cross-border structure is involved. Start with 15+ months of runway.
2. **Warm intros dominate.** Cold outreach converts poorly with LATAM funds; a portfolio-founder
   intro is the strongest path.
3. **One lead, then followers.** LATAM rounds are syndicated; focus first on 3-5 potential leads.
4. **USD denomination is standard.** FX exposure is the founder's to explain: show revenue and
   unit economics in USD at current and stressed exchange rates.
