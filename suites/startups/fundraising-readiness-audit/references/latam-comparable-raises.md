*(LATAM, LAVCA 2025)*

**LATAM discount:** All LATAM valuations are 40–60% below equivalent US stage at the same metrics.
This is a market pricing norm, not a quality signal. A $10M pre-money LATAM Seed round is
equivalent to a $20M–$25M pre-money US Seed round at the same metrics.

## Q4 2025 Market Context

- **Down rounds:** 14.5% of Q4 2025 rounds were down rounds — still elevated vs historical norms.
  Bridge financing is available but dilutive. *(US-sourced benchmark, Carta Q1 2026)*
- **Seed-to-Series A time:** Median 1.9 years in Q4 2025. *(US-sourced benchmark, Carta Q1 2026)*
- **LATAM VC total 2025:** $4.126B across 681 rounds, +13.8% YoY. *(LATAM, Cuantico 2026)*
- **Pre-seed contraction:** 152 pre-seed deals in 2025 vs 660 in 2022 — a 77% drop.
  Earlier-stage companies must demonstrate seed-level signals faster. *(LATAM, Cuantico 2026)*
- **Brazil + Mexico:** 78.5% of LATAM capital. If raising outside these markets, expect
  30–40% valuation discount vs Brazilian peers. *(LATAM, Cuantico 2026)*

## LATAM Fundraising Process Norms

These affect timing and strategy — apply to every audit regardless of stage:

1. **LATAM VCs require 2–3 meetings before term sheet.** US norms (term sheet after 2
   meetings) do not apply. Build 6–8 weeks into timeline per fund.

2. **Cold outreach to LATAM VCs has near-zero conversion.** Warm intros through portfolio
   founders or mutual advisors are the only reliable path to first meeting.

3. **Start 3 months earlier than US equivalents.** LATAM due diligence cycles are longer;
   legal closing takes 2–4 months after term sheet vs 4–8 weeks in US.

4. **LATAM investors often co-invest.** A lead investor is required; they will syndicate.
   Do not approach 10 funds simultaneously — focus on 3–5 leads.

5. **USD denomination is standard.** Even for Mexico and Brazil raises, term sheets are in USD.
   FX exposure is the founder's problem — address this in investor conversations.
