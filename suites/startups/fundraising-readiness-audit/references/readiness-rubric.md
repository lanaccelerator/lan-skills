# Fundraising Readiness Rubric: Stage-by-Stage Thresholds

**Sources:**
- YC, A Guide to Seed Fundraising: https://www.ycombinator.com/library/4A-how-to-raise-money
- a16z, 16 Startup Metrics: https://a16z.com/16-startup-metrics/
- Carta, State of Private Markets: 2025 in review (Feb 2026): https://carta.com/data/state-of-private-markets-q4-2025/
- shared/yc-metrics.md, metric definitions and calculation methods

**When to use:** Read this file in full before scoring any dimension. Apply the thresholds
for the founder's target stage. Do not mix stage thresholds.

---

## Pre-Seed Stage

**What investors are buying:** The team and the thesis. Not metrics.

### Metrics Threshold

- **SaaS:** MRR $0–$10k OR strong PMF signal (10+ paying customers with retention data)
- **Marketplace:** GMV growth directional (3× MoM); no minimum floor required
- **Fintech:** Regulatory pathway clear; pilot or waitlist with evidence of demand
- **Minimum bar:** Any paying customer with a credible explanation of why they paid

READY: Has paying customers or a waitlist with committed beta users and quantified problem.
CONDITIONAL: Idea stage with no customer evidence. Strong team can offset.
NOT READY: No customer conversations completed. No evidence of problem severity.

### Data Room Minimum

- Cap table (founders only is acceptable)
- Product demo, prototype, or Figma mockup
- 1-page financial overview (use of funds for 18 months)
- Founder bios with relevant background evidence

### Narrative Clarity Checklist

The pre-seed narrative must answer: Problem (have you experienced it?), Solution (why this,
why now), Why Us (unique ability to solve it, domain expertise, prior network, unfair access).
Market sizing is important but directional estimates are acceptable at pre-seed.

### Team Gaps

- Minimum: 2 co-founders with complementary skills (technical + domain OR technical + business)
- No gap: MVP shipped or deep customer insight from prior role
- Flag: Solo founder with no technical co-founder is a risk at pre-seed; acknowledge it

### Comparable Raises

Reference references/latam-comparable-raises.md: pre-seed LATAM = USD 100K-500K on a
USD 2M-5M cap (directional, LAN operator read). Accelerator + angel round typical.

## Seed Stage

**What investors are buying:** Evidence of PMF and a repeatable growth mechanism.

### Metrics Threshold

**SaaS:**
- MRR: $10k–$100k; strong signals at $25k–$50k
- MRR growth (MoM): 15–30%; sustained 20%+ for ≥3 months is investable
- Logo churn (annual): <10%; monthly churn <5% preferred
- NRR: >100% (net positive expansion)
- Gross margin: >65%
- LTV/CAC: ≥2:1 minimum; 3:1 or higher preferred

Read shared/yc-metrics.md for SaaS Benchmarks by Stage table.

**Marketplace:**
- GMV growth: 3–5× YoY
- Take rate: 5–20%, stabilizing
- Liquidity: >50% of demand met

**Fintech:**
- TPV or AUM growing consistently
- Unit economics of the transaction model positive or clear path to positive
- Regulatory license obtained or clear pathway

READY: Metrics at or above Seed floor on 3+ dimensions.
CONDITIONAL: 1–2 metric gaps; clear path to close within 60–90 days.
NOT READY: Core model not working (churn >10%/mo, negative margin, no retention).

### Data Room Minimum

- Cap table (updated, with any SAFEs or convertible notes listed)
- 12-month financial model: actuals for all months since founding + 12-18 month projections
- Product demo (recorded video, minimum 3 minutes covering core use case)
- 2+ customer references willing to speak with investors
- One-pager or pitch deck (narrative + metrics)

Missing any one of these: CONDITIONAL at best. Missing two: NOT READY.

### Narrative Clarity Checklist

Must answer all six: Problem (quantified pain), Solution (differentiation vs existing),
Why Now (specific enabling event in last 2–3 years), Why Us (unfair advantage),
Market (credible SAM/SOM with bottom-up calculation), Traction (PMF evidence).

"Why Now" failures are common. "The market is ready" is not a timing trigger.
A timing trigger is: new regulation, new infrastructure (PIX, SPEI), behavior shift
with data, or a technology unlock that did not exist 3 years ago.

### Team Gaps

- Expected: Full-stack founding team covering product, technology, and GTM
- Critical gap: Two technical founders with no GTM owner = NOT READY for Seed pitch
  (investors will ask who closes deals; must have an answer)
- Acceptable: One founder covering two functions if early traction validates the model

### Comparable Raises

Reference references/latam-comparable-raises.md: seed LATAM = USD 750K-3M on
USD 5M-12M pre-money (directional, LAN operator read). Only 7% of LATAM seed companies
raise a Series A within two years (LATAM, LAVCA 2025): the seed must fund the whole path.

## Series A Stage

**What investors are buying:** Repeatable, scalable growth with proven unit economics.

### Metrics Threshold

**SaaS:**
- MRR: $100k–$1M; strong at $200k+ MRR ($2.4M+ ARR)
- MRR growth (YoY): 2× minimum; 3× preferred
- Logo churn (annual): <7%
- NRR: >110%; top-quartile Series A at >120%
- Gross margin: >70%
- LTV/CAC: ≥3:1
- CAC payback: <12 months

Read shared/yc-metrics.md, a16z 16 Metrics section for full definitions.

Rule of 40 benchmark: target >20 at Series A close.

READY: All core metrics at or above floor for 2+ consecutive quarters.
CONDITIONAL: 1 metric gap with clear fix; strong NRR can offset weak MRR growth.
NOT READY: Unit economics broken (LTV/CAC <2:1, churn >10%/mo, payback >18 months).

### Data Room Minimum

Full data room per data-room-builder skill. Key additions vs Seed:
- 18 months of actuals (monthly P&L, MRR bridge, cohort retention by vintage)
- Audited financials if available (or CPA-reviewed)
- Customer contracts (anonymized with NDA) showing ACV and contract length
- Cap table with fully-diluted ownership model
- Reference customers willing to speak on the record

### Narrative Clarity Checklist

Series A narrative must include: unit economics that close, go-to-market that repeats,
market leadership argument (why you and not the next company), and use-of-funds that
maps to a clear Series B milestone 18–24 months out.

"We need capital to grow" is not a Series A story. "At $X MRR we will add $Y ARR
per sales rep with Z-month payback, and we need 15 reps to reach Series B metrics
in 18 months" is a Series A story.

### Team Gaps

- Department heads emerging or identified: VP Sales or Head of Sales, VP Product or PM leader
- Engineering team: 5+ engineers or clear hiring plan
- Critical gap: CEO still closing every enterprise deal = not Series A ready

### Comparable Raises

Reference references/latam-comparable-raises.md: Series A LATAM = USD 3M-15M on
USD 20M-60M pre-money (directional, LAN operator read). Active leads: KASZEK, monashees,
QED, Valor, NXTP, ALLVP (LAVCA most-active lists, 2024-1H 2025).

## Series B Stage

**What investors are buying:** Proven category leadership and a path to market dominance.

### Metrics Threshold

**SaaS:**
- ARR: $5M–$20M+ (MRR $400k+)
- ARR growth (YoY): 2× minimum; 3× top-quartile
- NRR: >120%; best-in-class at >130%
- Gross margin: >70%
- LTV/CAC: ≥4:1
- Rule of 40: >40

### Data Room Minimum

Full data room plus: audited financials (required), board deck (last 4 quarters),
management team org chart, customer concentration analysis (revenue from top 10 customers).

### Narrative Clarity Checklist

Series B must answer: market leadership (are you winning?), category creation or capture
(what's the endgame?), international expansion or vertical depth (what's next?), and
capital efficiency argument (why this amount, why now?).

### Team Gaps

- C-suite largely filled: CFO or VP Finance, CRO or VP Sales, CTO/CPO with dedicated teams
- Board composition: 2 independent board members expected

### Comparable Raises

Reference references/latam-comparable-raises.md: Series B+ LATAM = USD 15M+ on
USD 60M+ pre-money (directional). Series C+ capital in LATAM fell 56.8% in 2025
(LATAM, Cuantico 2026): late-stage money is scarce, plan for profitability as an option.

## AI-Native Startups (apply on top of the stage thresholds)

AI-native companies are judged on the same bar, with four extra checks:

- **Revenue quality.** Separate contracted recurring revenue from usage that can vanish and from
  paid pilots. Pilots and "experimental budget" revenue do not count as ARR. Show net revenue
  retention by cohort, not only top-line growth.
- **Gross margin after inference.** Report gross margin with model and inference costs included.
  A margin far below software norms needs a credible path (smaller models, caching, pricing).
- **Pricing model.** Outcome-based or usage-based pricing is normal; the founder must show what
  the customer pays per unit of value and how that scales.
- **Growth and team size.** Investors expect faster growth with smaller teams. LAN's read: top
  funds look for about 10% week-over-week at the earliest stage (Paul Graham, 2012, called 5-7%
  "good" and 10% "exceptional"). Revenue per employee is a standard question.
- **Valuation context.** US Series A AI startups priced 38% above non-AI peers in 2025
  (US-sourced benchmark, Carta 2025 in review). Do not import that premium to LATAM without
  local comparables.
