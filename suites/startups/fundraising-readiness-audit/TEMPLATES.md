## Overall Verdict: CONDITIONAL

**2 blockers before starting serious investor conversations:**

1. **Hit $25k MRR or sustain 20%+ MoM growth for 2+ consecutive months.** At current
   $18k MRR and 15% MoM growth, you are below the Seed threshold on metrics. At 15% MoM
   growth, you reach $25k MRR in approximately 2.5 months. Hold that level for 1 additional
   month before approaching VCs to show trend, not a single data point.

2. **Produce a 12-month financial model with actuals + projections.** No Seed fund will
   request a second meeting without seeing this. Cap table alone does not constitute a data
   room at Seed stage.

**Estimated time to READY:** 3–4 months if execution is strong.

## LATAM Context

- **(LATAM, LAVCA 2025):** only 7% of LATAM seed companies raise a Series A within two years and
  about 12% within three. EduTech MX's seed must fund the full path to Series A metrics; plan a
  bridge option from day one.
- **(US-sourced benchmark, Carta, Q4 2025):** median seed to Series A in the US is 1.9 years.
  Budget at least 24 months between seed close and Series A close in LATAM.
- **(LATAM, Cuantico 2026):** USD 4.126B across 681 rounds in 2025 with average ticket up 16%:
  capital is concentrating in fewer companies. Brazil + Mexico take ~73%, which helps a Mexico
  HQ.
- **(Directional, LAN operator read):** a LATAM seed process takes 6-9 months. Start outreach in
  month 1 to close by month 6-7, with 15+ months of runway at kickoff.
- **Warm intros are required.** First meetings with Magma Partners, ALLVP or Latitud should come
  through a portfolio founder or a trusted advisor, not a cold email.

## Next Steps

1. **Grow MRR to $25k.** At 15% MoM: $18k → $20.7k → $23.8k → $27.4k, month 3 crosses
   threshold. Track MoM growth rate; if it drops below 15%, diagnose cause immediately.

2. **Build the financial model this week.** 12 months of actuals + 18-month projection.
   Model three scenarios (base / bear / bull). Use cohort-based churn, not aggregate.
   This is a prerequisite for any first meeting, not a nice-to-have.

3. **Record a 3-minute product demo video.** Screen-record the full school admin flow. Host on
   Loom. This is mandatory for the data room and for async investor review.

4. **Identify your GTM lead.** This is not optional at Seed. Options: (a) early sales hire
   with edtech procurement experience, (b) advisor with deep SEP/school district relationships
   who commits to introductions, (c) promote an existing customer champion to part-time BD role.
   Present this person at your first investor meeting.

5. **Map your warm intro paths to 3 target funds.** Target: Magma Partners (Mexico/Colombia
   edtech focus), ALLVP (Mexico generalist Seed), LAN Accelerator (if not already a portfolio
   company). Identify the person in your network who can intro you. Do not cold email.
