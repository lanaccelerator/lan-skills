## Phase C: Closing

### Term Sheet Checklist (on receipt)

Review every term against references/shared/brad-feld-terms.md:

| Term | What to check | Red flag if... |
|------|---------------|----------------|
| Liquidation preference | 1× non-participating preferred is standard | Participating preferred or >1× multiplier |
| Anti-dilution | Broad-based weighted average is standard | Full ratchet |
| Board composition | 3-person board (2 founders + 1 investor) is standard Seed | Investor board majority before Series B |
| Drag-along | Requires majority of preferred AND common | Only preferred consent required |
| Pro-rata rights | Major investor threshold: investors >$500k typically get pro-rata | Super pro-rata (more than their share) |
| Option pool | Expansion pre-close (dilutes founders, not investors) | Size >20% post-money |
| Dividends | Non-cumulative standard | Cumulative dividends accrue and compound |
