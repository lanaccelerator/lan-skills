# Follow-up Sequence Rules

## Principles

1. **Every touch adds value.** A case study, an answer, an insight, a
   resource. "Just checking in" and "¿pudiste ver la propuesta?" alone are
   banned.
2. **Context first.** Reference something specific from the call (a quote,
   a number, a person) so the message cannot be mistaken for a template.
3. **Social proof by similarity** (Cialdini, *Influence*): proof works when
   the reference customer resembles the prospect in industry, size and
   problem. A similar mid-size distributor beats a famous logo from another
   industry.
4. **One ask per message.** Each message ends with a single, easy next step.
5. **Multi-threading.** About 13 people weigh in on the average B2B purchase
   (Forrester). Follow-ups go to every role in the account map (decider,
   champion, user, finance or procurement, IT), each written for that role,
   coordinated by one account owner so two messages never land the same day
   or contradict each other. Tell the champion who else you are talking to;
   never go over their head as pressure.
6. **Close the loop.** The last message makes it easy to say no. A clear no
   frees the pipeline; a perpetual maybe pollutes the forecast.

## Cadence

Day 0 is the decision meeting, not the day the proposal is written. The
business case goes out BEFORE the meeting; the proposal goes out the same
day AFTER it.

| Day | Step | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| -5 (approx.) | Business case to the champion + short pre-wire call | Full sentences; collect objections; confirm decider and process |
| -2 | Executive one-pager to decider and champion | Exactly 48 hours before; recommendation, three reasons, investment, risk, decision requested |
| 0 | Decision meeting; proposal sent the same day after it | Open with the recommendation; ask for the decision; leave with a dated next step |
| 2 | Case study for their top pain | Measured metric, same industry and size |
| 5 | Answer to the likely objection | Chosen by MEDDICC gap (table below) |
| 9 | Teaching insight + mutual action plan check | One insight backed by data |
| 14 | Direct question on decision and timing | Offer a call with the decider |
| 21 | Close the loop | Easy to say no |

If the decider cannot attend on Day 0, move the meeting; do not present to a
proxy and do not send the proposal into the void. Adjust:
- Prospect gave a decision date: compress the sequence before that date,
  pause after it until the date passes.
- Prospect asked for time ("después de fin de mes"): one acknowledgment,
  then resume the day after the stated date.
- Prospect replied: the sequence stops; respond to the reply.
- Prospect accepted: the sequence stops; send the contract draft and ask
  which vendor-portal documents they need, the same day.

## Objection pre-emption by MEDDICC gap

| Gap | Likely objection | Day 5 message content |
|---|---|---|
| Economic buyer MISSING | "Lo tengo que consultar" | This gap should be closed in the Day -5 pre-wire; if still open, a one-page summary for the signer: cost, risk, payback |
| Metrics MISSING | "No sé si vale la pena" | ROI worksheet with their numbers to fill |
| Competition MISSING | "Estamos viendo otras opciones" | Comparison criteria (not a competitor attack) |
| Decision process MISSING | Silence | Proposed mutual action plan with dates |
