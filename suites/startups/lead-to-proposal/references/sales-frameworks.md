# Sales Frameworks for Discovery and Proposals

## SPIN Selling (Rackham, 1988)

Based on Huthwaite's study of 35,000 sales calls. In larger B2B sales, the
seller's success correlates with the questions asked, not the pitch.

| Layer | What it captures | What goes in the proposal |
|---|---|---|
| Situation | Facts about their operation (fleet size, team, tools) | Context section, briefly |
| Problem | Difficulties they admit | "Lo que nos contaron", in their words |
| Implication | Consequences and cost of the problem | The cost of inaction, in their numbers |
| Need-payoff | Value they say a solution would have | Objectives section |

Rackham's key finding: implied needs (problems) do not predict success in
large sales; explicit needs (the buyer states a want) do. A proposal sent
on implied needs alone is premature.

## MEDDICC (MEDDIC from PTC, 1996; extended by Whyte, 2020)

| Letter | Element | Verified when |
|---|---|---|
| M | Metrics | The buyer stated a quantified outcome they want |
| E | Economic buyer | The person who signs is named and has been engaged |
| D | Decision criteria | The buyer said how they will compare options |
| D | Decision process | Steps, people and dates to signature are known |
| I | Identify pain | A pain with business consequences was stated |
| C | Champion | An insider with influence is actively selling for you |
| C | Competition | Alternatives (including do nothing) are known |

Status per element: VERIFIED (quote), INFERRED (reason), MISSING.

## Challenger Sale (Dixon and Adamson, 2011)

From a CEB study of 6,000+ reps: the "Challenger" profile outperforms in
complex sales by teaching, tailoring and taking control.
- **Teach:** bring an insight about the customer's business they did not
  know, which leads back to your unique strength.
- **Tailor:** the message for each stakeholder's priorities (operations
  cares about hours, finance about cost, owner about growth and risk).
- **Take control:** propose the next steps and dates; do not wait.

In the proposal: one teaching insight backed by data, tailored value
propositions per stakeholder, and a mutual action plan.

## Mutual action plan

A dated list of steps from today to go-live, owned by both sides (technical
review, security questionnaire, legal, signature, kickoff). It turns
"we'll get back to you" into commitments and exposes a missing decision
process early.

## Value proposition formula

Their pain (quote) + implication in their numbers + our capability + proof
from the closest case study + what changes in 90 days.
B2B value elements beyond price (Almquist, Cleghorn and Sherer, HBR 2018,
"The B2B Elements of Value"): reduced effort, risk reduction, time savings,
and the buyer's personal value (reputation, less anxiety) often decide.
