# LATAM Context: From Lead to Signed Proposal

See shared/latam-benchmarks.md for canonical LATAM benchmark data.
Label US figures as `(US-sourced benchmark, [Source])` and add the LATAM
figure when one exists.

## Decision makers

- **Family groups and SMBs:** the owner or Gerente General signs almost
  everything above a small threshold, even when a manager runs the
  evaluation. Treat "lo tengo que consultar" as Economic buyer MISSING.
- **Corporates and subsidiaries of multinationals:** procurement, legal and
  sometimes regional headquarters approval add weeks. Ask about vendor
  registration (alta de proveedor), required tax documents and payment terms
  in discovery.
- Titles: Gerente General, Director Comercial, Gerente de Operaciones,
  Jefe de Compras. Use the title the prospect uses.

## Channel

- WhatsApp is often the primary follow-up channel with owners and managers.
  When the contact shared a number or moved the conversation there, the
  sequence includes short WhatsApp-ready texts. Still drafts.
- Email remains the channel for the formal proposal and anything procurement
  or legal will read.

## Proposal conventions

- State currency explicitly (USD or local) and whether taxes (IVA) are
  included. Many buyers need the price with and without IVA.
- Payment terms of 30-60 days are common for corporates; SMBs may prefer
  monthly payments or local payment methods. Do not change the startup's
  terms; flag the likely friction.
- A free or paid pilot with clear success criteria is a common bridge to a
  first contract with a corporate; propose it only if the brain's offering
  includes pilots.

## Sales cycle

- Relationship-first: an in-person or video meeting with the decision maker
  often moves a deal more than another email. The Day 14 touch should offer
  that meeting.
- Expect longer cycles around year-end holidays, Carnaval and Semana Santa;
  adjust follow-up dates instead of firing into silence.
