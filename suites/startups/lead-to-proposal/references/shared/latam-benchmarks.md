## How to Annotate US Benchmarks in Skill Output

Every skill that presents quantitative benchmarks must follow this protocol:

1. Show the US benchmark first, labeled `(US-sourced benchmark)`
2. Immediately follow with the LATAM-adjusted figure, labeled `(LATAM, LAVCA 2025)` or `(LATAM, Cuantico 2026)`
3. Note the direction: LATAM advantage, LATAM discount, or same as US

**Example format:**
> CAC payback: 12–18 months is "good" *(US-sourced benchmark, Bessemer Cloud Index)*
> CAC payback in LATAM: 6–12 months typical; 60% of Brazilian SaaS recover CAC in <6 months *(LATAM, Cuantico 2026)* — LATAM structural advantage

## LP Base Characteristics (LAVCA 2025)

| LP Type | LATAM % | US % | Implication |
|---------|---------|------|-------------|
| Family offices | ~35% | ~15% | Dominant LATAM LP; shorter time horizons, relationship-based |
| Corporates / CVCs | ~25% | ~10% | Strategic LPs; can create conflicts |
| Government/DFIs | ~20% | ~5% | IDB Invest, CAF major LATAM LP; patient capital |
| Pension/Endowments | ~15% | ~55% | Underpenetrated; emerging institutional LP base |
| Fund of Funds | ~5% | ~15% | Growing; ACON, Kauffman secondary actors |

**Implication for LATAM GPs:** LP base is less institutional than US; governance standards, reporting cadence, and LP relations strategy must be calibrated to family office norms, not endowment norms.

## LATAM-Specific Risk Factors for Investors

| Risk | Description | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|------------|
| FX volatility | Revenue in local currency; VC returns in USD | USD-denominated contracts; cross-border revenue strategy |
| Regulatory fragmentation | No unified LATAM regulatory framework | Country-specific compliance; Brazil/Mexico first |
| Capital markets depth | Limited Series B+ follow-on capital locally | International co-investors at Series A required |
| Talent density | Engineering talent concentrated in BR/MX/COL | Remote-first team structures |
| Payment infrastructure | LATAM payment fragmentation | PIX (Brazil), SPEI (Mexico) reducing friction; 20 other markets remain fragmented |
| Pre-seed contraction | 152 deals in 2025 vs 660 in 2022 | Earlier-stage companies must hit seed metrics faster; accelerator funding critical |
