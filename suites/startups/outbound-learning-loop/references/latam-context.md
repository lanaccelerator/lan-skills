## 1. Reply-rate reference points (all directional)

No public dataset measures LATAM B2B outbound reply rates by channel. Use these to set a
first target, then replace them with your own tracked numbers after the first 100 delivered
messages.

| Motion | Reply | Positive | Label |
|---|---|---|---|
| Warm automated outreach (people who know the sender) | ~30% | ~12% | WARM, Nájera's own outreach. Never a cold target |
| Warm intro from a mutual contact | 60-80% | (track yours) | WARM, LAN practitioner range |
| Cold email after an accepted LinkedIn connection | 10-20% | (track yours) | COLD, LAN practitioner range |
| Cold email with no prior contact, LATAM B2B | 2-8% | (track yours) | COLD, LAN practitioner range |

**Account view:** with 3-5 recipients per account, the share of accounts with at least one
reply is the number that matters for pipeline, and it is always higher than the
per-recipient reply rate. Track both.

## 2. Roles and local titles

| Role | Common titles (Spanish) | Leads with |
|---|---|---|
| Decider | Gerente General, Director General, Gerente de Administración y Finanzas (when the purchase is financial) | Outcome and the number |
| Champion | Gerente o Jefe del área con el dolor (Operaciones, Cobranzas, Comercial, Logística) | Their team's pain, material to forward |
| User | Analista, Coordinador, Supervisor, Encargado | The daily workflow |
| Finance / procurement | Gerente Administrativo, Jefe de Compras, Contador General | Billing unit, terms, vendor documents |
| IT | Jefe de Sistemas, Gerente de TI | Integration effort and data security |

Use the title exactly as it appears on the person's public profile.

## 3. Language and register

- Write in the prospect's local register: voseo in Paraguay, Argentina and Uruguay; tú or
  usted in Chile, Mexico, Colombia and Peru (usted for senior roles on a first touch).
  Portuguese for Brazil, never Spanish. The templates in this skill use voseo.
- Short beats formal: three to five lines, one question.
- Spanish subject lines in lowercase with the company name read as written by a person.

## 4. Channels and timing

- Email for the cold first touch; LinkedIn for users and champions who are active there;
  WhatsApp only with opt-in (a gremio event, a referral that handed over the number, or the
  person wrote first).
- Send Tuesday to Thursday, morning local time, as a starting hypothesis; let the learning
  loop confirm or change it.
- Avoid mid-December to mid-January, Semana Santa and Carnaval weeks (Brazil); expect slower
  replies around fiscal year-end (December in most of LATAM).
- Gremios and associations: a contact found in a member directory responds better when the
  first line names the association event or committee where you will be (only if true).

## 5. Data protection by country

Brazil (LGPD) is in force; Chile (Ley 21.719) enters into force on 1 Dec 2026; Paraguay
(Ley 7593) was promulgated on 27 Nov 2025 with a 2-year adjustment period. Treat outbound
lists as personal data in all three: public business sources only, source recorded, opt-out
honored everywhere.
