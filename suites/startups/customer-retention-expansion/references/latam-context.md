# LATAM Context: Retention and Expansion

See shared/latam-benchmarks.md for canonical LATAM benchmark data. No public LATAM dataset
on churn, NRR or expansion exists at the quality of US benchmarks. Label any LATAM range
`(LATAM, directional)` and US figures `(US-sourced benchmark, [Source])`.

## Who really renews

- **Family groups and SMBs:** the owner or Gerente General decides the renewal, even when a
  manager runs the day-to-day. If the decider has not seen results in 90 days, the renewal is
  at risk no matter how happy the user is.
- **Corporates and subsidiaries of multinationals:** procurement, legal and sometimes regional
  headquarters approve renewals. Budgets close in the last quarter for the next year: an
  upsell not in the budget by October or November may wait a full year. Ask the champion
  when budgets are set.
- **Champion turnover:** managers change jobs often, and the new person may bring their own
  vendor. Keep at least three people engaged per account and re-onboard the newcomer within
  two weeks.

## Channel

- **WhatsApp is the daily channel** with owners and managers. Short check-ins, result
  snapshots and referral asks work there. Still drafts, and the formal renewal proposal goes
  by email so procurement and legal can read it.
- **In-person or video** for business reviews with the decider. Relationship-first accounts
  renew in a meeting, not by email.

## Invoicing and collections

- **Vendor portals:** large buyers register suppliers before the first invoice and require
  current documents (tax ID such as RUC, RUT, CUIT, RFC or CNPJ; good-standing and tax
  certificates; insurance; bank certificate). Expired documents block payment. Review the
  folder every quarter.
- **Purchase orders:** many corporates pay only against a valid purchase order. An expired PO
  at renewal is a common cause of 30-60 day delays.
- **Payment terms:** 30-60 days is common for corporates; SMBs pay faster but are more
  sensitive to FX and inflation. Quote with and without IVA.
- **Currency:** USD list price, local currency invoicing at the day's rate when the customer
  requires it, with the rate source in the contract. Renewal is the moment to fix FX clauses.

## Referrals

- **Gremios and associations** gather the ICP in one place. Ask the happy customer for an
  intro inside their gremio. Examples, Chile: SOFOFA, CChC, SNA, ABIF, FinteChile, AACH,
  Clínicas de Chile, CCS, ASECH, CONUPIA. Paraguay: UIP, ARP, CAPECO, CAPACO, ASOBAN, Cámara
  Paraguaya de Fintech, CISOFT, AMCHAM.
- **WhatsApp communities** of the ICP (industry groups, association chats): a forwardable
  two-line text from a customer travels further than a cold email.
- **Personal trust:** in many LATAM markets a referral from a peer owner opens a meeting that
  outbound cannot. Ask several times a year, at the thank-you moment.

## Calendar

- Expect slow responses in the second half of December, January (summer in the Southern
  Cone), Carnaval and Semana Santa. Move renewal milestones earlier instead of firing
  reminders into silence.
- Year-end budget cycles: run the T-90 business review before mid-November for January
  renewals.

## Case studies

- Many LATAM companies accept being cited anonymously ("una cadena de supermercados con 24
  sucursales") long before they accept their logo in public. Ask for the anonymized permission
  first, the logo later.
- Get permission in writing (email or WhatsApp is fine) and file it with the results page.
