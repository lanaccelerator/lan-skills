# Deck Review: ClimaAgro

**Date:** April 2026
**Stage:** Seed
**Investor Profile:** LATAM-focused sector VCs (agtech / climate)
**Geography:** LATAM raise (Colombia first, then Andean region)
**Reviewed by:** LAN Skills via Claude

## Per-Slide Scorecard

| Slide | Score (1–5) | Key Issue | Recommendation |
|-------|-------------|-----------|----------------|
| Company Purpose | 4/5 | Clear but passive voice weakens conviction | Rewrite in active declarative form: "We give smallholder farmers real-time crop alerts so they stop losing 30% of yield to preventable pest damage." Cut anything after the first sentence. |
| Problem | 3/5 | Quantified ($2.1B annual crop loss in Colombia) but no emotional anchor | Add the "day in the life" failure moment, a farmer who received a pest alert 4 days too late and lost the harvest. Investors need to feel the pain before they believe the solution. |
| Solution | 3/5 | Product features described, differentiation is not | Lead with what ONLY ClimaAgro can do: sub-24-hour alert latency from satellite + soil sensor fusion. That is the moat. Lead with it, not with the feature list. |
| Why Now | 1/5 | [FATAL] Missing entirely, no timing thesis presented | [MISSING SLIDE] This is the #1 investor question in any pitch. What changed between 2022 and 2025 that makes this possible now? Satellite imagery resolution improved 4×, IoT sensor costs fell 60%, and Colombia's MinAgricultura launched a digital farming subsidy in 2024. Add a slide with this thesis explicitly. |
| Market | 4/5 | TAM credible ($8.4B LATAM agtech) but no SAM/SOM breakdown | Show Year 1 SAM: 180k smallholder farms in Colombia's coffee belt × $240 annual subscription = $43M SAM. Then Year 3 SOM: 3% capture = $1.3M ARR. Investors do not fund TAM, they fund SAM/SOM progression. |
| Competition | 2/5 | 2×2 matrix uses wrong axes (price vs features, generic) | Axes should reflect ClimaAgro's actual differentiation: X-axis = alert latency (slow → real-time), Y-axis = LATAM smallholder focus (generic → specialized). Place Bayer FieldView, Syngenta Digital Farming, and local alternatives accurately, do not make the matrix tell a false story. |
| Product | 4/5 | Screenshots of the mobile app are effective | Add one "before/after" outcome comparison: "Farm A with ClimaAgro: 12% yield increase, 18% reduction in pesticide spend over 6 months." Outcome data closes the credibility gap between screenshots and real impact. |
| Business Model | 3/5 | Annual subscription pricing stated ($240/farm/year) but model rationale missing | Justify why annual subscription vs per-alert pricing. Investors will ask: "Why do farmers pay before they receive value?" Answer: "Local distribution partners pre-sell annual licenses as part of financing packages to farmer cooperatives, a proven go-to-market with zero churn risk." |
| Team | 2/5 | Bios list roles and education, no founder-market fit story | LATAM investor question is "Why are YOU the ones who will win this market?" Each bio needs the one credential that proves founder-market fit. CEO: "Grew up on a smallholder coffee farm in Caldas, this problem cost my family $40k." CTO: "Led precision agriculture R&D at Bayer LATAM for 6 years." That story is what bios need to tell. |
| Financials | 1/5 | [FATAL] Missing completely from the deck | [MISSING SLIDE] Missing financials in a Seed deck is a rejection signal, not a "fix for next version" issue. Investors will not request a follow-up without seeing the financial model. Add a 24-month P&L projection with three visible assumptions: monthly farm acquisition rate, average revenue per farm, and gross margin %. Current benchmark for LATAM agtech Seed: $200k–$500k ARR at raise *(LATAM, LAVCA 2025)*. |

**Total Score: 28/50**: Two fatal flaws (Why Now missing, Financials missing) make
this deck not ready to send.

---

## Deck-Level Verdict

**CONDITIONAL PASS**: Two slides scored 1/5 (fatal flaws). Investment conversations
cannot proceed with a deck missing Why Now and Financials. Fix these two slides before
sending to any investor. The underlying business has merit (strong Problem framing,
credible TAM, real product), the deck is not telling the right story yet.

---

## Top 3 Priority Fixes

1. **Add Why Now slide (Slide 4):** Document the three enabling shifts: (a) satellite
   imagery resolution improved 4× since 2021 enabling crop-level anomaly detection at
   cost, (b) IoT sensor costs fell from $180 to $42 per unit (2020–2024), (c) Colombia
   MinAgricultura launched digital farming subsidies covering 40% of ClimaAgro's target
   farms. Without this slide, investors assume the timing is arbitrary.

2. **Build and insert Financials slide (Slide 10):** 24-month projection required.
   Include: monthly farm acquisition (target: 150 farms/month by Month 12), ARPU ($20/farm/month),
   gross margin (82%), and burn rate. Financials are among the most-viewed slides
   in DocSend's data; its absence tells investors you have not modeled the business.

3. **Rewrite Team slide (Slide 9):** Replace bios with founder-market fit narratives.
   Each person needs one sentence of credential and one sentence of why this market
   specifically. LATAM investors weight local network and sector experience, logos from
   US companies do not signal LATAM operator credibility *(LATAM)*.

---

## LATAM Context

- **(LATAM)** No comparable raises cited. LATAM investors expect localized comparables -
  add Agrofy ($23M Series A, Argentina), Kilimo (Kenya, but structurally analogous),
  or CropIn (India) to show the global agtech Seed/Series A trajectory. US comps
  (Farmers Business Network, etc.) are less relevant for LATAM VCs.

- **(LATAM)** No FX risk section. Colombia COP has experienced 30%+ devaluation in
  recent years. Investors will ask: "What happens to your unit economics if COP
  weakens 20% against USD?" Answer it proactively with a sensitivity table or slide.

- **(LATAM)** Team section has no LATAM network signal. Who do they know in Colombian
  farmer cooperatives (Fedecafé, SAC, ASOHOFRUCOL)? Distribution in Colombian agtech
  is relationship-based, the deck gives no evidence this team can execute distribution.

---

## Next Steps

1. Add Why Now slide before sending to any investor, this is the #1 fix. Write it
   as three enabling events with specific dates and data points. Investors cannot
   approve a meeting without understanding the timing thesis.

2. Build the financial model and extract Slide 10 from it. The model does not need
   to be complex, 24 months, three assumptions, and a clear path to $1M ARR is
   sufficient for a Seed raise in LATAM.

3. Rewrite Team slide using the founder-market fit narrative format: "[Name] has
   [specific credential] which proves [specific capability required to win this market]."
   Two sentences per person. No logos without context.

4. Fix the Competition 2×2 axes to reflect ClimaAgro's actual differentiation vectors
   (alert latency + LATAM specialization), not generic price vs features. Investors will
   test this matrix against their knowledge of Bayer FieldView and Syngenta, if the
   axes don't hold up, the claim of differentiation collapses.

5. Add one customer outcome data point to the Product slide, a real farmer result
   (yield improvement %, pesticide spend reduction, income increase) from a current
   pilot. One real data point beats 10 feature screenshots.
