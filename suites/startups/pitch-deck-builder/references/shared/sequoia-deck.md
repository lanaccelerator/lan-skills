## Key Principles

Sequoia's structure reflects how partners actually make investment decisions: they first need to understand the mission, then the pain, then why this team, then why now, then scale. Each slide earns the next.

1. **Company Purpose** — Define the company in a single declarative sentence. Not a tagline. Not a mission statement. What does it do?
2. **Problem** — Describe the pain being solved. Articulate who has it, how acute it is, and why existing solutions fail. Best delivered as a story.
3. **Solution** — The product/service. Show it, don't describe it. Screenshots, demo video, or workflow. Show how it eliminates the problem from Slide 2.
4. **Why Now** — What has changed in the world in the last 2–3 years that makes this moment the right moment? Technology shift, regulatory change, behavioral shift, infrastructure availability.
5. **Market Size** — TAM/SAM/SOM. Must use bottom-up methodology. Top-down TAM claims without SAM/SOM calculations fail due diligence.
6. **Competition** — Be honest. All credible competitors. A 2×2 matrix with clear differentiation. Claiming "no competitors" is disqualifying.
7. **Product** — Deep dive into the product's current state. Architecture, roadmap, key features, technical differentiation. Show progress.
8. **Business Model** — How does the company make money? Pricing model, unit economics overview, revenue streams.
9. **Team** — Why is this team uniquely qualified? Prior domain experience, relevant exits, technical expertise. "We're passionate" is not a qualification.
10. **Financials** — 3-year projections with assumptions visible. Key metrics: MRR/ARR, burn rate, runway, path to profitability.

## Application Criteria

### Slide-by-Slide Execution Standards

| Slide | What Must Be Present | Common Failure Mode |
|-------|---------------------|---------------------|
| Company Purpose | One sentence. Verb + object + beneficiary. | Jargon-laden mission statement that says nothing |
| Problem | Size of pain (quantified), who has it, why current solutions fail | "People waste time on X" — not quantified, not specific |
| Solution | Product demo or screenshot, not description of features | Bullet list of features with no visual |
| Why Now | Specific trigger: technology, regulation, behavior, infrastructure | "The market is ready" — not an explanation |
| Market Size | Bottom-up: ICP count × contract value | Top-down: "Gartner says $50B" — not credible |
| Competition | 2×2 matrix with defensible axes, honest competitor placement | Magic quadrant with 0 credible competitors |
| Product | Screenshots, architecture diagram, roadmap with milestones | Powerpoint mockup that doesn't match live product |
| Business Model | Unit economics: CAC, LTV, gross margin, payback | "We'll charge a SaaS fee" with no numbers |
| Team | Domain-specific credentials, relevant prior experience | Headshots with titles but no achievements |
| Financials | Model assumptions visible, growth trajectory grounded in drivers | Hockey stick with no explanation of inflection |

### Must-Have Data Per Slide

- **Slide 5 (Market):** ICP count × average contract value = SAM. SAM × realistic capture rate = SOM.
- **Slide 8 (Business Model):** Gross margin %, CAC, LTV/CAC ratio, payback period.
- **Slide 10 (Financials):** Monthly burn, runway (months), current ARR/MRR, 3-year ARR projection.
