# Pitch Deck Builder — Template

**ALWAYS use this exact template structure.** Every slide must have: title,
talking points (filled with real data or [DATA NEEDED] flags), data needed,
and common errors. Do not abbreviate or skip sections.

---

## Example: Alegra — B2B SaaS, SME Accounting, Seed Stage, Raising $2M

**Company:** Alegra
**Stage:** Seed
**Raising:** $2M
**Framework:** Sequoia 10-slide (Seed standard)

---

### Slide 1 — Company Purpose

**Talking Points:**
- Alegra helps 500k+ Latin American SMEs manage their accounting in <30 min/month
- Replaces Excel, accountant visits, and paper receipts with a single mobile-first platform
- Founded 2013 in Colombia; operating in 8 LATAM countries

**Data Needed:**
- [available] Total registered users: 500k+ (as of Q4 2025)
- [available] Countries of operation: Colombia, Mexico, Argentina, Chile, Peru, Guatemala, Ecuador, Uruguay
- [available] Core value proposition: <30 min/month accounting

**Common Errors to Avoid:**
- Writing a tagline instead of a declarative company-purpose sentence
- Saying "we're the Intuit of LATAM" without explaining what that actually means
- Listing features instead of customer outcomes

---

### Slide 2 — Problem

**Talking Points:**
- SME owners in LATAM spend 8+ hours/month on manual accounting and invoicing
- 70% of LATAM SMEs still use Excel or paper-based systems; most cannot afford a
  dedicated accountant ($300–$600/month in Mexico and Colombia)
- Existing accounting software (SAP, QuickBooks) is designed for large enterprises —
  too complex and too expensive for a 5-person business
- Non-compliance with local tax authorities (DIAN, SAT) costs SMEs 15–30% of net
  income in fines annually

**Data Needed:**
- [available] % of LATAM SMEs using Excel/paper: 70% (SME Finance Forum 2024)
- [available] Monthly accountant cost in Mexico/Colombia: $300–$600/mo
- [available] SAT/DIAN non-compliance fine rate: 15–30% of net income

**Common Errors to Avoid:**
- Stating the problem without quantifying it ("people waste time on accounting")
- No customer quote illustrating the pain
- Failing to explain why existing solutions (QuickBooks, SAP) don't work for this segment

---

### Slide 3 — Solution

**Talking Points:**
- Alegra is a cloud-based accounting + invoicing platform built for SMEs with
  zero accounting background — set up in 10 minutes, no accountant needed
- Key features: electronic invoicing (CFDI/DIAN compliant), bank reconciliation,
  expense tracking, payroll basics, real-time P&L
- Mobile-first: 65% of Alegra's active sessions are on mobile (founders manage
  business from their phone)

**Data Needed:**
- [available] Setup time: 10 minutes to first invoice
- [available] Mobile session share: 65%
- [DATA NEEDED: demo screenshot or short screen recording embedded in slide]

**Common Errors to Avoid:**
- Feature bullet list with no product visual — every Solution slide needs a screenshot
- Describing what the product will do vs what it does today
- Overloading one slide with the full product roadmap

---

### Slide 4 — Why Now

**Talking Points:**
- Electronic invoicing mandates: Mexico (CFDI 4.0, 2022), Colombia (e-factura
  requirement, 2023) — 8M+ SMEs now legally required to issue digital invoices
- Mobile internet penetration in LATAM reached 78% in 2024 (vs 45% in 2018) —
  a mobile-first accounting app was impossible to distribute at scale 5 years ago
- PIX (Brazil) and SPEI (Mexico) real-time payment rails created infrastructure
  for bank reconciliation that didn't exist pre-2020

**Data Needed:**
- [available] CFDI 4.0 mandate date: Jan 2022
- [available] LATAM mobile internet penetration: 78% in 2024 (GSMA Mobile Economy 2024)
- [DATA NEEDED: number of SMEs newly required to use electronic invoicing in target markets]

**Common Errors to Avoid:**
- "The market is ready" is not a Why Now explanation — name the specific trigger
- Conflating market growth with enabling conditions — growth is not the same as a
  new infrastructure or regulatory forcing function
- Why Now must answer: what changed in the last 2–3 years that makes this solvable now

---

### Slide 5 — Market Size

**Talking Points:**
- **TAM:** 15M+ formal SMEs in Mexico and Colombia combined (INEGI 2024, DANE 2023)
- **SAM:** 8M SMEs with >5 employees, digital banking access, and $100+/month
  budget for software (based on Alegra's ICP criteria)
- **SOM:** 500k SMEs reachable via current channels in 36 months
- **Bottom-up calculation:** 8M SAM × $240 ACV (Alegra's current Seed plan) = **$1.9B SAM**

**Data Needed:**
- [available] Mexico SME count: 4.9M formal SMEs (INEGI 2024)
- [available] Colombia SME count: 1.6M formal SMEs (DANE 2023)
- [available] Current ACV: $240/year (Seed plan, Mexico and Colombia pricing)
- [DATA NEEDED: % of target SMEs with digital banking access — use CNBV or Banca de
  las Oportunidades data]

**Common Errors to Avoid:**
- Using a top-down TAM ("global accounting software = $15B — we want 1%") — no
  credible investor accepts percentage-of-giant-market sizing
- Not showing SAM calculation — TAM alone without SAM/SOM is incomplete
- Claiming LATAM TAM without country-level breakdown

> **LATAM Context:** Seed investors in LATAM expect SAM calculation with country-level
> data, not LATAM-wide estimates. Brazil and Mexico represent 78.5% of LATAM VC — a
> Mexico + Colombia TAM is fundable without requiring pan-LATAM coverage claims.
> *(LAVCA 2025)*

### Slide 6 — Competition

**Talking Points:**
- **Direct competitors:** Contpaqi (Mexico, legacy desktop), Siigo (Colombia, legacy),
  QuickBooks Mexico (US-designed, expensive, complex)
- **Indirect competitors:** Excel, WhatsApp-based accountants, paper invoicing
- **Alegra's differentiation:** Mobile-first, <10 min onboarding, LATAM tax compliance
  built-in (CFDI + DIAN), pricing designed for SME budgets ($20/month vs QuickBooks
  $80+/month)
- **Moat:** Local tax compliance integrations are hard to replicate quickly — Alegra
  has 3 years of DIAN and SAT integration work as a barrier

**Data Needed:**
- [available] QuickBooks Mexico pricing: $80+/month starter plan
- [available] Alegra pricing: from $20/month
- [DATA NEEDED: 2×2 matrix axes — suggest "local compliance depth" vs "SME ease of use"]

**Common Errors to Avoid:**
- Claiming "no competitors" — this is disqualifying in any investor meeting
- 2×2 matrix where you win on both axes with no credible competitor in any quadrant
- Failing to address why incumbents (Contpaqi, SAP) won't simply add mobile features

### Slide 7 — Product

**Talking Points:**
- **Current state:** Live in 8 countries; 500k registered users; 95k+ paying customers
- **Core product modules:** Electronic invoicing, expense tracking, bank reconciliation,
  basic payroll, real-time P&L
- **Roadmap (next 12 months):** Embedded lending (working capital for SMEs based on
  Alegra invoicing history), open banking integrations, AI-powered expense categorization
- **Technical differentiator:** Tax authority API integrations (SAT, DIAN, SII, AFIP)
  maintained and updated with each regulatory change

**Data Needed:**
- [available] Paying customers: 95k+
- [available] Tax authority integrations: SAT (Mexico), DIAN (Colombia), SII (Chile),
  AFIP (Argentina), 4 others
- [DATA NEEDED: screenshot of main dashboard showing live product, not mockup]

**Common Errors to Avoid:**
- PowerPoint mockups that don't match the live product — investors check your app
- Roadmap that lists 30 features with no prioritization or timeline
- Listing integrations without explaining which integrations actually drive retention

### Slide 8 — Business Model

**Talking Points:**
- **Pricing model:** Monthly SaaS subscription — $20/month (basic) to $60/month (pro)
- **Current MRR:** $190k (as of Q4 2025)
- **Unit economics:**
  - CAC: $85 (blended, primarily self-serve digital)
  - LTV: $720 (avg subscription duration 36 months × $20 ACV)
  - LTV/CAC: 8.5× — top-decile for LATAM SaaS *(Cuantico 2026)*
  - Payback period: 5.4 months
- **Gross margin:** 72% (SaaS standard; LATAM infrastructure costs slightly higher)

**Data Needed:**
- [available] Current MRR: $190k
- [available] CAC: $85 (blended)
- [available] LTV/CAC: 8.5×
- [DATA NEEDED: monthly churn rate — separate logo churn from revenue churn]

**Common Errors to Avoid:**
- "We'll charge a SaaS fee" with no pricing numbers
- Presenting CAC without the acquisition channel breakdown (self-serve vs paid
  ads vs partnerships have very different CAC profiles)
- Presenting LTV without stating the churn assumption behind it

> **LATAM Context:** LTV/CAC of 8.5× puts Alegra in the top quartile of LATAM SaaS
> at Seed. Seed benchmark in LATAM: ≥3:1 expected, top-quartile ≥5:1 *(Cuantico 2026)*.
> Series A benchmark (US-sourced): ≥3:1 *(Bessemer Cloud Index — US-sourced benchmark)*.

  (3) reach $500k MRR milestone in 18 months
- **3-year projection:** $2.3M ARR → $8M ARR → $22M ARR (based on: current

**Data Needed:**
- [available] Current ARR: $2.3M
- [available] Burn: $85k/month
- [available] Runway: 18 months
- [DATA NEEDED: projection model with assumptions visible — show growth driver breakdown

## LATAM Context Box

> **Seed investors in LATAM expect at least 1k active paying customers or $100k ARR.**
> US-style "growth at all costs" narratives underperform vs "unit-economics-first"
> framing with LATAM LPs and local family offices — 35% of LATAM VC LP capital comes
> from family offices with shorter time horizons than US endowments.
> *(LAVCA 2025)*
>
> **Revenue multiple reality check:** At equivalent ARR and growth rates, LATAM SaaS
> companies receive valuations 30–50% below US peers. A $2M ARR company growing 3×
> YoY that commands a $15M pre-money in Silicon Valley will typically get $8–10M pre-money
> in LATAM. Calibrate your ask to LATAM comps, not US TechCrunch headlines.
> *(Cuantico 2026)*
