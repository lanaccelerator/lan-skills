# B2B Sales Playbook: LogiTrack

**Date:** November 2026
**Stage:** 5 customers, founder-led, building a repeatable process
**ACV:** USD 8,400/year
**Geography:** Chile (Santiago, Concepción, Antofagasta), Paraguay expansion (Asunción, Ciudad del Este)
**Biggest Sales Challenge:** Generating leads (top of funnel)

## ICP: Ideal Customer Profile

| Field | Definition |
|-------|-----------|
| **Industry** | Industrial manufacturing with own distribution, wholesale distribution, construction materials |
| **Company Size** | 20-200 vehicle fleet; revenue USD 3M-60M |
| **Geography** | Chile: Santiago, Concepción, Antofagasta. Paraguay: Asunción, Central, Alto Paraná |
| **Trigger Events** | GPS contract expiry, new branch or route, fuel cost spike, driver accident or claim, insurance renewal |
| **Buyer Title** | Gerente de Operaciones / Gerente General (companies under 50 employees) |
| **User Title** | Jefe de Flota / Supervisor de Despacho |

**ICP Tightness Check:** "Gerente de Operaciones of a Chilean industrial SME with 20-200
vehicles, member of SOFOFA or CChC" is a person you can find on LinkedIn and in a member
directory. "Logistics SME" is not.

## Buying-Group Map (every target account)

About 13 people weigh in on the average B2B purchase and 86% of purchases stall (Forrester).
One contact per account is one point of failure.

| Role | Title at target | What they care about | Asset they need |
|------|----------------|----------------------|-----------------|
| **Decider** | Gerente General | Margin, risk, fit with the year's plan | One-page business case with their fuel and overtime numbers |
| **Champion** | Gerente de Operaciones | Fewer fires, looking good with the GM | Material they can forward without editing |
| **User** | Jefe de Flota | Will this make the morning dispatch easier or harder | 10-minute demo on their own routes |
| **Finance / procurement** | Gerente de Administración y Finanzas / Jefe de Compras | Price, terms, vendor risk | Clear billing unit (per vehicle), no lock-in, vendor documents ready |
| **IT** (only if ERP integration) | Jefe de Sistemas | Data, integration effort | One-page integration note and security answers |

**Account owner:** the founder (Ana) coordinates all threads per account. No two roles get a
message the same day.

**Super whos (find three, across different industries or associations):**

| Super who (hypothetical) | Access to | Ask |
|---|---|---|
| Gerente de un comité de transporte en una asociación industrial | ~400 industrial companies with fleets | Intro to 5 Gerentes de Operaciones + 10-minute slot at the committee |

"Hola [nombre], estoy ayudando a pymes industriales con flota propia a bajar el gasto de

cold.)

**Gremios that gather the ICP:**

**WhatsApp communities:** 2 groups of fleet managers (entry through 2 current customers who are
members). Contribute first: answer one fuel-cost question per week. Never broadcast a pitch.

**Referral calendar (5 current customers):** ask at each thank-you moment: after the first
monthly savings report (month 1), at renewal (month 12) and when a customer compliments the
product in a meeting. Target: 1 intro per customer per quarter. Logged in the CRM the same day.

## Account Sequence (multi-threaded, Day 1-21)

Example account: hypothetical Distribuidora Andes, 60 vehicles, Santiago. Three roles
sequenced, coordinated by Ana.

| Day | Channel | Recipient role | Message type | Snippet | Expected response |
|-----|---------|----------------|--------------|---------|-------------------|
| 1 | Warm intro (super who) | Champion | Intro by the association contact | "Te presento a Ana, de LogiTrack; trabaja con varias distribuidoras del comité." | 50-70% reply (warm, directional) |
| 1 | LinkedIn | User | Connection request | "Hola [nombre], vi que coordinás el despacho de [empresa]. Trabajamos con flotas industriales de Santiago." | 35-45% accept |
| 3 | Email | Decider | Short email, outcome first | Asunto: "[empresa]: combustible por entrega". "Una distribuidora de 45 camiones bajó 18% el combustible por entrega en 90 días. ¿Te sirve ver cómo lo midieron?" | 3-8% reply (cold, LATAM directional); every reply logged |
| 4 | WhatsApp (after intro) | Champion | First message | "Hola [nombre], gracias por aceptar la intro de [super who]. ¿Tenés 15 minutos el jueves para contarme cómo arman hoy las rutas?" | 40-60% reply |
| 6 | WhatsApp | Champion | Value-add | One-page case: "Distribuidora Sur (ejemplo)". No ask. | 70% open |
| 8 | LinkedIn | User | Message after connect | "¿Cuánto te lleva armar el despacho de la mañana? Te paso cómo lo resolvió otro jefe de flota." | 20-30% reply |
| 10 | Email | Finance | Billing logic | "Cobramos por vehículo, sin permanencia mínima. Te dejo el detalle por si [champion] lo trae a la mesa." | Open is the goal; reply 5-10% |
| 14 | Email | Decider | Second touch, mentions the champion | "Estoy hablando con [champion] sobre las rutas de la zona sur. Si te sirve, te mando una página con el ahorro estimado." | 5-10% reply |
| 17 | WhatsApp | Champion | Direct ask | "¿Lo vemos 20 minutos con [decider] el martes o el miércoles?" | 30% of engaged |
| 21 | WhatsApp | Champion | Breakup | "Entiendo si no es prioridad ahora. ¿Te escribo en 3 meses, antes de la renovación del GPS?" | 20% revive later |

**Sequence rules:**
- Warm first; cold email runs in parallel as a learning channel, not as a last resort.
- WhatsApp only after an intro, a connection or an opt-in. No mass sends.
- Every message is a DRAFT. Ana reviews the first 20 before any batch goes out.
- Campaign execution and the weekly learning loop: `/outbound-learning-loop`.

## Objection Handling Matrix

| Objection | Root Cause | Challenger Response | Evidence to Use |
|-----------|-----------|--------------------|----|
| "Ya tenemos GPS" | Status quo bias: switching-cost fear, not satisfaction | "¿Qué resolverías de tu operación si arrancaras de cero?" Reframe around what the GPS does not solve (dispatch, overtime). | Brain case: "Pensamos lo mismo. A los 30 días recuperamos el costo de migrar." (Distribuidora Sur, ejemplo) |
| "No hay presupuesto" | Not allocated, not "no money": budget goes to perceived priorities | "¿Cuánto les cuestan por mes las horas extra del despacho manual?" Quantify the cost of today before price. | ROI worksheet with their numbers: 18% fuel per delivery at 60 vehicles vs USD 700/month |
| "Mandame información" | Avoiding a decision, or not the right contact | "Te mando lo más útil: ¿cuál es el dolor principal de la flota hoy?" Discovery before any PDF. | One targeted case from the same industry, never a generic brochure |
| "Lo tengo que ver con mi jefe" | Talking to the user, not the decider | "Perfecto, ¿quién sería? ¿Nos presentás 5 minutos con [Gerente General]?" Multi-thread, do not wait. | One-page business case the champion can forward |
| "Es caro" | Value gap, not price | "¿Caro comparado con qué alternativa?" Move to ROI. Never discount before the ROI conversation. | Brain case: 45 trucks, fuel per delivery from USD 3.10 to USD 2.54 (ejemplo) |

**Root Cause Principle (Challenger Sale):** the surface objection is rarely the real one.
Diagnose before responding.

## Meeting Script (45 minutes, founder talks max 33%)

| Phase | Minutes | Founder talk budget | Goal | Must-ask questions |
|-------|---------|---------------------|------|--------------------|
| **Short opening** | 3 | 2 min | Why we are here, agenda agreed | (none) |
| **Questions first** | 15 | 3 min | Their situation in their words | 1. ¿Qué herramientas usan hoy para esto? 3. ¿Están buscando activamente una solución así? 4. ¿Qué otras opciones evaluaron, incluido no hacer nada? |
| **Brief demo, only on their pain** | 10 | 6 min | Show only what solves what they just said | (none: no feature tour) |
| **More questions** | 12 | 3 min | Reactions, gaps, scope | 7. ¿Qué funcionalidad haría que esto sea obvio para ustedes? 6. ¿Qué requisitos técnicos, legales o de seguridad tiene que cumplir? 2. ¿Hay presupuesto para esto, y de cuánto? 5. ¿Cuál es su plazo? |
| **Close with a dated next step** | 5 | 1 min | Calendar invite, not "te escribo" | 8. ¿Quién decide y quién aprueba? ¿Cuál es el proceso? |
| **Total** | **45** | **15 min (33%)** | | |

**After every call:** score must-ask coverage x/8 in the CRM. Below 6/8 means a
qualification call before any proposal.

**Anti-patterns:**
- Feature tour instead of the 2 features that solve their stated pain.
- No next step: "te mando el resumen" is not a next step. A date is.
- Talking more than a third of the call. Reference only: Gong Labs found top calls around
  43% seller talk; the LAN rule is stricter because founders over-pitch.

## Full B2B Process

| Stage | Owner | Exit criterion | Documents |
|-------|-------|----------------|-----------|
| **Prospect** | Ana | Account with 3-5 named roles | Account map |
| **First touch** | Ana | Reply from any role | Drafts approved |
| **Discovery** | Ana | Must-ask 6/8 or more, pain quantified | Call notes in CRM |
| **Technical and integration meetings** | Ana + CTO (Diego) | Integration path agreed with Jefe de Sistemas | Integration note, security answers |
| **Proposal** | Ana | Business case sent before the decision meeting; proposal sent the same day after it | Business case, proposal (`/lead-to-proposal`) |
| **Legal and contract** | Ana + lawyer | Contract signed | Contract draft, data-processing annex |
| **Vendor-portal onboarding** | Admin (Lucía) | LogiTrack registered as supplier | Folder below |
| **Onboarding** | CS (Martín) | 3 routes live, first savings report sent | Onboarding plan |
| **Invoicing and collections** | Lucía | First invoice paid | Invoice, purchase order number |
| **Retention** | Martín | Monthly savings report read by the champion | Savings report |
| **Upsell** | Ana | New branch or module added | Business case update |
| **Referrals** | Ana | 1 intro per customer per quarter | Referral ask logged |

**Vendor-portal folder (ready before the first proposal):** RUT (Chile) or RUC (Paraguay);
certificate of good standing and tax compliance; insurance policies (civil liability); bylaws;
legal representative's ID and powers; bank account certificate; security documentation in
use. A missing document can delay the first payment by weeks.

## CRM Setup Recommendation

| Stage | Definition | Conversion Target | Days in Stage | Key Activity |
|-------|-----------|------------------|-------------|
| **Prospecting** | Account in sequence, 3-5 roles mapped, no reply | (none) | Next touch per role on schedule |
| **Qualified** | Reply + ICP fit + interest | 15-25% of accounts | Discovery booked |
| **Discovery Done** | Must-ask 6/8 or more | 60-75% of qualified | Pain and decider documented |
| **Technical Review** | Integration or security review open | (only if needed) | Integration note sent |
| **Proposal Sent** | Business case and proposal delivered | 30-50% of discovery done | Decision meeting on the calendar |
| **Legal / Vendor Portal** | Contract and supplier registration in progress | 80%+ of verbal yes | Folder sent the same day it is requested |
| **Closed Won / Lost** | Signed, or documented loss reason | 20-35% of proposals | Lost: root cause (price / ICP / timing / champion / feature / single-threaded) |

**Key fields:** every account role (name, title, WhatsApp if opted in); trigger event;
champion; must-ask coverage x/8; objection raised; lost reason (mandatory, no "Unknown").

**AI assist per stage:** account research and first-line personalization drafted from the
prospect's site and LinkedIn; call notes summarized into the CRM; follow-ups drafted for Ana
to approve.

## Outbound Operating Rules

- Every outbound message is a DRAFT (email connector or copy-paste text). Nothing is sent
  automatically.
- Ana reviews the first 20 drafts of any new sequence before a batch goes out.
- CRM updated the same day for every touchpoint: intro, reply, meeting, proposal, payment.
- Every Friday, what worked (subject, opening, role, segment) is saved as a learning page in
  the company brain, `sales/learnings/2026-11-outbound`, not in this playbook.

## LATAM Context

| Factor | LATAM Reality | Implication for LogiTrack |
|--------|--------------|--------------------------|
| **Primary channel** | WhatsApp read and answered far more than cold email (directional; track your own rates) | WhatsApp after the intro on Day 4, not Day 10 |
| **Warm sources** | Gremios and super whos gather the ICP in one place | SOFOFA, CChC and UIP committees before buying any list |
| **Sales cycle** | Industrial SMEs: 2-4 months; corporates add legal and vendor portal (directional) | Pipeline targets assume 3 months average |
| **Reference accounts** | LATAM buyers ask for references more than US buyers | 2 briefed lighthouse customers before scaling outbound |
| **Budget calendar** | Fiscal year January-December in Chile and Paraguay | Outbound in September-October for Q4 closes; avoid mid-December to mid-January |

Focused on LogiTrack's biggest challenge: generating leads.

1. **Esta semana:** identificá tus 3 super whos (asociación industrial, contador de pymes,
   corredor de seguros) y mandales el guion como borrador para aprobar. Meta: 10 intros en

   lote. Medí % respuesta por rol desde el primer día y pasá la campaña a
