# B2B Sales Playbook: LogiTrack

**ACV:** $8,400/year

## ICP: Ideal Customer Profile

| Field | Definition |
|-------|-----------|
| **Industry** | Transportation & Logistics (road freight, last-mile, distribution) |
| **Company Size** | 20–200 vehicle fleet; revenue $2M–$50M COP |
| **Geography** | Colombia Tier 1 cities (Bogotá, Medellín, Cali); Mexico City expansion |
| **Trigger Events** | Fleet insurance renewal, new route expansion, driver management crisis, GPS system contract expiry, new compliance regulation |
| **Buyer Title** | Operations Manager / Logistics Director / General Manager (companies <50 employees) |
| **User Title** | Fleet Coordinator / Operations Supervisor |

**ICP Tightness Check:** This ICP is specific enough to execute outbound against.
"Operations Manager of a Colombian road freight company with 20–200 trucks" is a
person you can find on LinkedIn and reach on WhatsApp. "Logistics SME" is not.

## Prospecting Sequence (8 touches, Day 1–21)

| Day | Channel | Message Type | Template Snippet | Expected Response Rate |
|-----|---------|-------------|-----------------|----------------------|
| Day 1 | LinkedIn | Connection request | "Hi [Name], I see you manage [Company]'s fleet. We work with Colombian logistics companies — 35% less time on reports. Connect?" (sent in Spanish in market) | 35–45% accept |
| Day 3 | WhatsApp | First message (after LinkedIn connect) | "Hi [Name], reaching out from LogiTrack. Saw your company on LinkedIn — we help [similar company] reduce fleet costs 22% in 90 days. Got 15 min this week?" (in Spanish locally) | 50–65% read |
| Day 5 | WhatsApp | Value-add (case study) | Send 1-pager PDF: "[Similar company] case study" — no ask, pure value | 70% open |
| Day 7 | WhatsApp | Soft ask | "Did you get a chance to look at the case study? Does it apply to your operation?" (in Spanish locally) | 40% response |
| Day 10 | Email | Formal intro | Subject: "How [Company] can cut fleet costs 22%" — LogiTrack (in Spanish locally) | 15–25% open, 3–8% reply |
| Day 14 | LinkedIn | Engagement | Meaningful comment on their company post — not "Great post!" | N/A — visibility |
| Day 17 | WhatsApp | Final direct ask | "Last shot this week — 15 minutes Thursday or Friday?" (in Spanish locally) | 30% from engaged |
| Day 21 | WhatsApp | Breakup message | "Totally get it if it's not a priority now. Can I follow up in 3 months when the timing is right?" (in Spanish locally) | 20% revive later |

**LATAM Sequence Notes:**
- WhatsApp before email — always. This is not a nice-to-have, it is the channel.
- Do not send cold email Day 1. Cold email without a LinkedIn touchpoint first will be
  treated as spam in LATAM markets.
- Voice notes on WhatsApp are culturally accepted in LATAM and drive higher response rates
  than text messages for senior decision-makers.

## Objection Handling Matrix

| Objection | Root Cause | Challenger Response | Evidence to Use |
|-----------|-----------|--------------------|----|
| "We already have a system" | Status quo bias — switching cost fear, not satisfaction with current solution | "What problem in your current operation would you fix if you were starting from scratch?" — reframe around unsolved problems the current system leaves open | Customer testimonial: "Thought the same thing. After 30 days, we recovered the migration cost." |
| "We don't have budget" | Budget not allocated — not "no money." The budget exists for things perceived as priorities. | "How much does the time your team loses on manual fleet reports cost you per month?" — quantify cost of inaction before discussing price | ROI calculator: 22% cost reduction at fleet of 50 trucks = $X/month saved vs $700/month cost |
| "Send me information" | Avoiding decision — not genuine interest. Generic PDFs will be ignored. | "Sure — to send you what's most relevant, what's your biggest pain in fleet management today?" — re-engage with discovery question before sending anything | Do NOT send generic brochure — send one targeted case study matching their profile |
| "Talk to my boss first" | Wrong level of decision-maker — you're talking to the user, not the buyer | "Sure — who would be the right person? Got 5 minutes to introduce me to the [General Manager / Director]?" — champion-building play | Champion enablement: 1-page exec summary for them to use internally |
| "You're too expensive" | Value not yet communicated — price objection is almost always a value gap | "Expensive compared to what alternative?" — then move to ROI conversation, not price negotiation. Never discount before the ROI conversation. | Hard numbers: "Customer X with 45 trucks cut costs from $14M COP/month with LogiTrack to $4.2M COP/month." |

**Root Cause Principle (Challenger Sale):** The surface objection is rarely the real
objection. Diagnosing root cause before responding prevents the most common mistake —
answering the wrong objection with the right answer.

## Demo Script Outline (45 minutes total)

| Phase | Duration | Goal | Key Question / Action |
|-------|---------|------|----------------------|
| **Opening** | 5 min | Set agenda and confirm their problem statement | "Before we start — what's the biggest pain in fleet management today?" |
| **Discovery** | 10 min | Understand their specific pain (listen 70% / talk 30%) | "How much time does your team spend on manual reports each week?" / "What would happen if that time could be reduced to zero?" |
| **Demo** | 20 min | Show ONLY the 2 features that solve the pain they described — not a feature tour | Map demo modules directly to pain points uncovered in Discovery. Never show features they didn't ask about. |
| **Close** | 5 min | Diagnose fit, not push to buy | "What would have to be true for this to be a good fit for your company?" |
| **Next Step** | 5 min | Leave with a specific next step with a date | "Can we set a call with the [General Manager] for Tuesday at 3pm?" — never end without a date |

**Demo Anti-Patterns:**
- Feature tour: Do not show every module. Show the 2 that solve their stated pain.
- No next step: "I'll send you the summary by email" is not a next step. A date is a next step.
- Talking 70% of the time: Gong.io data shows top reps talk 46%. If you're talking more, stop.

## CRM Setup Recommendation

| Stage | Definition | Conversion Target | Key Activity |
|-------|-----------|------------------|-------------|
| **Prospecting** | In sequence (Day 1–21), not yet responded | — | Send next sequence touch on schedule |
| **Qualified** | Responded + confirmed ICP fit + expressed any interest | 15–25% of prospects | Discovery call completed, pain documented |
| **Demo Scheduled** | Demo call on calendar | 40–60% of qualified | Send prep email 24h before: "To get the most out of our 45 minutes..." |
| **Demo Done** | Demo completed, next step defined | — | Log: pain points, objections heard, champion identified |
| **Proposal Sent** | Formal proposal or quote delivered | 30–50% of demo done | Follow-up within 48h by WhatsApp, not email |
| **Closed Won / Lost** | Signed contract OR documented reason for loss | 20–35% of proposals | Lost: document root cause (price / ICP / timing / champion / feature) |

**Key CRM Fields to Track:**
- Decision-maker name + title + WhatsApp number
- Trigger event that made them receptive
- Champion name (internal sponsor at company)
- Objection raised (from the 5-objection matrix above)
- Lost reason (mandatory — no "Unknown" entries)

## LATAM Context

| Factor | LATAM Reality | Implication for LogiTrack |
|--------|--------------|--------------------------|
| **Primary channel** | WhatsApp — 85%+ open rates vs 22% cold email | WhatsApp is Day 3 of sequence, not Day 10 |
| **Sales cycle** | SME logistics in Colombia: 2–4 months vs 1–2 months US | Pipeline targets need realistic monthly close rates |
| **Reference accounts** | LATAM buyers request references at higher rate than US | Build 2–3 "lighthouse" clients willing to take reference calls before scaling outbound |
| **Decision-making** | Relationship-first — the General Manager often decides even for operational tools | Always map champion to ultimate decision-maker before presenting proposal |
| **Contract timing** | Budget cycles often tied to fiscal year-end (December in Colombia) | Outbound in Q3 (September) for Q4 closes; avoid January cold outreach |

## Next Steps

*Focused on LogiTrack's stated biggest challenge: generating leads.*

1. **This week:** Build a list of 50 target accounts — company name, Operations
   Manager name, LinkedIn URL, WhatsApp number if available. Sources: LinkedIn
   Sales Navigator search (logistics + Colombia + 20–200 employees), industry
   association membership lists, your existing 5 customers' referral network.

2. **Day 1:** Send LinkedIn connection requests to all 50. Personalize the request
   with one specific observation about their company (e.g., "I see you have
   operations in Medellín and Bogotá..." — sent in Spanish in market). Generic
   requests get 20% accept; personalized get 40%+.

3. **Day 3 (after connections accepted):** Send first WhatsApp message to everyone who
   accepted. Use the Day 3 template from the sequence above. Track: % who read, % who
   respond. Target: 3+ demo calls booked from the first 50.

4. **End of Week 2:** Review who did NOT respond. Are they the wrong ICP? Wrong title?
   Wrong industry? If >70% of non-responses are a specific profile, narrow your ICP
   before continuing.

5. **Ask for 1 referral from each of your 5 current customers.** Warm intros convert
   at 5–10× the rate of cold outbound. Ask: "Do you know any Operations Manager at
   another transportation company I could help?" (in Spanish locally) — ask this
   week, not "sometime."
