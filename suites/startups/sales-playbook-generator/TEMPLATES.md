# B2B Sales Playbook: LogiTrack

**Date:** April 2026
**Stage:** 5 customers — founder-led, building repeatable process
**ACV:** $8,400/year
**Geography:** Colombia (Bogotá, Medellín, Cali primary), Mexico City expansion
**Biggest Sales Challenge:** Generating leads (top of funnel)

---

## ICP: Ideal Customer Profile

| Field | Definition |
|-------|-----------|
| **Industry** | Transportation & Logistics (road freight, last-mile, distribution) |
| **Company Size** | 20–200 vehicle fleet; revenue $2M–$50M COP |
| **Geography** | Colombia Tier 1 cities (Bogotá, Medellín, Cali); Mexico City expansion |
| **Trigger Events** | Fleet insurance renewal, new route expansion, driver management crisis, GPS system contract expiry, new compliance regulation |
| **Buyer Title** | Gerente de Operaciones / Director de Logística / Gerente General (companies <50 employees) |
| **User Title** | Coordinador de Flota / Supervisor de Operaciones |

**ICP Tightness Check:** This ICP is specific enough to execute outbound against.
"Gerente de Operaciones of a Colombian road freight company with 20–200 trucks" is a
person you can find on LinkedIn and reach on WhatsApp. "Logistics SME" is not.

## Prospecting Sequence (8 touches, Day 1–21)

| Day | Channel | Message Type | Template Snippet | Expected Response Rate |
|-----|---------|-------------|-----------------|----------------------|
| Day 1 | LinkedIn | Connection request | "Hola [Name], veo que gestionas la flota de [Company]. Trabajamos con empresas de logística colombiana — 35% menos tiempo en reportes. ¿Conectamos?" | 35–45% accept |
| Day 3 | WhatsApp | First message (after LinkedIn connect) | "Hola [Name], te escribo desde LogiTrack. Vi tu empresa en LinkedIn — ayudamos a [similar company] a reducir costos de flota un 22% en 90 días. ¿Tienes 15 minutos esta semana?" | 50–65% read |
| Day 5 | WhatsApp | Value-add (case study) | Send 1-pager PDF: "[Similar company] case study" — no ask, pure value | 70% open |
| Day 7 | WhatsApp | Soft ask | "¿Tuviste oportunidad de ver el caso de estudio? ¿Aplica a su operación?" | 40% response |
| Day 10 | Email | Formal intro | Subject: "Cómo [Company] puede reducir 22% sus costos de flota" — LogiTrack | 15–25% open, 3–8% reply |
| Day 14 | LinkedIn | Engagement | Meaningful comment on their company post — not "Great post!" | N/A — visibility |
| Day 17 | WhatsApp | Final direct ask | "Última oportunidad esta semana — ¿15 minutos el jueves o viernes?" | 30% from engaged |
| Day 21 | WhatsApp | Breakup message | "Entiendo si no es prioridad ahora. ¿Puedo seguirte en 3 meses cuando sea el momento?" | 20% revive later |

**LATAM Sequence Notes:**
- WhatsApp before email — always. This is not a nice-to-have, it is the channel.
- Do not send cold email Day 1. Cold email without a LinkedIn touchpoint first will be
  treated as spam in LATAM markets.
- Voice notes on WhatsApp are culturally accepted in LATAM and drive higher response rates
  than text messages for senior decision-makers.

## Objection Handling Matrix

| Objection | Root Cause | Challenger Response | Evidence to Use |
|-----------|-----------|--------------------|----|
| "Ya tenemos un sistema" | Status quo bias — switching cost fear, not satisfaction with current solution | "¿Qué problema de su operación actual solucionaría si empezaran de cero?" — reframe around unsolved problems the current system leaves open | Customer testimonial: "Pensaba lo mismo. Después de 30 días, recuperamos el costo de migración." |
| "No tenemos presupuesto" | Budget not allocated — not "no money." The budget exists for things perceived as priorities. | "¿Cuánto les cuesta hoy el tiempo que pierden en reportes manuales de flota por mes?" — quantify cost of inaction before discussing price | ROI calculator: 22% cost reduction at fleet of 50 trucks = $X/month saved vs $700/month cost |
| "Mándame información" | Avoiding decision — not genuine interest. Generic PDFs will be ignored. | "Claro — para enviarte lo más relevante, ¿cuál es su mayor dolor en gestión de flota hoy?" — re-engage with discovery question before sending anything | Do NOT send generic brochure — send one targeted case study matching their profile |
| "Primero habla con mi jefe" | Wrong level of decision-maker — you're talking to the user, not the buyer | "Claro — ¿con quién sería la conversación correcta? ¿Tienes 5 minutos para presentarme con [Gerente General / Director]?" — champion-building play | Champion enablement: 1-page exec summary for them to use internally |
| "Están muy caros" | Value not yet communicated — price objection is almost always a value gap | "¿Caros comparado con qué alternativa?" — then move to ROI conversation, not price negotiation. Never discount before the ROI conversation. | Hard numbers: "Cliente X con 45 camiones redujo costos en $14M COP/mes con LogiTrack a $4.2M COP/mes." |

**Root Cause Principle (Challenger Sale):** The surface objection is rarely the real
objection. Diagnosing root cause before responding prevents the most common mistake —
answering the wrong objection with the right answer.

## Demo Script Outline (45 minutes total)

| Phase | Duration | Goal | Key Question / Action |
|-------|---------|------|----------------------|
| **Opening** | 5 min | Set agenda and confirm their problem statement | "Antes de comenzar — ¿qué es lo que más les cuesta hoy en gestión de flota?" |
| **Discovery** | 10 min | Understand their specific pain (listen 70% / talk 30%) | "¿Cuánto tiempo pasa su equipo en reportes manuales por semana?" / "¿Qué pasaría si ese tiempo se pudiera reducir a cero?" |
| **Demo** | 20 min | Show ONLY the 2 features that solve the pain they described — not a feature tour | Map demo modules directly to pain points uncovered in Discovery. Never show features they didn't ask about. |
| **Close** | 5 min | Diagnose fit, not push to buy | "¿Qué tendría que ser verdad para que esto fuera una buena opción para su empresa?" |
| **Next Step** | 5 min | Leave with a specific next step with a date | "¿Hacemos una llamada con [Gerente General] el martes a las 3pm?" — never end without a date |

**Demo Anti-Patterns:**
- Feature tour: Do not show every module. Show the 2 that solve their stated pain.
- No next step: "Te mando el resumen por email" is not a next step. A date is a next step.
- Talking 70% of the time: Gong.io data shows top reps talk 46%. If you're talking more, stop.

## CRM Setup Recommendation

| Stage | Definition | Conversion Target | Key Activity |
|-------|-----------|------------------|-------------|
| **Prospecting** | In sequence (Day 1–21), not yet responded | — | Send next sequence touch on schedule |
| **Qualified** | Responded + confirmed ICP fit + expressed any interest | 15–25% of prospects | Discovery call completed, pain documented |
| **Demo Scheduled** | Demo call on calendar | 40–60% of qualified | Send prep email 24h before: "Para aprovechar los 45 minutos..." |
| **Demo Done** | Demo completed, next step defined | — | Log: pain points, objections heard, champion identified |
| **Proposal Sent** | Formal proposal or quote delivered | 30–50% of demo done | Follow-up within 48h by WhatsApp, not email |
| **Closed Won / Lost** | Signed contract OR documented reason for loss | 20–35% of proposals | Lost: document root cause (price / ICP / timing / champion / feature) |

**Key CRM Fields to Track:**
- Decision-maker name + title + WhatsApp number
- Trigger event that made them receptive
- Champion name (internal sponsor at company)
- Objection raised (from the 5-objection matrix above)
- Lost reason (mandatory — no "Unknown" entries)

## LATAM Context

| Factor | LATAM Reality | Implication for LogiTrack |
|--------|--------------|--------------------------|
| **Primary channel** | WhatsApp — 85%+ open rates vs 22% cold email | WhatsApp is Day 3 of sequence, not Day 10 |
| **Sales cycle** | SME logistics in Colombia: 2–4 months vs 1–2 months US | Pipeline targets need realistic monthly close rates |
| **Reference accounts** | LATAM buyers request references at higher rate than US | Build 2–3 "lighthouse" clients willing to take reference calls before scaling outbound |
| **Decision-making** | Relationship-first — Gerente General often decides even for operational tools | Always map champion to ultimate decision-maker before presenting proposal |
| **Contract timing** | Budget cycles often tied to fiscal year-end (December in Colombia) | Outbound in Q3 (September) for Q4 closes; avoid January cold outreach |

## Próximos Pasos

*Focused on LogiTrack's stated biggest challenge: generating leads.*

1. **This week:** Build a list of 50 target accounts — company name, Gerente de
   Operaciones name, LinkedIn URL, WhatsApp number if available. Sources: LinkedIn
   Sales Navigator search (logistics + Colombia + 20–200 employees), industry
   association membership lists, your existing 5 customers' referral network.

2. **Day 1:** Send LinkedIn connection requests to all 50. Personalize the request
   with one specific observation about their company ("veo que tienen operaciones
   en Medellín y Bogotá..."). Generic requests get 20% accept; personalized get 40%+.

3. **Day 3 (after connections accepted):** Send first WhatsApp message to everyone who
   accepted. Use the Day 3 template from the sequence above. Track: % who read, % who
   respond. Target: 3+ demo calls booked from the first 50.

4. **End of Week 2:** Review who did NOT respond. Are they the wrong ICP? Wrong title?
   Wrong industry? If >70% of non-responses are a specific profile, narrow your ICP
   before continuing.

5. **Ask for 1 referral from each of your 5 current customers.** Warm intros convert
   at 5–10× the rate of cold outbound. "¿Conoces algún Gerente de Operaciones de
   otra empresa de transporte a quien le pueda ayudar?" — ask this week, not "sometime."
