# Sales Playbook Framework — Predictable Revenue + Challenger Sale

**Purpose:** Structural reference for the sales-playbook-generator skill. Read this
before designing any prospecting sequence or objection handling matrix.

## 1. Predictable Revenue — The Outbound Prospecting Machine

Aaron Ross built Salesforce's outbound machine from $0 to $100M ARR by separating
prospecting from closing. The insight: the same person cannot do both well.

### Role Separation

| Role | Focus | Metric | When to Hire |
|------|-------|--------|-------------|
| **Prospector (SDR)** | Cold outreach → qualified meeting | Meetings booked/week | When founder closes consistently and has a repeatable ICP |
| **Closer (AE)** | Demo → close | Win rate, ACV | When there are more qualified meetings than founder can run |
| **Customer Success** | Onboard → expand → renew | NRR, churn | Before you need them (hire at first 10 customers) |

**Founder-led phase:** Before $500k ARR, the founder IS the SDR and the AE. The goal
is to learn what works before systematizing. Do not hire SDRs until the founder can
demo and close with >25% win rate.

### Pipeline Stage Definitions

1. **Suspect** — In ICP but no contact yet
2. **Prospect** — In sequence, not yet qualified
3. **Qualified** — Confirmed ICP + expressed pain + has budget + has authority
4. **Demo Scheduled** — Meeting confirmed on calendar
5. **Proposal Sent** — Pricing/contract delivered
6. **Closed Won / Lost** — Final outcome recorded

**Cold Calling 2.0 Principle:** Do not cold call. Sequence via email + LinkedIn +
WhatsApp to get permission for a meeting. The goal of the first touch is not to sell —
it is to qualify interest and book a call.

### Sequence Design Principles

- **7–9 touches** over 21 days is optimal for B2B outbound. Fewer touches = low
  conversion. More touches without response = prospect is not ICP.
- **Multi-channel** — each touch in a different channel increases total read rate
  by 30–40% vs single-channel sequences.
- **Specific intervals** — Day 1, 3, 5, 7, 10, 14, 17, 21. Not "follow up in a week."
  Specific calendar dates prevent drift.
- **Personalization threshold** — first touch must reference something specific about
  the prospect or their company. Generic openers: 10–15% open. Personalized: 30–45%.
- **Breakup message** at Day 21 — a polite close to the sequence. Often generates
  more responses than earlier touches because it creates a "last chance" dynamic.

## 2. Challenger Sale — Profile and Tactics

Dixon & Adamson's CEB research across 6,000+ salespeople identified 5 profiles:
Hard Worker, Relationship Builder, Lone Wolf, Problem Solver, Challenger.

**Result:** Challengers are the top performers at complex B2B sales. Relationship
Builders are the worst performers for complex sales (despite being the most commonly
hired profile). For enterprise deals, Challenger profile wins 54% of time vs 4% for
Relationship Builders.

### The Three Challenger Behaviors

| Behavior | Definition | Example |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| **Teach** | Offer insight that reframes how the prospect thinks about their problem | "Most logistics companies think their cost problem is fuel. Our data shows it's unplanned overtime from manual route adjustment — 3× higher impact." |
| **Tailor** | Customize the message to the specific economic driver of this buyer | For Gerente de Operaciones: "Your fuel costs." For Gerente General: "Your EBITDA margin." Same product, different frame. |
| **Take Control** | Own the sales process — push back constructively, hold to timeline, qualify hard | "Para avanzar juntos necesito hablar con quien aprueba presupuesto esta semana. ¿Puedes presentarme con esa persona?" |

### Objection Root Cause Analysis

The surface objection is rarely the real objection. Diagnosing root cause before
responding is the most important Challenger skill.

| Surface Objection | Possible Root Causes | Diagnostic Question |
|------------------|---------------------|---------------------|
| "We already have a system" | Fear of switching cost / internal political risk / genuinely happy | "¿Qué problema de su operación actual solucionaría si empezaran de cero?" |
| "No budget" | Not a priority / budget not allocated yet / waiting for year-end | "¿Cuánto les está costando no resolver este problema por mes?" |
| "Send me information" | Avoiding decision / not the right contact / no urgency | "¿Qué necesitaría ver en la información para que valiera una llamada de 15 minutos?" |
| "Too expensive" | Value not communicated / wrong tier of buyer / bad timing | "¿Caro comparado con qué alternativa específica?" |
| "Talk to my boss first" | Wrong level — talking to user not buyer | "¿Quién sería la persona correcta? ¿Me puedes presentar?" |

## 3. Gong.io Win Rate Benchmarks

Gong.io analyzed 100M+ sales calls and interactions. Key findings for B2B sales:

### Talk-to-Listen Ratio

- **Top reps** talk 46% of time in discovery calls (listen 54%)
- **Average reps** talk 68% of time
- **Implication:** If you're talking more than 50% during discovery, stop. You're
  diagnosing based on your assumptions, not their reality.

### Discovery Question Volume

- **8+ questions** per discovery call = highest win rate
- **4 or fewer questions** = significantly lower win rate
- **Implication:** Design discovery with at least 8 specific questions mapped to
  your value hypothesis before each call.

### Killer Phrase

- **"Does that make sense?" / "¿Tiene sentido?"** kills deal momentum
- Implies the prospect may not understand (condescending) or that you're unsure of
  your own pitch (weak)
- Replace with: "¿Qué parte de esto aplica más a su situación?" (discovery) or silence

### Competitor Mentions

- **3–4 competitor mentions** per call = highest win rate
- **0 mentions** = likely not in a real evaluation (prospect uncommitted)
- **10+ mentions** = buyer using your pitch to negotiate with existing vendor
- **Implication:** When competitors come up 3–4 times, you are likely in an active deal.

## 4. ICP Definition Template

A useful ICP has 6 fields minimum. Each field must be specific enough to support
outbound prospecting.

| Field | Bad (Too Vague) | Good (Specific Enough to Act On) |
|-------|----------------|----------------------------------|
| **Industry** | "B2B companies" | "Road freight and last-mile logistics" |
| **Company Size** | "SMEs" | "20–200 vehicle fleet; revenue $2M–$50M" |
| **Geography** | "LATAM" | "Colombia Tier 1 (Bogotá, Medellín, Cali)" |
| **Trigger Events** | "When they need us" | "GPS contract expiry, new route expansion, compliance regulation change" |
| **Buyer Title** | "Decision maker" | "Gerente de Operaciones / Gerente General (companies <50 employees)" |
| **User Title** | "The team" | "Coordinador de Flota / Supervisor de Operaciones" |

**ICP Tightness Test:** Can you find 50 people matching this ICP on LinkedIn in under
2 hours? If not, it's too vague. If you find 5,000 in 10 minutes, it may be too broad.
The right ICP has 500–2,000 named prospects per geography.

## 5. Prospecting Sequence Design Principles

### Number of Touches

| ACV Range | Recommended Touches | Rationale |
|-----------|---------------------|-----------|
| <$1,000 | 3–5 touches | Low value; diminishing returns beyond 5 |
| $1k–$10k | 7–9 touches | Core LATAM B2B range — enough to build familiarity |
| $10k–$50k | 9–12 touches | Higher value justifies more investment per prospect |
| $50k+ | 12+ touches with account-based approach | Enterprise requires multi-stakeholder sequencing |

### Channel Mix by Geography

| Market | Primary | Secondary | Last Resort |
|--------|---------|-----------|------------|
| **Colombia / Mexico / Argentina** | WhatsApp | LinkedIn | Email |
| **Brazil** | WhatsApp | Email | LinkedIn |
| **US** | Email | LinkedIn | Phone |
| **Mixed LATAM + US** | WhatsApp (LATAM) + Email (US) | LinkedIn (both) | Phone (US only) |

### Personalization Requirements by Touch

- **Touch 1 (LinkedIn connect):** Must reference company or role specifically
- **Touch 2 (WhatsApp first message):** Must reference LinkedIn connection + one
  specific detail about their business
- **Touch 3 (value asset):** Send case study from same industry/geography — never generic
- **Touches 4–7:** Can templatize but maintain first-name personalization
- **Final touch (breakup):** Must acknowledge time has passed and leave door open
