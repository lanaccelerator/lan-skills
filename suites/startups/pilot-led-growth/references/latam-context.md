See shared/latam-benchmarks.md for canonical LATAM VC benchmark data.

When presenting benchmarks, follow this annotation protocol:
- Label US benchmarks: `(US-sourced benchmark, [Source])`
- Follow with LATAM figure: `(LATAM, LAVCA 2025)` or `(LATAM, Cuantico 2026)`
- Note the direction: LATAM advantage, LATAM discount, or same as US

## How corporate pilots play out in LATAM

- **Regional holdings and multinationals are expansion routes.** A pilot with good traction
  in one country of a group often opens the same solution in the group's other countries,
  if the problem and the solution repeat. Ask early which countries share the process.
- **Tenders after the pilot.** Large companies and state-owned firms may be required to run
  a tender even after a successful pilot, and compliance rules can stop the startup from
  writing the tender terms. Ask before the pilot; decide consciously whether to compete. The
  startup's data from that same client is its advantage. Public buyers in several countries
  are adopting innovative public procurement, which fits ambiguous problems better than
  classic tenders (verify the mechanism in each country before citing it).
- **Free pilots are sometimes expected.** One founder in the workshop reported that charging
  for the pilot blocked the whole sale in Argentina (a single data point). Keep the pilot cheap, and put the negotiation weight on the post-pilot contract.
- **Sustainability and impact areas sit far from innovation units.** Climate, health,
  financial inclusion and agro solutions often have their real buyer outside the innovation
  team. Map the owner of the problem, not only the door.
- **Vendor portal and invoicing gate the first payment.** Keep the supplier folder ready
  (tax ID such as RUC, RUT, CUIT, RFC or CNPJ; good standing; bank certificate; security
  documents) and check local electronic invoicing before Day 1. See
  references/shared/b2b-buying-2026.md, section 7.
- **Data protection:** LGPD (Brazil), LFPDPPP (Mexico), Ley 1581 (Colombia), Ley 19.628 as
  reformed by Ley 21.719, in force from 1 December 2026 (Chile), Ley 29733 (Peru), Ley 25.326
  (Argentina): agree data access and retention before any customer data moves.
- Do not quote LATAM pilot conversion rates as statistics: no traceable regional source
  exists. Use the founder's own pilot-to-contract rate instead.
