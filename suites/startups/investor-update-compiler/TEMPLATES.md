# Investor Update Compiler — Output Template

ALWAYS use this exact structure for cap-table audience updates. Adapt down for prospect/newsletter audiences (per audience adaptation table in SKILL.md). Every section here is required.

---

## Example Output: Trezo — April 2026 (Cap Table audience)

*This is a filled example for Trezo — a Paraguayan B2B SaaS for recurring billing of SMBs, $95K MRR, 47 customers, early post-revenue. Use it as the structural template for every monthly investor update.*

---

```
Subject: Trezo — April 2026 Update

Hi [Name],

TL;DR: We closed April with $95K MRR (+19% MoM), an all-time record. We launched the WhatsApp Business integration, already active in 12 customers with +18% in orders processed. Looking for 3 intros to Heads of Operations at regional logistics companies to validate the enterprise segment.

KPIs April 2026

| KPI | Current | Mar 2026 | Q2 Target | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MRR | $95K | $80K | $130K | yellow |
| Paying customers | 47 | 45 | 65 | yellow |
| Monthly logo churn | 4.4% | 4.7% | 2.5% | red |
| NPS | 42 | 41 | 45 | green |
| CAC payback | 14 months | 13 months | 11 months | red |
| NRR | 108% | 103% | 115% | yellow |

Financials April 2026
- Revenue: $95K (+19% MoM)
- Total costs: $105.2K (payroll $60K, marketing $38K, infra $5.7K, other $1.5K)
- Net burn: -$10.2K
- Cash balance at close: $327.3K
- Runway at current pace: ~32 months (assuming stable burn)
- Base-case runway (no new capital and growth flat): ~14 months

Capital plan: opening Seed $1.5M round in June 2026 to close before September.

Wins
1. MRR record: closed at $95K, +19% MoM. Driver: 4 new premium customers (Pro tier) and one upgrade from Starter to Pro.
2. Launched WhatsApp Business integration on April 14. 12 customers activated it in their first week. They report +18% in orders processed vs the previous flow.
3. Confirmed the hire of María González as Head of Customer Success (ex-Rappi). Starts May 5.

Lowlights
1. Logo churn rose to 4.4% (vs 2.5% target). 2 SMB customers left due to price sensitivity. Plan: launching $99/month tier on May 10 to retain this segment. If it works, reduces churn 1–1.5 pts in 60 days.
2. CAC payback rose to 14 months (vs 11 target). Driver: LinkedIn ads cost rose 40% MoM. Paused the channel on April 25, redirecting that budget to the referral program.

Asks
1. Intros to Heads of Operations at regional logistics companies with 50+ drivers. Validating enterprise tier and need 2 more conversations.
2. Pricing feedback on the $99 tier before May 7. Sending a separate draft to whoever wants to review.

Looking ahead — May 2026
- Close 5 new customers above $200/month
- Launch $99 tier relaunch on 05/10
- Onboard María (CSM)
- Begin active Seed pipeline (15 conversations target Q2)

Sofía Ramírez
CEO, Trezo

---

Appendices on request: 12-month KPIs, April cohort retention, Q2 OKRs with detailed scoring.
```

---

## Anti-patterns Detected and Removed

- **"Things are going great"** — vague. Replaced with the +19% MoM MRR delta in TL;DR.
- **"Need help with intros"** — vague ask. Replaced with specific persona, industry, and quantity ("Heads of Operations at regional logistics companies with 50+ drivers, 2 more conversations").
- **"Working on reducing churn"** — lament without plan. Replaced with concrete action (launch $99 tier on May 10) plus expected impact (1–1.5 pts in 60 days).
- **No financial section in original draft** — added mandatory section with revenue, costs, burn, cash, and runway. Cap-table investors expect this every month.

---

## Audience Adaptation

This template is the **cap_table** version (most detailed). For other audiences:

- **hot_prospects**: keep TL;DR, KPIs, and a financials summary (revenue + runway only). Drop lowlights detail. Keep asks general.
- **warm_prospects**: keep TL;DR, KPI highlights, and revenue line only. No lowlights, no asks.
- **newsletter**: TL;DR + 2–3 highlights + revenue line. No financial detail, no asks.

## Quarterly example (cadencia trimestral, cap table, Q2 2026)

*Same hypothetical company (Trezo). Use this structure when the cadence is
quarterly. For semi-annual, keep the same sections, compare HoH (H1 vs H2 of
the previous year), show 6 monthly values in the appendix, and score both
quarters' OKRs. Read from the company brain: `metrics/kpis-2026`,
`okrs/2026-q2`, `finance/monthly-2026`, `investors/updates/2026-03-quarterly-cap-table`.*

```
Cadencia inferida: trimestral (cierre de junio y la actualización anterior a este público fue trimestral).

Asunto: Trezo, actualización Q2 2026: MRR USD 121K (+51% QoQ), primer mes con caja positiva

Hola [Nombre]:

TL;DR: Cerramos el Q2 con USD 121K de MRR (+51% vs Q1), por debajo de la meta de USD 130K. Junio fue
el primer mes con flujo de caja positivo (+USD 3K). Abrimos la ronda Seed de USD 1,5M y buscamos 2
intros a fondos con tesis en logística B2B.

KPIs Q2 2026

| KPI | Cierre Q2 | Cierre Q1 | QoQ | Meta Q2 | Estado |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MRR | USD 121K | USD 80K | +51% | USD 130K | amarillo |
| Clientes pagos | 58 | 45 | +29% | 65 | amarillo |
| Churn mensual de logos (promedio del trimestre) | 3,6% | 4,7% | -1,1 pp | 2,5% | rojo |
| NRR | 112% | 103% | +9 pp | 115% | amarillo |
| CAC payback | 12 meses | 13 meses | -1 mes | 11 meses | amarillo |

Finanzas Q2 2026 (flujos sumados, saldos al cierre)
- Ingresos del trimestre: USD 324K (Q1: USD 222K, +46%)
- Costos del trimestre: USD 335,2K (nómina USD 186K, marketing USD 112K, infraestructura USD 18K, otros USD 19,2K)
- Burn neto del trimestre: -USD 11,2K (abril -10,2K, mayo -4K, junio +3K)
- Caja al 30/06: USD 326,3K
- Runway al ritmo actual: sin consumo de caja desde junio; por encima de 24 meses aunque se repita el burn promedio del Q2 (USD 3,7K por mes)
- Runway caso base (crecimiento plano y las 3 contrataciones del Q3, +USD 25K por mes de costos): ~15 meses
- Cambio vs la actualización del Q1: el runway caso base pasa de ~14 a ~15 meses

Lo más importante del trimestre
1. Integración con WhatsApp Business: activa en 31 clientes; quienes la usan procesan 18% más pedidos.
2. Plan de USD 99 lanzado el 10/05: retuvo 6 de 8 clientes que habían pedido la baja por precio; el churn bajó de 4,4% en abril a 2,9% en junio.
3. Primer mes con flujo de caja positivo (junio), sin recortar marketing.

Lo que dijimos en la actualización del Q1
| Compromiso | Estado |
|---|---|
| Lanzar integración con WhatsApp Business | Hecho (14/04) |
| Contratar Head of Customer Success | Hecho (05/05) |
| Llegar a USD 130K de MRR | Parcial (USD 121K) |
| Abrir la ronda Seed en junio | Hecho (02/06) |

OKRs Q2 (puntaje final, 0,0 a 1,0)
- O1 Escalar ingresos recurrentes: KR1 MRR USD 130K: 0,82 | KR2 65 clientes: 0,65
- O2 Retener la base: KR3 churn menor a 2,5%: 0,6 | KR4 NRR 115%: 0,75
Prioridad Q3: churn menor a 2,5% sostenido 3 meses; sin eso la ronda se negocia peor.

Lo que no salió
1. No llegamos a la meta de clientes (58 de 65): el canal de LinkedIn Ads se pausó en abril y el programa de referidos tardó 6 semanas en compensarlo. Plan: meta de 12 clientes por referidos en el Q3, medida mensualmente.
2. El churn sigue en rojo. Plan: Customer Success llama a cada cliente con uso decreciente durante 2 semanas seguidas; meta 2,5% en septiembre.

Pedidos
1. Intros a 2 fondos Seed con tesis en logística o software B2B en la región; la ronda es de USD 1,5M con USD 600K comprometidos.
2. Un CFO fraccional para preparar el data room antes del 31/08.
3. Una intro a un Gerente de Operaciones de una distribuidora con más de 50 choferes para validar el plan enterprise.

Próximo trimestre (Q3 2026)
- MRR USD 160K, 75 clientes, churn 2,5%
- Cerrar la ronda Seed antes del 30/09
- 3 contrataciones: 2 ventas, 1 ingeniería

Sofía Ramírez
CEO, Trezo

---

Anexos a pedido: valores mensuales abril-junio por KPI, cohortes del Q2, OKRs del Q3 completos.
```
