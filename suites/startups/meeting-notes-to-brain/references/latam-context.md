# LATAM Context: Meeting Notes

See shared/latam-benchmarks.md for canonical LATAM benchmark data
when a note cites a metric.

## Language

- Meetings frequently switch between Spanish, Portuguese and English inside
  the same call. Write the note in the dominant language of the meeting;
  keep quoted commitments in the original language.
- Recorders transcribe local proper nouns poorly (surnames, city names,
  company acronyms such as SRL, SA, SAS, SAECA, RUC, CUIT, CNPJ). Resolve
  every name against the brain's people and company pages before filing.
  If no match exists, keep the transcript spelling and mark `[verificar]`.

## Follow-up channel

- In LATAM B2B, follow-up often happens on WhatsApp, not email, especially
  with owners and general managers of SMBs and family groups. When the
  meeting shows that channel (they shared a number, said "te escribo por
  WhatsApp"), produce the follow-up as a short plain-text message in
  addition to, or instead of, the email draft. It is still a draft for the
  user to send.
- Formality: use "usted" with senior counterparts in corporate or family
  group settings unless the meeting itself was informal; voseo or "tú" only
  if the counterpart used it.

## Commitments

- Verbal "lo vemos" or "lo charlamos" is not a commitment. Record as an open
  question.
- Dates stated relative to holidays ("después de Semana Santa", "pasando el
  receso") must be converted to an absolute date and marked
  `[fecha propuesta]`.
- Date format DD/MM/YYYY in all output.
