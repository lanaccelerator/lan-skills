## Target Reality Check

| Target | Verdict | Evidence |
|--------|---------|----------|
| 5x in 8 years | Not supported | Even $1-10M funds, the best size bucket, reached 4.91x TVPI only at the 90th percentile (2019 vintage, Carta Emerging Manager Paradox, Oct 2026). 75th-pct net IRR is in the mid-teens for recent vintages (2017: 15.8%; 2024: 19.1%) (Carta Q2 2026) |
| 2.5-3x in 10 years | Achievable with discipline | Requires one outlier with meaningful ownership plus a few 3-5x outcomes |

**Reset:** plan for 2.5-3x over 10 years; treat anything above as upside.

## Route: Direct vs Funds

**split**: $2M direct (25 companies, Mexico-led) and $1M in one or two LATAM seed funds with Brazil
exposure. The funds buy the access the family does not have. Run vc-fund-due-diligence on each.

## Portfolio Diagnosis: Current State vs Optimal

| Dimension | Current State | Optimal for 2.5-3x Target | Gap |
|-----------|--------------|----------------------|-----|
| Company count | 6 | 25 direct + 1-2 funds | Under-diversified by 19 companies |
| Stage mix | 100% Seed | ~35% pre-seed, ~65% seed; Series A only via follow-on | No early-stage optionality |
| Geographic mix | 100% Mexico City | Mexico core + Brazil via funds + 7 other LATAM deals | Brazil entirely absent: major gap |
| Reserve capital | $0 (fully deployed) | 35% of direct sleeve ($700K) | No follow-on capacity |
| Pro-rata rights | Unknown / not stated | 100% of deals | Confirm and negotiate where absent |
| Sector | 100% B2B SaaS | 60% SaaS, 25% fintech, 15% other | Keep SaaS as core; add fintech |

**Diagnosis:** six companies is not a portfolio. Only 12% of LATAM seed startups raise a Series A
within three years (LAVCA 2025): six seed investments give 0.7 expected Series A rounds and a 46%
chance that none of them gets one. With a 2% LATAM 10x rate *(directional)*, the chance of even one
outlier is 11%.

All six are Mexico City B2B SaaS at seed. Brazil, ~49% of 2025 LATAM VC dollars (Cuantico 2026), is
absent. The portfolio is one bet on one city's exit market.

## Optimal Portfolio Size

**Return target:** 5x on $3M = $15M gross return

**Target for the direct sleeve (2.5-3x):** 25 companies (6 existing + 19 new).

**Probability math** (P of at least one 10x+ = 1 - (1 - p)^N):
- 6 companies: 22% at the US base rate of 4% (Correlation Ventures via Seth Levine, 2014); 11% at a
  LATAM 2% *(directional)*
- 25 companies: 64% (US rate) / 40% (LATAM rate)
- Expected Series A rounds at 25 companies: 25 x 12% = 3 (LAVCA 2025)

**Implication:** even 25 bets leave a real chance of no outlier. The fund sleeve adds more draws
without more family time.

## Reserve Capital Allocation

**Total reserve:** $700,000 (35% of the $2M direct sleeve)

| Reserve Use | Amount | Criteria |
|-------------|--------|----------|
| Follow-on into top 3 existing companies | $350K | Companies that beat the plan they raised on and have a new institutional lead |
| Follow-on into new portfolio additions | $250K | Pro rata in the best 3-4 new deals at their next round |
| Opportunistic reserve | $100K | Exceptional deals outside the plan |

**Rule:** Do not deploy reserve capital until the portfolio company has demonstrated at least
one of: (1) closed a priced round with institutional co-investor, (2) reached $1M ARR, or
(3) achieved 12-month retention >80%.

why the 5x target fails and why pro rata on the winners matters most.

near 0.5% and the outlier tier shrinks by half.

## Next Steps

1. **Audit existing 6 deals within 30 days.** For each company: confirm current ownership
   percentage (fully diluted), verify whether pro-rata rights exist in the investment
   documents, and evaluate whether to follow on: only companies that beat the plan they raised on and have a
   new institutional lead get reserve capital.

2. **Make pro rata a hard requirement on every new deal.** It is not standard in LATAM seed
   documents; ask for a side letter. Without it, reserves have nowhere to go.

3. **Shortlist two LATAM seed funds with Brazil exposure within 60 days** and run
   vc-fund-due-diligence on each. Brazil took ~49% of 2025 LATAM VC dollars (Cuantico 2026) and is
   0% of your portfolio. Funds are the fastest route; direct Brazil deals only as co-investments
   alongside those funds.

4. **Open a follow-on reserve account.** Physically segregate $700K from deployment capital
   into a separate account or money market fund. Label it "Follow-on Reserve: Not for New
   Deals." The biggest family office error is deploying reserve capital in new deals
   and having nothing left when winners need your pro-rata.
