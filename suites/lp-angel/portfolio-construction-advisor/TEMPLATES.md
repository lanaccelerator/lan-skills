represents 65% of LATAM exits in 2024 — is entirely absent. Portfolio is functionally
betting on Mexico exits only.

## Optimal Portfolio Size

**Return target:** 5x on $3M = $15M gross return

**Power law requirement for 5x:**
- Need 1 company returning 10x+ (the "fund returner"): provides ~$5–7M
- Need 4–5 companies returning 2–5x: provides ~$6–8M combined
- Remainder write-offs: ~$2–3M loss on deployed capital

**Minimum company count for 5x target:** 25 companies
**Recommended target:** 28–30 companies (provides statistical buffer for LATAM conversion rates)

**LATAM conversion math:**
- 25 companies × 12% LATAM angel-to-Series A = 3 expected Series A conversions
- 3 Series A companies × 20% Series A-to-exit rate = 0.6 expected exits with meaningful returns
- At 25 companies: ~20% probability of including a 10x+ outlier

**Implication:** Even 25 companies is not a guarantee of a fund-returner. It is a probability
game. The goal is to make enough bets at the right ownership levels that the power law works
in your favor.

## Next Steps

1. **Audit existing 6 deals within 30 days** — For each company: confirm current ownership
   percentage (fully diluted), verify whether pro-rata rights exist in the investment
   documents, and evaluate whether to follow on or not based on current metrics. Companies
   with >15% MoM growth AND >90% NRR are follow-on candidates. Companies below both thresholds
   should not receive reserve capital.

2. **Negotiate pro-rata rights on next 3 deals before deploying** — Pro-rata rights are
   not standard in all LATAM Seed term sheets. Make them a hard requirement on every new
   investment from today forward. Without pro-rata, you will be diluted to <0.5% ownership
   by Series A — which makes the 5x return target arithmetically impossible.

3. **Identify 2 Brazil leads within 60 days** — Brazil is 65% of LATAM exits and 0% of
   your portfolio. Contact at least 3 Brazil-based accelerators (Cubo, Liga Ventures,
   ACE Startups) and 2 Brazil-focused angels to establish deal flow pipeline. Do not deploy
   capital until you have relationships — cold deal flow in Brazil is low quality.

4. **Open a follow-on reserve account** — Physically segregate $1.05M from deployment capital
   into a separate account or money market fund. Label it "Follow-on Reserve — Not to Deploy
   in New Deals." The biggest family office error is deploying reserve capital in new deals
   and having nothing left when winners need your pro-rata.
