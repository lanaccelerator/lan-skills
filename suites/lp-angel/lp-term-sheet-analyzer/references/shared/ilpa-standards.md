## Alignment of Interest

| Term | ILPA Principles 3.0 | VC market (Carta 2025) | Red flag |
|------|---------------------|------------------------|----------|
| GP commit | "Substantial", contributed in cash rather than through a management fee waiver | Median 1.7% of fund; $1-10M funds median 2% (75th pct 6.15%); $25-100M funds 1.5% | Under 1% cash, or funded mainly by fee waiver |
| Waterfall | Whole-of-fund (European) is best practice | Not reported | Deal-by-deal with no interim clawback or escrow |
| Preferred return (hurdle) | Calculated from capital call date (or from when capital is at risk if a credit line bridges) | Rare in VC: 9.5% of $1-10M funds, 12.4% of $100M+ funds have one | Not a red flag by itself in VC; check what the anchor LP requires |
| Carry | Not set by ILPA | Median 20% across vintages 2018-2025; 90th pct 25%, rising to 30% in 2025 for top funds | Above 20% without a top-quartile DPI record |
| Management fee | Should reflect a reasonable budget; offsets below | Median 2% in the (median 5-year) investment period; 75th pct 2.5%; 81.9% of funds step down at least once; median after first step-down 1.9% | Above 2.5%, or no step-down |
| Clawback | Gross of taxes; repaid within two years of recognition; escrow with significant reserves (e.g., 30% of carry or more); joint and several GP liability or creditworthy guarantee | Not reported | No clawback; no escrow or guarantee |

## Governance

- **Key person:** a key person event triggers an **automatic suspension** of the investment period, which becomes permanent within 180 days unless a supermajority in interest of LPs votes to reinstate (ILPA 3.0). Changes to key person provisions need a majority in interest of LPs. LPs should hear about any material personnel change, not only key persons.
- **No-fault rights:** a supermajority in interest of LPs should be able to remove the GP or dissolve the fund without cause (ILPA 3.0); two-thirds in interest is the commonly cited ILPA reference level. Removal should bring a meaningful carry reduction.
- **LPAC:** workable size, a cross-section of LPs by commitment size, type and relationship with the GP; approves conflicts and related-party transactions.
- **Subscription lines:** outstanding no more than 180 days, capped as a % of commitments, never used to fund early distributions (ILPA 3.0). Ask for IRR with and without the facility.

## Transparency: Fees and Expenses

| Item | ILPA Principles 3.0 | Abuse to flag |
|------|---------------------|---------------|
| Portfolio company fees (transaction, monitoring, director) | "100% offset against the management fee and subject to standard disclosure" | GP keeps them, or offset below 100% |
| Organizational expenses | Reasonable and capped; excess offsets the management fee | Uncapped org costs |
| Placement agent, consultant, operating partner costs | Offset against the management fee when charged to the fund | Charged to the fund on top of the fee |
| Fund operating expenses | Disclose granularly | Carta 2025: median $1-10M fund spends ~3.4% of commitments on opex in five years vs ~1% for $100M+ funds; much higher deserves questions |

**Lifetime fee load benchmark:** the median 2017-vintage VC fund had spent 19% of committed capital on fees and expenses after about eight years, and 87% on investments (Carta 2025). Use ~20% of commitments as the market reference for lifetime fees plus expenses.

## Reporting Standards (ILPA Reporting Template 2025)

- **Reporting Template (updated, released 22 Jan 2025):** quarterly; standardizes fund-level economics: cash and non-cash flows, management fees with gross-to-net reconciliation, partnership expenses with GP chargebacks, capitalized fees, incentive allocation, offsets, waivers and rebates. The old tiered structure was removed for uniform, granular reporting. ILPA recommends it for funds still in their investment period during Q1 2026 or starting on or after 1 Jan 2026. Adoption is voluntary.
- **Performance Template (new):** IRR and TVPI/MOIC, gross and net, with and without the impact of subscription facilities; "granular" (each capital call) or "gross-up" methodology. For funds starting on or after 1 Jan 2026, first delivery four full quarters after commencement.
- **LP ask:** a GP raising in 2026 should commit to both templates. "We report in our own format" is a NEGOTIATE item for any institutional or DFI LP.

### Fund-Level Metrics

| Metric | Definition |
|--------|-----------|
| TVPI | (Distributions + remaining value) / paid-in capital |
| DPI | Distributions / paid-in capital (the only realized number) |
| RVPI | Remaining value / paid-in capital; TVPI = DPI + RVPI |
| Net IRR | Cash-flow-weighted return after fees and carry; report with and without the credit line |
| MOIC | (Realized + unrealized value) / invested capital (gross, deal level) |
| PME | Net return vs a public index with matched cash flows |

**Market anchors (US-sourced):** 75th-percentile net IRR is 15.8% for the 2017 vintage and 19.1% for 2024; the 2021 vintage's 90th percentile is only 15.7% (Carta VC Fund Performance Q2 2026, 23 Sep 2026). Median DPI for the 2017 vintage is 0.37x (Carta VC Fund Performance, 2026). Read any GP claim of "top quartile" against the current Carta report for that exact vintage.

### Capital Account Statement (per LP, quarterly)
Opening balance, contributions, distributions, fees and expenses charged (gross and net of offsets), carry accrued, unrealized gain or loss, closing balance, and IRR since inception.

## LATAM Fund Context

- LATAM emerging managers often run below ILPA best practice on governance (nominal LPAC, no audit on very small funds) because the LP base is DFIs, family offices and local corporates. Carta 2025 confirms the smallest US funds ($1-10M) often waive the audit too.
- DFI anchors (IDB Lab, IDB Invest, CAF) usually negotiate their own side letters on E&S policy, exclusion lists, integrity and reporting *(directional)*. Check whether those rights extend to other LPs through an MFN clause.
- Local vehicles (Brazil FIP regulated by CVM, Mexico CKD/CERPI) can override standard LPA terms; ask for a legal opinion on equivalence.
- Institutional LATAM funds (Fund II+, above ~$100M) should meet ILPA governance and the 2025 templates.

## Red Flags in Fund Terms (LP perspective)

- No clawback, or clawback with no escrow and no GP guarantee
- Management fee above 2.5%, or no step-down after the investment period
- Portfolio company fees not 100% offset
- GP commit under 1% in cash, or funded by fee waiver
- Key person clause limited to death or disability, or without automatic suspension
- No-fault removal threshold above two-thirds plus a buffer (above ~80%) in a fund with a concentrated LP base
- LPAC made up of GP-affiliated parties
- Uncapped organizational expenses
- Subscription line with no cap, no 180-day limit, or used to fund distributions
- GP refuses quarterly reporting in the ILPA format
