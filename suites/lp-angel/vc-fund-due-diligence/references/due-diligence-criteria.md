# VC Fund Due Diligence Criteria: 5-Dimension Scoring Rubric

**For use with:** vc-fund-due-diligence SKILL.md
**LP perspective:** evaluating whether to commit capital to a fund manager

---

## Dimension 1: Track Record

**Investor question:** "Can this GP demonstrate they have actually returned capital to LPs,
not just marked up a portfolio?"

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Top quartile vs the current Carta report for that vintage on TVPI AND DPI. Audited. Returns not dependent on a single mark. |
| 4 | At or above vintage median TVPI with DPI above the vintage median (e.g., above 0.37x for a 2017 fund, Carta 2026). Named, realized exits. |
| 3 | TVPI above median but DPI at or below median; value mostly unrealized but marked by recent priced rounds. |
| 2 | TVPI near 1.0x on a fund 5+ years old, OR most TVPI in one company marked on a stale round (over 18 months). |
| 1 | TVPI under 1.0x on a mature fund, or the GP cannot provide audited or administrator-verified numbers. |

**First-time and emerging managers:** score attributable deals (as angel, operator, scout or junior
at a prior fund): number of deals, realized outcomes, whether the GP led or followed, and references
from co-investors. Without verifiable attribution, cap Track Record at 3. Do not score 1 only because
it is a Fund I.

**Red flag trigger:** DPI under ~0.3x on a fund 7+ years old *(directional)*. Calibrate against the
current Carta report: liquidity is slow across the industry (median 2017-vintage DPI was 0.37x,
Carta 2026), so compare with the vintage, not with an absolute rule.

## Dimension 2: Portfolio Construction

**Investor question:** "Does this GP's portfolio construction strategy maximize my return
probability given the capital I am committing?"

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Company count, ownership target and reserves that add up after fees (see benchmark below). Clear follow-on policy. Pro rata in term sheets. |
| 4 | 12–20 companies. Reasonable ownership targets. Reserve ratio disclosed and defensible (20–35%). |
| 3 | Portfolio size is reasonable but reserve policy unclear. Ownership targets are aspirational not contractual. |
| 2 | Highly concentrated (≤8 companies) with no diversification rationale OR over-diversified (40+ companies) with insufficient ownership for returns. |
| 1 | No stated portfolio construction framework. GP cannot articulate reserve strategy or ownership targets. |

**Key benchmark (check the arithmetic):** investable capital is roughly the fund minus lifetime fees
and expenses, ~19% of commitments for the median 2017-vintage US fund (Carta 2025), unless the LPA
allows recycling. A $60M fund has ~$49M to invest. 20 companies at $2.5M initial = $50M: no reserves
left. Any plan that does not add up after fees scores 2 at most.

**LATAM graduation check:** only 12% of LATAM seed startups raise a Series A within three years
(LAVCA 2025). A seed fund that reserves for follow-ons in most of its portfolio has misread the market;
reserves should concentrate in the few that graduate.

## Dimension 3: Terms

**Investor question:** "Are the fund terms structured to align GP interest with LP outcomes,
or do they transfer value from LPs to GPs regardless of performance?"

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | 2% or less with a step-down; 20% carry, whole-of-fund; GP commit at or above the Carta median for its size (1.7% overall) in cash; clawback with escrow or guarantee; 100% fee offsets; ILPA Reporting Template 2025. Hurdle optional (rare in VC: 9.5-12.4% of funds, Carta 2025). |
| 4 | One or two small deviations with a reason (e.g., 2.25% on a small Fund I with a step-down; GP commit 1% in cash). |
| 3 | One material deviation the GP explains and will fix (e.g., clawback escrow under ILPA's ~30% reference). |
| 2 | Two or more material deviations, or any deal-breaker that is negotiable: no step-down, clawback without escrow or guarantee, GP commit mostly via fee waiver. |
| 1 | LP-adversarial and refused: no clawback, no key person clause, fee above 2.5% with no step-down, zero cash GP commit. |

**Reference:** `shared/ilpa-standards.md` (ILPA 3.0 recommendations and Carta 2025 market
data). Full LPA review: lp-term-sheet-analyzer.

## Dimension 4: Team

**Investor question:** "Is this team stable, experienced, and capable of executing this
fund strategy across a 10-year fund life?"

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | 2+ GPs with complementary skills. 5+ years working together. Each GP has 2+ board seats or founder exits. Strong key person clause (departure/reduced time triggers). Succession plan documented. |
| 4 | 2 GPs with aligned experience. 2–5 years as team. One GP has strong track record; second building theirs. Key person clause present. |
| 3 | Team has relevant background but short tenure together OR key person risk is high (one GP drives all value). Key person clause present but narrow (death/disability only). |
| 2 | Single GP with no co-investor or team depth. Key person risk unmitigated. Track record is individual not team-verified. |
| 1 | Cannot articulate how investment decisions are made, partners part-time with no commitment in the LPA, or unresolved integrity issues in background checks. |

Note: being a first-time manager is not by itself a 1 or 2. Score the team on decision process,
time commitment, complementary skills and attributable judgment.

**LATAM context:** LATAM GPs often have shorter fund track records. Weight operator experience
(LATAM company exits, regulatory navigation) when a formal fund record is absent.

---

## Dimension 5: LATAM Fit

**Investor question:** "Does this GP have genuine edge in LATAM — or is LATAM a label
applied to a generalist thesis?"

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Proprietary deal flow in its stated markets, with a credible route to Brazil and Mexico capital and acquirers (~73% of 2025 LATAM VC dollars, Cuantico 2026 country table). GP has navigated LATAM regulation (CNBV, BACEN, SFC). Value-add beyond capital, confirmed by founders. |
| 4 | Strong Brazil or Mexico concentration with expansion thesis to others. 2+ GP team members with LATAM operating background. Regulatory navigation documented. |
| 3 | LATAM focus stated but deal flow concentrated in one smaller market, without a named exit thesis or co-investor network in Brazil or Mexico. |
| 2 | LATAM-labeled fund with significant non-LATAM deal flow. GPs have limited LATAM operator background. |
| 1 | GP is US-based with no LATAM presence, no LATAM team, labeling fund as "LATAM-focused" without deal flow evidence. |

---

## Red Flag Reference

Triggers that automatically floor a dimension score at 2 regardless of other factors:

- **DPI well below the vintage median on a fund 7+ years old** → caps Track Record at 2
- **One company is more than half of TVPI and its mark is over 18 months old** → caps Track Record at 2
- **No no-fault removal right** → caps Terms at 2
- **No fee step-down** → caps Terms at 2
- **GP commit under 0.5% in cash, or mostly fee waiver** → caps Terms at 2
- **Key person clause limited to death or disability** → caps Team at 2
- **GP cannot provide audited or administrator-verified fund numbers** → Track Record = 1
