# VC Fund Due Diligence Criteria — 5-Dimension Scoring Rubric

**For use with:** vc-fund-due-diligence SKILL.md
**LP perspective:** evaluating whether to commit capital to a fund manager

---

## Dimension 1: Track Record

**Investor question:** "Can this GP demonstrate they have actually returned capital to LPs,
not just marked up a portfolio?"

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | DPI >1.5x on realized portfolio AND TVPI >2.0x across all investments. Audited financials. Identified specific companies driving returns. Top-quartile vs Carta vintage. |
| 4 | DPI >1.0x on realized investments. TVPI >1.5x. Some exits with named returns. At or above US vintage median. |
| 3 | TVPI >1.2x but DPI <0.5x. Most value is unrealized paper marks. Returns exist but not yet liquid. |
| 2 | TVPI barely above 1.0x OR DPI <0.2x on a fund 5+ years old. Returns are mostly paper. No exits. |
| 1 | No realized returns. TVPI <1.0x (fund below cost). First-time manager with no track record. |

**Red flag trigger:** DPI <0.3x on a fund that is 5 or more years old. At that fund age, top-quartile
funds are beginning meaningful capital distributions. Below 0.3x DPI at year 5+ is a structural
concern, not just a J-curve effect.

---

## Dimension 2: Portfolio Construction

**Investor question:** "Does this GP's portfolio construction strategy maximize my return
probability given the capital I am committing?"

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | 15–25 companies. Initial check 2–5% ownership at entry. Reserve ratio 30–40% of fund. Clear follow-on policy. Pro-rata rights standard in term sheets. ILPA-aligned. |
| 4 | 12–20 companies. Reasonable ownership targets. Reserve ratio disclosed and defensible (20–35%). |
| 3 | Portfolio size is reasonable but reserve policy unclear. Ownership targets are aspirational not contractual. |
| 2 | Highly concentrated (≤8 companies) with no diversification rationale OR over-diversified (40+ companies) with insufficient ownership for returns. |
| 1 | No stated portfolio construction framework. GP cannot articulate reserve strategy or ownership targets. |

**Key benchmark:** For a $60M LATAM fund, 20 companies at $3M average check = full deployment
with no follow-on reserves. Any fund claiming 20+ companies at that fund size without a reserve
line must explain how they will participate in follow-on rounds.

---

## Dimension 3: Terms

**Investor question:** "Are the fund terms structured to align GP interest with LP outcomes,
or do they transfer value from LPs to GPs regardless of performance?"

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | 2% management fee with step-down to 1.5% or lower after investment period. 20% carry. 8% preferred return (hurdle). GP commit ≥1% in cash. Clawback with escrow. No-fault divorce at 75% supermajority. |
| 4 | Terms deviate slightly from ILPA standard with reasonable explanation (e.g., 2.25% fee on first fund, GP commit 0.75% with plan to increase). No structural misalignment. |
| 3 | One term deviates materially but GP explains it. Example: 2.5% management fee with no step-down but GP has no other income sources and fund size is $10M. |
| 2 | Two or more terms deviate from ILPA standard without justification. Examples: management fee on invested capital post-investment period; no clawback; GP commit funded by management fee waiver. |
| 1 | Terms are LP-adversarial: >25% carry, 3%+ management fee, no clawback, GP commit <0.5%, no-fault divorce requires >80% supermajority. |

**ILPA reference:** See shared/ilpa-standards.md for complete fee/carry/clawback
benchmarks and red flag definitions.

---

## Dimension 4: Team

**Investor question:** "Is this team stable, experienced, and capable of executing this
fund strategy across a 10-year fund life?"

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | 2+ GPs with complementary skills. 5+ years working together. Each GP has 2+ board seats or founder exits. Strong key person clause (departure/reduced time triggers). Succession plan documented. |
| 4 | 2 GPs with aligned experience. 2–5 years as team. One GP has strong track record; second building theirs. Key person clause present. |
| 3 | Team has relevant background but short tenure together OR key person risk is high (one GP drives all value). Key person clause present but narrow (death/disability only). |
| 2 | Single GP with no co-investor or team depth. Key person risk unmitigated. Track record is individual not team-verified. |
| 1 | First-time fund manager, no prior fund experience, no institutional investor background. Cannot articulate how team decisions are made. |

**LATAM context:** LATAM GPs often have shorter fund track records. Weight operator experience
(successful LATAM company exits) when formal fund track record is absent.

---

## Dimension 5: LATAM Fit

**Investor question:** "Does this GP have genuine edge in LATAM — or is LATAM a label
applied to a generalist thesis?"

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Deal flow from primary LATAM markets (Brazil + Mexico at minimum). Portfolio company exposure >70% LATAM. GP has personally navigated LATAM regulatory environments (CNBV, BACEN, SFC). Demonstrated LATAM-specific value-add beyond capital. |
| 4 | Strong Brazil or Mexico concentration with expansion thesis to others. 2+ GP team members with LATAM operating background. Regulatory navigation documented. |
| 3 | LATAM focus stated but deal flow is concentrated in one country only (often Brazil). No evidence of regulatory navigation or LATAM-specific value-add. |
| 2 | LATAM-labeled fund with significant non-LATAM deal flow. GPs have limited LATAM operator background. |
| 1 | GP is US-based with no LATAM presence, no LATAM team, labeling fund as "LATAM-focused" without deal flow evidence. |

---

## Red Flag Reference

Triggers that automatically floor a dimension score at 2 regardless of other factors:

- **DPI < 0.3x on a fund 5+ years old** → floors Track Record at 2
- **No-fault divorce clause absent** → floors Terms at 2
- **Management fee on committed capital with no step-down** → floors Terms at 2
- **GP commit < 0.5%** → floors Terms at 2
- **Key person clause limited to death/disability only** → floors Team at 2
- **GP cannot provide audited fund financials on request** → floors Track Record at 1
