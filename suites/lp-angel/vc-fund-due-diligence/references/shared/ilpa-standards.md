# ILPA Reporting Standards + Principles

**Source:** [ILPA Reporting Template — ilpa.org/reporting-template/](https://ilpa.org/reporting-template/)
**License/Attribution:** ILPA guidelines are publicly available. Cite as "ILPA Reporting Template (2016, updated 2024)" or "Institutional Limited Partners Association (ILPA)."
**When to use:** portfolio-reporter (Phase 2), lp-term-sheet-analyzer (Phase 3), vc-fund-due-diligence (Phase 3). Loaded when evaluating LP-GP relationships, fund reporting quality, or fee structures.

## Key Principles

ILPA represents institutional LPs (pension funds, endowments, sovereign wealth funds, fund of funds). Their standards represent what sophisticated LPs expect as minimum table stakes for transparency and reporting.

1. **Alignment of interest:** GP co-investment (typically 1–2% of fund) signals skin in the game.
2. **Transparency first:** Fees, expenses, and portfolio performance must be reported without ambiguity.
3. **Standardized reporting:** The ILPA Reporting Template enables LPs to compare funds across managers using a common format.
4. **Governance independence:** LP advisory committees (LPAC) must have meaningful authority, not just advisory rubber-stamp function.

## ILPA Principles Summary

### Principle 1: Alignment of Interest

| Requirement | Standard | Red Flag |
|------------|----------|---------|
| GP commit | 1–2% of fund (minimum) | <1% or GP commit funded by management fee waiver |
| Carry structure | 20% carried interest; 8% preferred return | >25% carry without exceptional track record |
| Management fee | 2% on committed capital (active investment period), 1.5–1% on invested capital thereafter | 2% on invested capital post-investment period; no step-down |
| Clawback | GP must return carry if fund underperforms | No clawback provision; escrow <10% of carry |

### Principle 2: Governance

**LP Advisory Committee (LPAC):**
- Majority of LPAC should be independent LPs (not GPs or their affiliates)
- LPAC approval required for: conflicts of interest, investment period extension, key person events, removal of GP
- LPs should have no-fault divorce rights after investment period (typically requiring 75% supermajority of LP interests)
- Key person clause: suspension/termination trigger if named key persons leave or reduce time commitment

**Investor eligibility:** Every LP must meet qualified purchaser or accredited investor requirements in their jurisdiction.

### Principle 3: Transparency

#### Fee Disclosure Standards

| Fee Type | ILPA Standard | Common Abuse to Flag |
|---------|--------------|----------------------|
| Management fee | Disclose calculation basis (committed vs. invested capital) and step-down schedule | Hidden continuation of full fee post-investment period |
| Transaction fees | 100% offset to management fee (or disclose split) | Transaction fees kept by GP without LP offset |
| Monitoring fees | Disclose if charged; 100% offset recommended | Annual monitoring fees with no LP offset |
| Broken deal expenses | Disclose fund-level vs. GP responsibility | All broken deal costs pushed to fund |
| Organizational expenses | Cap at 0.1% of fund or $500K | Uncapped org expenses |
| LP due diligence expenses | Should be borne by GP, not fund | Charged to fund |

#### Portfolio Company Expense Transparency

- All fees charged by GP to portfolio companies must be disclosed to LPs
- Board seat compensation received by GP partners must be offset or disclosed
- Related party transactions require LPAC approval

## Performance Reporting Standards

### Fund-Level Metrics (ILPA Standard)

| Metric | Definition | Calculation |
|--------|-----------|-------------|
| TVPI | Total Value to Paid-In | (Distributions + Remaining Value) ÷ Capital Called |
| DPI | Distributions to Paid-In | Total Distributions ÷ Capital Called |
| RVPI | Residual Value to Paid-In | Remaining Portfolio Value ÷ Capital Called |
| IRR | Internal Rate of Return | Net IRR (after fees and carry) | Cash flow-weighted return |
| MOIC | Multiple on Invested Capital | Realized + Unrealized Value ÷ Invested Capital |
| PME | Public Market Equivalent | Net IRR vs. equivalent public market index | Comparison benchmark |

**Relationship:** TVPI = DPI + RVPI

**Stage interpretation:**
- Early fund life (Years 1–4): TVPI <1.0x expected (J-curve); evaluate by stage progress and markups
- Mid-fund (Years 5–8): TVPI >1.5x target for top quartile
- Mature fund (Years 8+): DPI >1.0x minimum for LP return; top quartile >2.5× DPI

### Portfolio Company Reporting

Per ILPA template, GPs must report for each portfolio company:
- Current valuation and % ownership (fully diluted)
- Total invested capital and follow-on reserves
- Last 12-month revenue (or ARR for SaaS)
- Key milestones since last report
- Known risks and mitigation status
- Exit readiness assessment

### Capital Account Statement

Each LP receives a capital account statement showing:
- Opening balance
- Capital contributions (called during period)
- Distributions received
- Unrealized value (mark-to-market)
- Management fees and expenses charged
- Closing balance
- Cumulative IRR to LP

## LATAM Fund Context

LATAM funds often deviate from ILPA standards due to:
- Smaller LP bases (family offices, local corporates) with less institutional governance experience
- Shorter track records requiring more flexible governance
- Local regulatory requirements (e.g., Brazilian FIDC structures, Mexican CKDs) that override standard LP agreement terms

**For LAN portfolio evaluation:**
- Emerging manager first fund: expect simplified governance; LPAC may be nominal
- Institutional LATAM funds (2nd+ fund, >$100M): ILPA compliance expected
- Corporate LP-backed funds: alignment standards may differ; GP is quasi-employee of corporate sponsor

## Red Flags in Fund Terms (LP Perspective)

- No clawback provision or clawback escrow <10% of carried interest
- Management fee does not step down after investment period
- Transaction fees not offset against management fee
- LPAC composed of GP-affiliated parties
- No-fault divorce requires >80% LP supermajority (hard to achieve)
- GP commit funded by management fee waiver (not real cash)
- Key person clause limited to death/disability only (not voluntary departure or reduced time)
- Unlimited organizational expense budget
- Audited financials not delivered within 180 days of fiscal year-end
