## Red Flags

**[RED FLAG] No fee step-down.** 2.5% on $60M for ten years is $15M, 25% of commitments, vs a ~19%
median reference for fees plus expenses (Carta 2025). Ask: 2.5% for 4 years, then 1.5% on invested
capital.

**[RED FLAG] GP commit 0.5%, source unknown.** A third of the median for this fund size. If it is fee
waiver, it is our money. Ask: 1% in cash by final close.

**[WATCH] No Brazil or Mexico network.** Brazil ~49% and Mexico ~24% of 2025 LATAM VC dollars
(Cuantico 2026). Follow-on rounds and buyers for Fund II companies will often come from there. Ask for
a written exit and co-investor thesis.

## LATAM Context Box

- Benchmarks above are US-sourced (Carta). No public LATAM vintage dataset exists at this depth; no
  fixed LATAM discount is applied.
- Fund I's 0.9x DPI matters more than its TVPI in a region where 62.7% of GPs call exits unfavorable
  (Cuantico 2026).
- Seed graduation is 12% within three years in LATAM (LAVCA 2025): the GP's reserves should go to the
  few graduates, which Fund I's record (follow-ons in 5 of 21 companies) suggests they do.
- ARS exposure: Fund I held 40% Argentina. Ask where company cash sits and how distributions leave
  the country.

## Recommendation: WATCH

**Commit $1.5M (25% of the venture allocation) if, by final close:**
1. Fee steps down to 1.5% on invested capital after the investment period.
2. GP commit rises to 1% in cash, with evidence in the GP capital account.
3. Clawback escrow at 30% of carry or a joint and several GP guarantee.

**PASS if** the GP refuses the step-down. The other items are negotiable separately.
