## Red Flags

**[RED FLAG] Management fee at 2.5% with no step-down**

On a $60M fund over a 5-year investment period, LPs pay $7.5M in management fees vs $6M
under ILPA standard 2% fee. That $1.5M excess is structural LP value transfer. At fund
close ($42M), the excess is smaller ($1.05M) but the percentage impact on LP returns is
the same. GP must justify: is this because they have no other income source, or is this
a feature, not a bug?

**[RED FLAG] GP commit at 0.5% ($300K on $60M target)**

ILPA standard is 1% minimum in cash. At 0.5%, GP's financial exposure is $300K vs
$600K ILPA standard. This is a governance signal, not just a financial one: GP commitment
size below 1% indicates reduced alignment. Verify the 0.5% is funded in cash, not via
management fee waiver (which would be a zero-cash commitment in practice).

**[WATCH] Argentina-concentrated deal flow with no Brazil presence**

Fund I portfolio: 40% Argentina, 30% Colombia, 30% Chile. Zero Brazil investments. Brazil
represents 65% of LATAM exits in 2024 and 55% of LATAM VC deployed. A fund without Brazil
deal flow is structurally limited in exit optionality. GP must present a credible plan for
Brazil entry — not just a stated intention.

## Recommendation: WATCH

**Score: 16/25**

Pampas Ventures Fund II has a GP team with genuine LATAM operating credentials and a Fund I
track record that is above US vintage median. The Track Record (4/5) and Team (4/5) scores
are strong. However, two structural concerns prevent an INVEST recommendation:

1. **Terms (2/5):** The 2.5% management fee with no step-down and 0.5% GP commit are both
   below ILPA standard. These are not trivial deviations — they represent $1.5M+ in excess
   fees and a governance misalignment signal.
2. **LATAM Fit (3/5):** No Brazil presence in Fund I. Brazil is 65% of LATAM exits. This is
   a material gap that limits Fund II's exit optionality.

**Conditions for INVEST:**
- GP agrees to management fee step-down to 1.5% after investment period (Year 5+)
- GP commits to increasing GP commit to 0.75% minimum (cash-funded, not management fee waiver)
- GP presents a credible Brazil deal flow plan with at least one Brazilian advisor or partner

If all three conditions are met, recommend INVEST at $1.5M commitment (25% of $6M venture
allocation).

## Next Steps

1. **Request audited Fund I financials within 10 business days** — specifically: DPI breakdown
   by portfolio company, confirmation that all exits are realized (not secondary sales to GP
   affiliates), and auditor certification. Fund I DPI at 0.9x on year-6 vintage needs
   explanation of why realizations are below TVPI.

2. **Verify GP commit source** — Request a written confirmation that the $300K GP commit
   (0.5%) is funded in cash by GP partners personally, not via management fee waiver.
   Management fee waiver GP commit is a zero-cash commitment in practice.

3. **Call 3 portfolio CEOs from Fund I** — Specifically ask: (a) Did GP participate in all
   follow-on rounds? (b) What was the GP's value-add beyond capital? (c) Has GP ever asked
   the company to do something primarily beneficial to fund returns rather than company?

4. **Negotiate management fee step-down** — Before signing the side letter, require a written
   fee step-down schedule: 2.5% during investment period, 1.75% thereafter. This is a
   standard term for any ILPA-conscious LP program.
