## 3. Top Performers

**PortCo B (LogiScan BR): 4.77x on $650k.** ARR $1.8M (from $1.1M two quarters ago); signed a
national retail distributor adding 340 B2B accounts in 90 days. Series A term sheet expected
in Q3 2026; our $250k pro-rata is reserved.

**PortCo C (DataPago CO): 4.67x on $600k.** MRR $320k, up 52% in the quarter. About 40% of new
revenue comes from an AI collections agent priced per resolved account; that is also why the
mark is the most sensitive in the book (calibrated to the last round, not marked up further).

**PortCo A (FleetPro MX): 3.10x on $1.0M.** Secondary in Q3 2025 returned $1.9M; residual 8.6%
stake held at $1.2M.

## 4. Watch List

**PortCo E (TalentBridge BR), high risk.** Burn $120k/month vs $85k plan (41% over). Runway 90
days. Root cause: enterprise sales cycle doubled from 45 to 90 days; two LOIs slipped.
Escalation: if fewer than 2 LOIs convert by Aug 31, no bridge; start an acqui-hire process.
Marked down to $1.2M this quarter.

**PortCo H (EduFlow BR), moderate risk.** MRR flat 3 months at $78k; monthly churn 8.2% vs 3.5%
at investment, failing at month 3 of each cohort. Six-week onboarding rebuild in progress.
Escalation: if MRR is not growing by Sep 30, pivot or wind-down discussion with founders.
