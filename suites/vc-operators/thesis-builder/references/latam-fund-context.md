# LATAM Fund Context (Thesis Builder)

Read before finalizing any LATAM-focused thesis (Market Thesis, Market Sizing, LP fit).
Figures with a source are data. "Directional" means practitioner judgment: use it to reason,
never quote it to an LP as a statistic. When web access is available, refresh the market
numbers from the newest Cuantico and LAVCA editions.

## 2. How to use the numbers in a thesis

- **Fintech (61% of capital):** a generic "fintech in LATAM" thesis competes with the most
  crowded part of the market. Require a sub-sector and a mechanism (SME credit on new data
  rails, embedded insurance, payroll, regtech).
- **SaaS (13% of capital and deals):** under-allocated versus US norms; the thesis must say
  why buyers will now pay for software (labor cost, compliance mandates, AI replacing services
  spend) rather than assume the gap closes by itself.
- **Logistics and energy:** few deals, large tickets; usually not a seed-fund game unless the
  thesis is software on top of the physical asset.
- **Geography:** a pan-LATAM seed thesis must explain how it wins outside Brazil and Mexico; a
  single-country thesis must explain what it gives up.

## 3. Structural enablers to cite (check the current figure at the source)

- **Brazil PIX (Nov 2020) and Open Finance:** real-time payments and consented data sharing
  that make transaction-based underwriting possible. Pull current volumes from Banco Central do
  Brasil PIX statistics: https://www.bcb.gov.br/en/financialstability/pixstatistics
- **Mexico CFDI electronic invoicing (SAT):** invoice-level revenue data for every formal
  business, the base for invoice-based credit and accounting automation.
- **Mexico SPEI / CoDi real-time payments (Banxico).**
- **AI cost curve:** open-weight models competitive with frontier models on meaningful
  benchmarks (YC RFS Winter 2027, "Own Your Intelligence"), which lowers the cost of serving
  small LATAM customers in Spanish and Portuguese.

## 4. Regulatory timelines (directional)

| Country | Regulator | What it means for a thesis |
|---------|-----------|----------------------------|
| Mexico | CNBV | Full fintech licences take long; many lenders start as SOFOM. Make the regulatory path a diligence item and, when relevant, a closing condition. |
| Mexico | COFEPRIS | Health products and devices need approval; budget long timelines. |
| Brazil | BCB | Payment institution and credit licences (SCD, SEP) gate fintech models. |
| Brazil | ANVISA | Medical devices and SaMD approvals are slow. |
| Brazil | ANPD (LGPD) | Data protection applies to any B2B software holding Brazilian personal data. |

Do not quote licence durations as facts; ask local counsel or the portfolio company.

## 5. LP fit

**DFIs (IDB Invest, IDB Lab, CAF, DFC, FMO, Proparco, IFC):** the thesis needs additionality
(why commercial capital alone would not fund this), impact metrics tied to the thesis (SMEs
financed, underbanked users reached, jobs, women-led companies), and an ESG framework.

**Family offices (often the largest LP group in a LATAM Fund I, directional):** want GP
trust and access they cannot replicate, sectors they understand, clean LPAs. Speak to
return asymmetry and to deal access, not to impact alone.

**Institutional LPs:** want a repeatable process and fund economics near market norms:
management fee median 2% during the investment period then stepping down, 20% carry, median
GP commit 1.7% (Carta, "Fund Economics Report 2025", 4 Dec 2025). Established managers took
90.9% of US capital raised in Q1 2026 (PitchBook-NVCA Venture Monitor Q1 2026): an emerging
manager's thesis must explain an unfair edge, not a market trend.
