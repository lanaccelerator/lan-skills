# Decile Hub Deal Memo Template — Enhanced for LAN

**Source:** Decile Hub IC memo structure, enhanced with a16z/Sequoia/Benchmark rigor
**When to use:** deal-memo-writer loads this as the definitive section guide. Every section below MUST be addressed with data and informed opinion — not generic filler.

## Overview

1. **Problem** — What specific pain point does this startup solve? Who feels it? How painful is it (hair-on-fire, nice-to-have, or vitamin)?
2. **Product/Service** — How does the product solve the pain point? What's the mechanism? Is it 10x better than the alternative or incremental?
3. **Market** — TAM/SAM/SOM with methodology (top-down AND bottom-up). If the numbers diverge by >3x, call it out.
4. **Key Terms** — Instrument (SAFE/priced/convertible), round type, round size, valuation (pre/post), key terms. Reference shared/brad-feld-terms.md for standard benchmarks.

## Why Invest

1. **Top 3 Conviction Reasons** — Not generic ("great team", "large market"). Specific and defensible: "They have 40% MoM growth with zero paid acquisition" or "The founder built and sold the competitor they're now disrupting."
2. **The Bet** — What external condition must be true for this to work? "LATAM SMBs must adopt digital invoicing within 3 years" is a bet. Name it explicitly.
3. **Thesis Fit** — How does this map to the fund's investment thesis? If it doesn't fit cleanly, say so — partial fit is acceptable if the upside justifies it.
4. **Fund Economics Fit** — Can this return the fund? At what exit multiple? At what ownership? If the math doesn't work at current valuation, flag it.
5. **LAN Value-Add** — How does LAN add value beyond capital? Portfolio connections, corporate intros, operational support? Be specific — "access to our network" is not specific enough.
6. **Path to Next Round** — How does this round's capital get the startup to the milestones needed for the next raise? What's the target runway? What metrics must hit?

## Situation & Market

1. **Geography/Demographics** — Target market geography and customer profile. For LATAM: which countries first, expansion sequence, and why.
2. **Timing (Why Now)** — What changed in the last 12-24 months that makes this possible/necessary NOW? Regulatory shift, technology inflection, behavioral change?
3. **Competition + Differentiation** — Named competitors with specific differentiation. NOT feature comparison — defensibility comparison.
4. **Barriers to Entry** — What stops a well-funded competitor from copying this in 6 months?
5. **Current or Future Moat** — Network effects, switching costs, data advantages, regulatory capture, brand? If none exist yet, what's the path to building one?
6. **Macro Risks** — FX risk, political instability, recession sensitivity, funding winter exposure. For LATAM: USD/BRL, USD/MXN, USD/COP sensitivity analysis.
7. **High-Impact Regulations** — Positive (Open Banking, PIX, CFDI mandates) or negative (data localization, foreign ownership restrictions, banking licenses).

## Team

1. **Why This Team** — Relevant experience, domain expertise, customer understanding. Not just resumes — why are THEY the ones to build THIS?
2. **Scaling Capacity** — Can they recruit? Have they built teams before? What's the hiring pipeline?
3. **Missing Skills** — What's not on the team yet? What's the plan to fill gaps? Timeline?
4. **Long-term Relationship (4-10 years)** — Do we want to work with these founders for a decade? Chemistry, integrity, communication style.
5. **Management Issues** — Be brutally honest. Co-founder tension? Single founder risk? Visa issues? Prior failures they haven't addressed? Domain expertise gaps they're ignoring?

## Business, Economics, Ethics

1. **Path to Market Presence** — Segments, brand strategy, GTM approach, sales motion, channel/partnership strategy. Reference the startup's GTM plan if available.
2. **Unit Economics** — Current and target. CAC, LTV, LTV/CAC, contribution margin, payback period. If they don't know these numbers, that's a flag. Reference shared/yc-metrics.md.
3. **12-24 Month Milestones** — Specific roadmap with KPIs and capital requirements per milestone. What does success look like in 12 months? 24 months?
4. **Exit Potential** — Realistic exit paths. Strategic acquirers (name them), IPO viability, secondary. For LATAM: limited buyer universe is a real constraint — address it.
5. **Positive Impact / Mensarius Oath** — Does this business create genuine positive impact? Not ESG theater — actual impact on customers, communities, or systems.

## Fundraising Round

1. **Use of Funds** — Specific allocation. "Hiring" is not specific. "4 engineers + 1 sales lead + $X marketing over 18 months" is specific.
2. **Cash on Hand + Runway** — Current bank balance, monthly burn, months of runway without this raise.
3. **Other Investors** — Who else is in this round? Who invested before? Signal value of existing investors.
4. **Deadline** — When does the round close? Is there a lead? Are terms set or negotiable?

## Flags (CRITICAL)

1. **Cap Table Health** — Does the cap table reflect best practices? Founder ownership sufficient for motivation through Series B+? Option pool adequate? Dead equity from departed co-founders? Reference shared/brad-feld-terms.md for standard benchmarks.
2. **Conflict of Interest** — Any economic conflicts between founders, investors, or advisors? Related-party transactions?
3. **Legal Structure** — LLC, LP, C-Corp, passthrough? Is the structure investment-ready for institutional follow-on?

## FLAG: Corporate Structure (LATAM-CRITICAL)

This is a deal-breaker common in LATAM and MUST be investigated for every deal:

- **Local Entity Origin** — Did the startup start with a local company (SRL, SA, SAS, LTDA)? Which jurisdiction?
- **Subsidiaries** — Does it have subsidiaries in other countries? What entities exist?
- **IP/Asset Transfer** — Has the correct transfer of IP, ownership, and assets to the holding company (Delaware C-Corp or equivalent) been completed?
- **Cap Table Location** — Is the cap table clean at the holding level, or is ownership dispersed across subsidiaries?
- **Flip Status** — If the corporate flip/restructuring has NOT been done:
  - **RED FLAG MAYOR.** Document exactly what's missing.
  - Estimate cost and timeline to complete the restructuring.
  - Assess whether this is a condition precedent to investment or a post-close requirement.
  - Common pattern: founders raised from local angels into the local SAS/SRL, now need to restructure to a Delaware C-Corp for institutional VC. This is fixable but costs $10-30k and takes 2-4 months.

**If the corporate structure is a mess, say so explicitly.** "The cap table has 3 entities across 2 jurisdictions with no holding company. This needs $25k and 3 months of legal work before institutional investors will participate. Condition this investment on completing the flip within 60 days of close."

## Counterfactual Analysis (REQUIRED by Decile)

1. **What happens if we don't invest?** Does the company raise anyway, from whom, at what valuation? Do we lose access to the relationship or to pro-rata in future rounds?
2. **Alternative scenarios + regret test** — What would make us regret passing? Name the specific upside we forgo.
3. **Pre-mortem** — Assume 24 months passed and the company failed. List the 3-4 most likely causes of death, concrete (for AI deals include: model commoditization, copyright/IP, talent flight, round-tripping revenue). Prospective hindsight improves identification of failure causes by ~30%.
4. **Anti-portfolio check** — Is the recommendation falling into a known reasoning failure: anchoring on price/multiple ("outrageously expensive"), dismissing a category by association, reasoning by false analogy to a prior loser, or treating a solvable risk as fatal? (Bessemer passed on Google, Apple, FedEx, eBay, PayPal, Tesla, Zoom this way.)
5. **Name the irreducible bet** — Separate the ~70% you can diligence (plumbing) from the ~30% that is an irreducible bet (poetry). State the bet explicitly.

## Recommendation up front (quality bar)

Open the memo with the decision, not the description. First sentence: INVEST / PASS / DEFERRED + check size + terms + a graded conviction word. A mediocre memo describes; an excellent memo decides. A reasoned PASS is as valuable as an INVEST.
