## 1. Market Standards by Stage

### Pre-Seed

| Term | Market Standard |
|------|----------------|
| Instrument | YC SAFE (post-money, valuation cap only) — no priced round |
| Valuation cap (LATAM) | $1.5M–$8M depending on traction; see shared/safe-note.md for benchmarks |
| Liquidation preference | None — SAFE has no liquidation preference at signing |
| Anti-dilution | None — SAFE terms set at conversion |
| Board | No board rights; advisory board only |
| Pro-rata | Standard for checks above $50K |
| Option pool | Not set at pre-seed; established at seed priced round |
| Information rights | Annual financials, quarterly updates — minimal |

**When to use SAFE vs. priced round at this stage:** SAFE if no clear lead investor, deal closing in <60 days, or round is under $1M. See section 4.

---

### Seed

| Term | Market Standard |
|------|----------------|
| Instrument | YC SAFE (post-money with or without discount) OR priced Series Seed preferred |
| Pre-money valuation (LATAM) | $6M–$10M typical (LAVCA 2025); $10M–$15M only with top-quartile traction (directional) |
| Option pool | 10–15% pre-money unissued (adjust to 18-month hiring plan) |
| Liquidation preference | 1× non-participating preferred |
| Anti-dilution | Broad-based weighted average |
| Dividends | Non-cumulative, non-compounding |
| Board | 3-person: 2 founders + 1 lead investor |
| Protective provisions | NVCA model term sheet list |
| Pro-rata | Full pro-rata for lead investor (major investor threshold: $500K+) |
| Information rights | Monthly financials, quarterly board meeting, annual audited statements |
| No-shop window | 30–45 days |

---

### Series A

| Term | Market Standard |
|------|----------------|
| Instrument | Priced round only — no SAFE at Series A |
| Pre-money valuation (LATAM) | $15M–$25M typical (LAVCA 2025); above that needs top-quartile traction (see startup-valuation-benchmark) |
| Option pool | 10–15% post-close; often requires pool refresh |
| Liquidation preference | 1× non-participating preferred |
| Anti-dilution | Broad-based weighted average |
| Dividends | Non-cumulative |
| Board | 5-person: 2 founders + 2 investors + 1 independent director |
| Protective provisions | NVCA standard; consult which require only lead consent vs. preferred supermajority |
| Pro-rata | Full pro-rata for lead (threshold: $1M+); super pro-rata is aggressive |
| Information rights | Monthly financials, quarterly board meeting, annual audit for major investors |
| No-shop window | 30–45 days |

---

### Series B+

| Term | Market Standard |
|------|----------------|
| Instrument | Priced round |
| Liquidation preference | 1× non-participating standard; participating preferred may appear in down markets or bridge structures |
| Board | 5–7 person board; investors often seek parity or majority |
| Anti-dilution | Broad-based weighted average; narrow-based possible in competitive rounds |
| Dividends | Cumulative dividends begin to appear in structured/distressed situations — flag if present |
| Information rights | Monthly management accounts, quarterly financials, audited annual statements, annual budget, inspection rights for major investors (NVCA model Investors' Rights Agreement). ILPA standards apply to funds, not to portfolio companies. |

## 2. Negotiation Lever Framework

For each economic term, apply this Hold vs. Give decision framework:

### Valuation

- **HOLD:** If founder's ask produces ownership below fund's minimum check-size economics (typically <10% ownership for a lead Seed investor at a $500K check)
- **GIVE:** If comparable recent LATAM deals support the ask (check references/latam-fund-context.md comparable data)
- **SIGNAL:** Valuation is the founder's emotional anchor. Do not open with valuation negotiation — establish terms first, then valuation.

### Option Pool

- **HOLD:** Pool size must be justified by the 18-month hiring plan. Require founders to present the plan. Pool expansion dilutes founders, not investors.
- **GIVE:** If founders present a credible hiring plan justifying a smaller pool (e.g., 12% vs. 15%), accept it.
- **SIGNAL:** Option pool shuffle is a core mechanic in brad-feld-terms.md. Founders who do not understand the shuffle are accepting unnecessary dilution — educate them before using this as a lever.

### Liquidation Preference

- **HOLD:** Non-participating is the only market-standard structure at Seed/Series A. Participating preferred is not a concession to make.
- **GIVE:** Nothing — 1× non-participating is already the founder-friendly standard.
- **SIGNAL:** If an investor is asking for participating preferred at Seed, it signals they do not believe the company will achieve an exit above 3–4× — a misalignment worth surfacing explicitly.

### Anti-Dilution

- **HOLD:** Broad-based weighted average. Full ratchet is a red flag.
- **GIVE:** Narrow-based weighted average is a marginal concession if under pressure, but not recommended.
- **SIGNAL:** Anti-dilution only triggers in down rounds. If both parties are aligned on company success, this term matters only in failure scenarios.

### Board Seat

- **HOLD:** Lead investor gets a board seat. Non-negotiable for any check above $500K or any lead investor role.
- **GIVE:** Observer rights instead of a seat for non-lead investors. Observer rights carry no voting power and can be withdrawn — appropriate for small-check follow-on investors.

## 3. Red Flag Terms

The following terms signal an aggressive investor and should ALWAYS be flagged — not negotiated around:

| Term | Why It Is a Red Flag | Market Standard Instead |
|------|---------------------|------------------------|
| Full ratchet anti-dilution | Catastrophic in any down round; does not scale by down-round severity | Broad-based weighted average |
| 2× participating preferred | Not market standard; penalizes founders in every exit scenario | 1× non-participating preferred |
| No pro-rata for lead investor | Signals lead may not follow on — misaligned incentive structure | Full pro-rata for any check ≥$500K |
| No information rights | Investor withholding standard governance rights is an investor-quality red flag | Standard monthly/quarterly reporting |
| No-shop window >45 days (practitioner norm) | Long no-shop windows expose founder to negotiating leverage loss while the company continues operating | 30–45 days maximum |
| Cumulative dividends (Seed/A) | Hidden liquidation preference that compounds over time; not standard until distressed late-stage | Non-cumulative dividends |
| Drag-along with preferred-only trigger | Enables forced sale without founder consent | Drag-along requires both preferred AND common majority |
| Super pro-rata rights | Investor takes disproportionate ownership in future rounds, diluting subsequent investors | Standard pro-rata only |
| Personal liability reps from founders | Founders personally liable for company-level warranties — standard in PE, not VC | Company-level reps and warranties only |

## 4. SAFE vs. Priced Round Decision Guide

### Use a SAFE (pre-priced instrument) when:

- Stage: Pre-seed or early Seed — company has not yet established revenue or clear growth metrics
- No lead investor with clear conviction — SAFEs allow a rolling close
- Deal must close fast (SAFEs close in days; priced rounds take 6–10 weeks with legal work)
- Round size under $1M — legal costs of a priced round (typically $25K–$50K for both sides) are disproportionate
- Founders are first-time and not ready to negotiate complex preferred stock terms

**Mechanics and variants:** See shared/safe-note.md for all four SAFE variants, LATAM valuation cap benchmarks (Cuantico 2026 + LAVCA 2025), and conversion mechanics.

### Use a priced round when:

- Stage: Seed with a clear lead investor at conviction, or Series A+
- Round is the company's first institutional round with a named lead willing to take board responsibility
- Round size above $1M — legal costs are proportionate; priced round terms provide cleaner cap table for Series A
- Investor requires board seat or formal protective provisions — these require preferred stock
- Prior SAFE stack is becoming complex (multiple caps, multiple discounts) — priced round clears the cap table

### LATAM-specific guidance:

- Brazil: SAFE-equivalent structures available via CVM rules — consult Brazilian counsel on instrument recognition
- Mexico: SAPI structure accommodates convertible instruments equivalent to YC SAFE mechanics
- Colombia/pan-LATAM: US-law Delaware SAFEs are increasingly accepted for Cayman holdco structures — confirm with legal counsel
