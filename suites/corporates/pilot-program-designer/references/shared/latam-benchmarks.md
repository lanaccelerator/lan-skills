# LATAM VC Benchmarks (verified, refreshed 28 Oct 2026)

**Primary sources:**
- [Cuantico LatAm VC Report 2026](https://reports.cuanticovp.com/latin-america-vc-report-2026/) (2025 actuals). Cite as "Cuantico 2026".
- LAVCA 2026 Latin American Startup Ecosystem Insights (data as of 30 Jun 2026, released Oct 2026; shared as PDF, no public URL confirmed yet). Cite as "LAVCA 2026". Newest source for graduation rates, new-startup pipeline, AI share, hubs, gender and repeat founders.
- [LAVCA Startup Ecosystem Insights 2025](https://www.lavca.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/LAVCA_Startup-Ecosystem-Insights_2025.pdf) (data as of 30 Jun 2025). Cite as "LAVCA 2025".
- [LAVCA 2026 Trends in Tech](https://www.lavca.org/research/2026-lavca-trends-in-tech/) (2025 actuals). Cite as "LAVCA Trends 2026".
- US anchors: Carta data (see `shared/frameworks/yc-metrics.md` and the Carta rows below).

**When to use:** every skill that shows a quantitative benchmark. This file is the canonical LATAM data layer. Do not show a US benchmark without the annotation protocol below.

**Source rule:** LATAM totals differ by source because each counts rounds differently (Cuantico 2026: US$4.126B for 2025; LAVCA 2026: US$4.436B for 2025; Endeavor: US$4.696B for 2025; Crunchbase: US$4.2B for 2024). Pick ONE source per output and say which. Never mix totals from two sources in the same comparison.

**Verification rule:** every number below carries source and date. Rows marked *(directional)* are practitioner ranges from LAN deal flow, not a published dataset: use them for orientation, never as a hard threshold, and say so in the output. If a number is not in this file and you cannot verify it, do not state it.

## Annotation Protocol (all skills)

1. Show the US benchmark first, labeled `(US-sourced benchmark, <source>, <date>)`.
2. Follow with the LATAM figure, labeled `(LATAM, <source>, <date>)` or `(LATAM, directional)`.
3. State the direction: LATAM advantage, LATAM discount, or same as US.

**Example:**
> Seed to Series A: ~24% of 2022 US seed startups had raised a Series A *(US-sourced benchmark, Carta)*.
> LATAM: only 4% of the 2022 seed cohort raised a Series A within three years, down from 21% for the 2020 cohort *(LATAM, LAVCA 2026)*. LATAM discount: a fraction of the US graduation rate.

## SaaS and Unit Economics

No public LATAM-wide dataset on CAC payback, LTV/CAC or NRR exists at the quality of US benchmarks. Use the US benchmark (`shared/frameworks/yc-metrics.md`) and explain local drivers qualitatively: lower salary cost for sales teams, but lower ACV, higher churn in SMB and FX on local-currency revenue. Do not cite a LATAM payback percentage unless the user provides the data.

| Fact | Source |

| LAVCA was founded in 2002 with support from the IDB's Multilateral Investment Fund (now IDB Lab) | LAVCA 2025 |
| 65 LATAM-dedicated funds closed in 2024 | LAVCA 2025 Industry Data |
| Median 2025-vintage US VC fund had 23 LPs, under half of four years earlier; median anchor check at least 22% of fund since 2023 | Carta Fund Economics Report, 4 Dec 2025 |

Qualitative pattern *(directional)*: LATAM emerging-manager funds are typically anchored by DFIs (IDB Lab, IDB Invest, CAF, national development banks) and filled by family offices, local corporates and HNWIs. Pension funds are a small, growing share. Implications: DFI side letters bring E&S policy, exclusion lists, integrity and reporting requirements; family offices bring short horizons and relationship-based governance.

## LATAM Risk Factors for Investors

| Risk | What to check |
|------|---------------|
| FX | Revenue in local currency, returns in USD. Ask for USD-linked pricing or cross-border revenue |
| Exit scarcity | M&A is 67% of exits (Cuantico 2026). Name the acquirers; do not underwrite an IPO |
| Follow-on capital | Seed to A at 4% in three years for the 2022 cohort (LAVCA 2026; pooled 12%, LAVCA 2025). Plan for a company that must reach breakeven on the round it raised |
| Regulation | Fintech needs a license path per country (CNBV Mexico, BACEN Brazil, SFC Colombia, CMF Chile) |
| Concentration | ~73% of 2025 dollars in Brazil and Mexico (Cuantico 2026 country table); five companies took ~25% of 2025 dollars |
| Payments | PIX (Brazil) and SPEI (Mexico) cut friction; most other markets stay fragmented |
